## 1. Goth-scene identity words: vampire music, goth set, elder goth, baby bat  (strength 5/5, 11 posts)

- This is the most repeated scene vocabulary, and it comes from many different commenters on Book Club Radio sets tagged EBM and darkwave. People call the music "vampire music" or "vampiric". They call themselves "elder goth" or "baby bat". They name the space a "goth industrial dance floor", recall "German goth clubs" and "Belgian clubs", and praise a "batcave feel". The DJ herself uses "vampire music" as a hook.
- Use: Use in titles and descriptions (e.g. "vampire music" or "goth dance floor" in a mix or track blurb) and in bio copy for scene positioning. On SoundCloud and Bandcamp, "goth" works as a secondary tag. Avoid making it the primary genre tag, because it pulls a guitar-based post-punk crowd.

## 2. Stacked slash genre strings and their spelling variants  (strength 5/5, 11 posts)

- Nearly every dark mix that draws engagement is titled with 3–8 stacked labels. Spelling is inconsistent across channels: Darkwave / Dark wave / DARK WAVE, Coldwave / Cold wave, Post-Punk / Postpunk, Synthwave / Synth wave / dark synth wave. Viewers repeat these strings back, sometimes copy-pasting the full title. Minimal wave and minimal synth appear as companion labels to coldwave.
- Use: Titles and descriptions should cover both spellings (Darkwave and Dark Wave, Coldwave and Cold Wave), because the platforms index them separately. Tags: Darkwave, Coldwave, EBM, Dark Techno, Minimal Wave. Per house rules, normalize "Synth wave" or "dark synth wave" to "Synthwave". "New wave" shows up in these stacks but belongs only in bio influence copy, never as a tag.
- Examples: Dark Techno / EBM / Industrial Bass; Post-Punk, Darkwave, Coldwave; Dark Dance, Darkwave, EBM, Synthpop, Postpunk, Industrial; Dark wave/minimal wave/New wave/Synth wave/Cold wave; Cold Wave / Minimal synth / Minimal wave; dark synth wave; Coldwave Goth Mix

## 3. "Dark disco" is a contested search term  (strength 5/5, 11 posts)

- Searching "dark disco mix" returns a mix titled "dark disco, ebm, italo disco" alongside a Ukrainian pop single called "Dark Disco" (Jerry Heil). The pop single and its studio clip produce dozens of Eurovision and pop-fan comments. "Dark disco" also pairs with "Italo" in the "Dark Italo Disco Rework/Remix" format, where 80s classics are reworked. Those commenters praise the "dark dance" feel, the vocoder and new life for old hits.
- Use: Do not rely on bare "dark disco" in YouTube titles or Spotify-facing copy, because the pop crowd dominates it. Pair it instead, e.g. "dark disco / EBM" or "dark disco, coldwave". Keep "Dark Disco" as a Beatport and Bandcamp tag. "Italo" is useful in descriptions as an influence word, but a "Dark Italo rework" framing pulls a nostalgia-for-80s-hits crowd.
- Examples: dark disco, ebm, italo disco mix; Dark Disco (pop single); Dark Italo Disco Rework; Dark Italo Disco Remix; dark dance, deep vibes; vocoder

## 4. Industrial/EBM legacy names as reference points  (strength 4/5, 8 posts)

## 7. Mood/texture words: dark but cozy, sad and groovy, melancholic, atmosphere, cinematic scenes  (strength 4/5, 15 posts)

They talk about "real emotions" and "great atmosphere", say "Aura/Atmosphäre", and use gallows humour ("Pain Zone", 1.5x speed to "feel depressed" faster). Channel titles use "Melancholic", "Doomer Playlist" and "Cinematic Darkwave". Cinematic feelings are voiced through film imagery: Underworld, Blade, a Bond villain's lair, Gus Fring.

## 9. Wrong-crowd traps: EBM read as EDM, "night drive / bass boosted", "new beat" megamixes  (strength 4/5, 11 posts)

- The search "EBM techno mix" returns "EDM MIX MEGA BASS BOOSTED… NIGHT DRIVE GYM WORKOUT" uploads, full of generic, reupload and spam comments. Likewise, "new beat mix" returns pop remix megamixes and Mexican 90s nostalgia mixes. In those nostalgia mixes, purists complain that most tracks aren't new beat, and one notes that La Tour isn't new beat. One goth commenter explicitly contrasts the scene with "edm kids". Note the overlap, though: genuine "night time highway drive" use also appears on acid techno.
- Use: Never let "EBM" stand alone where autocorrect or the algorithm could read "EDM". Pair it with "industrial", "darkwave" or "dark disco". Avoid "bass boosted" and "gym workout" in titles. "Night drive" is acceptable only alongside dark genre words. Use "new beat" only as an influence word in bio or description, not as a tag.
- Examples: EDM MIX; BASS BOOSTED; NIGHT DRIVE; GYM WORKOUT; edm kids; New Beat 90s; not new beat

## 10. Track-ID, tracklist and "where can I stream it" requests  (strength 4/5, 17 posts)
