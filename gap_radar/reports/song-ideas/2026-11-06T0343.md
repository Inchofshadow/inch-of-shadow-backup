## 1. Living in the ruins of a lost future  (strength 5/5, 10 posts)

- Decaying modernist and brutalist spaces trigger a double feeling: these places look sad and ugly but also beautiful. Listeners say they miss a past they never lived, or a future that was taken from them. The ache is about time and loss more than politics.
- Use: A night walk through a concrete housing block where the narrator mourns the future the building once promised, then dances in its stairwell.
- Examples: feels like we now live in leftover ruins; grief for a stolen future, not for the past itself; recognizing a place as home despite never living there; ugly and sad yet somehow beautiful and atmospheric

## 2. Dancing alone through the sadness  (strength 5/5, 7 posts)

- Listeners describe crying and dancing at once. They dance alone in their rooms, and despair turns into euphoria on the floor. Sadness is something you move through, not something you sit in.
- Use: A chorus built around permission to dance while grieving, with the body moving forward while the heart stays behind.
- Examples: goth nerds dancing alone in their bedrooms; wanting to dance in sadness and dark memories; cried and danced at the same show; euphoric despair

## 3. Solitude as identity and wound  (strength 5/5, 6 posts)

- Use: Someone who locked their own cage and threw away the key, singing to the key rather than to a lover.
- Examples: dark morning commute, thinking about being alone; these songs got me through lonely broken times; left the city for freedom but loneliness followed; a manifesto of modern loneliness

## 4. Stuck in routine, dreaming of escape  (strength 4/5, 7 posts)
