## 1. Track ID requests pinned to a timestamp  (strength 5/5, 10 posts)

- The most repeated question is 'what is the track at minute X'. People ask by timestamp, ask for full tracklists, and other commenters answer or wait for a 'track ID guy'. When the uploader posts timestamps, people explicitly thank them. Almost nobody asks about gear or production; the question is the track's name.
- Use: Track list with timestamps below. If a moment stays with you, tell me the minute.
- Examples: track in the 1:50; cancion del minuto 13; rola del minuto 17:45; track id pls; puedes poner el track list; included all the songs and timestamps
