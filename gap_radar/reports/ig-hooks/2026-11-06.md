## 1. Age and era confessions in the nostalgia frame  (strength 5/5, 16 posts)

- The most repeated opening move in comments is stating one's age or era, then tying the music to a memory: clubs in Belgium, DC or Mexico City, a discman in '93, radio shows taped at night. Older listeners announce they are still dancing or that they only now found the genre. New beat, EBM and gothic dance sets trigger this most.
- Use: Some of you danced to this in Belgian clubs before I was born. I still hear those rooms in every kick I program.
