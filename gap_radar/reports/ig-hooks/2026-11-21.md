## 1. Timestamped track ID questions  (strength 5/5, 10 posts)

- The most repeated question across mixes and sets is what a specific track is, almost always pinned to a timestamp. Askers write in English and Spanish, and some just wait for the 'tracklist guy'. Other people answer, so these threads build comment volume on their own.
- Use: The track at 0:38 is unreleased. Ask for it and I will tell you when it comes out.
- Examples: track in the 1:50; 43:42 does someone know this one; la rola del minuto 17:45; cual es la cancion del minuto 27:00; need tracklist guy; whats the opening track; track list porfa

## 5. Let the music run  (strength 4/5, 9 posts)

- Fans strongly resent anything that interrupts the flow. A host talking over a set drew a whole cluster of complaints, and others criticised frequent rewinds and clumsy transitions. The shared value is that the music deserves undivided attention.
- Use: No talking, no reloads. Forty minutes left to run as one piece.
- Examples: kick off the host; killing the vibe; the music deserved better; talking over the music; calm down with those reloads; frequent rewind
