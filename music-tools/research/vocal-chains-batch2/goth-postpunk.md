## 3. Liz Fraser (Cocteau Twins)

Source quality: **partly documented** (one real 1987 engineer-level interview, but it is about how Guthrie recorded her, not the finished effects).

Sources read:
- [Making Music, July 1987, "Twin Voice" (Robin Guthrie)](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/twin-voice/13098). **engineer says** (Guthrie produces and engineers). No album named.
- [Tape Op #59, Lincoln Fong](https://tapeop.com/interviews/59/lincoln-fong): only the intro was readable. Fong engineered much of the catalogue from about 1984. The vocal part is paywalled.
- Search snippet (Muzines/Guthrie, not read): effects were recorded mostly at tracking, more added in the mix. Fong (snippet): vocals got thick, "probably never more than six layers", bounced together. **engineer says** (secondhand ❓).
- No source names Eventide or any pitch box. Guitar delay/reverb pieces (MusicRadar, Reverb) are about Guthrie's guitar, not her voice.

### The documented chain (Guthrie 1987)

- Mic: **Neumann U87** in the booth ("an industry standard vocal mike"). Early sessions used a small mic in front of a big one to make her comfortable.
- Headphones: cue level matters. Too loud and the singer goes sharp. She sometimes sang without headphones, once to a small room speaker.
- Compression: light, about **3:1 to 4:1**. The vocal should not sound "working".
- Noise and sibilance: **Valley People Dyna-mite** for breaths, clicks, pops. **Brooke Siren DPR402** and **Valley People 415 De-Esser** for esses.
- EQ: he cuts around 1 kHz and raises the gain, rather than cutting mids and adding top and bottom.
- Delay: set for repeats, mixed quietly behind.
- Reverb: sparing, added late. He prefers "a nice room sound, from a digital reverb" over plate.
- Doubling: he advises not hard-panning doubles (about 3 and 9 o'clock for mono). Not confirmed for her records ❓.
- Mix: lyric lines up front, atmospheric lines further back.

🎃 Note: this is his advice and method in 1987. It does not say how the dense multi-layer sound on *Heaven or Las Vegas* was built. Her layers (up to ~6, Fong, snippet) are the documented part of that.

### Mapping

| Step | Closest he owns |
|---|---|
| U87 | TLM 103 (closest Neumann he has) |
| Light comp 3-4:1 | Pro-C 2 (Opto/Classic style, 3:1) or UADx LA-2A / Teletronix |
| Dyna-mite (breath, click, pop) | RX 11 De-plosive / De-click / Breath Control, or Waves DeBreath |
| DPR402 + 415 de-ess | Waves DeEsser, Sibilance, or RX 11 De-ess |
| 1k cut + gain up | Pro-Q 3 bell cut at 1 kHz, then Utility gain |
| Quiet delays | EchoBoy or Live Echo, 15-25% mix feel (on a Return) |
| Digital room reverb | UADx Lexicon 224 or ValhallaRoom (small room, not plate); Valhalla Room preset "VOCAL - Upfront" (path above) as a start |
| Doubles | Record real doubles; or Waves Reel ADT / UltraPitch; ValhallaUberMod "VocalDualPitch" |
| Shimmer, to taste (🎃, not documented) | ValhallaShimmer "TajMahal" or "DeepBlueDay" preset |

### Proposed rack: "Fraser (Guthrie 1987 + 🎃 layers)"

Inserts: Utility > RX 11 De-plosive > Pro-Q 3 (HP 90 Hz, bell cut 1 kHz) > Utility (+gain) > Pro-C 2 (3:1, 3-4 dB) > DeEsser.
Sends rack: Dry 0 dB | Room (Lexicon 224 small room, 1.2 s, 100% wet, chain -14 dB, added late) | Delay (EchoBoy or Live Echo, dotted 1/8, 2-3 repeats, HP 400 Hz, -20 dB) | Layer (ValhallaUberMod VocalDualPitch or Reel ADT, -12 dB) | Shimmer (🎃 optional, ValhallaShimmer, -22 dB).
Idea 🎃: record 4-6 takes, bounce them to one track like Fong describes, then treat that.
