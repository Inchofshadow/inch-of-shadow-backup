## 1. Boy Harsher (Jae Matthews, Augustus Muller)

### Sources
- [Synth History: Three Questions with Boy Harsher](https://www.synthhistory.com/post/three-questions-with-boy-harsher) (live rack, old pedals)
- [Synth History: Interview with Boy Harsher](https://www.synthhistory.com/post/interview-with-boy-harsher) (Jae: Voice Live rack is her favourite piece)
- [Arturia: Augustus Muller](https://www.arturia.com/stories/augustus-muller) (he builds in Ableton, synth-heavy, nothing on vocals)

### What is documented
- Live: a TC-Helicon "Voice Live" rack unit. **artist says** (Jae, Synth History). Earlier she sang through a delay pedal into a chorus pedal, feedback-heavy. **artist says**.
- Studio: no mic, preamp, comp, EQ, reverb or plug-in named anywhere I could read. Muller works in Ableton. **artist says** (Arturia story).
- Style: Muller pushed for vocals "minimal and free of affect"; Jae says she is not a trained singer. **artist says** (paraphrase). Early vocals were close to spoken word.
- Method note: Jae records scratch vocals to Muller's bassline and pad, sends them back, he decides what stays. **artist says**. So the vocal is often a near-dry demo take with effects added around it. 🎃

### Chain (best community-style recreation, all 🎃)
Dry, close, low-key vocal. Light compression. Short slap or tight delay with chorus. Big dark reverb behind it. Little pitch work.

### Plug-in / preset names found
- TC-Helicon VoiceLive rack (Jae live). Not owned.
- No signature preset exists. Nothing to name.

### Mapping

| Step | Closest he owns |
|---|---|
| Voice Live rack (live) | Little AlterBoy (double, light) + EchoBoy + ValhallaRoom, in a rack |
| Delay pedal into chorus pedal | EchoBoy (feedback up) > Live Chorus-Ensemble, or ValhallaUberMod |
| Plain compression | UADx LA-2A or Tube-Tech CL 1B, 2-3 dB |
| Dark hall | ValhallaRoom `Halls/Vocal-HugeDarkRoom`, or ValhallaVintageVerb dark hall |

### Proposed rack: "FX - Vocal Boy Harsher" 🎃
Inserts:
1. Live EQ Eight: high-pass 90 Hz, soft dip 300 Hz.
2. UADx LA-2A: 2-3 dB.
3. Waves DeEsser or RX 11 De-ess.
4. Live Saturator, tiny drive.
5. Pro-Q 3: gentle air shelf only if dull.

Sends rack (Audio Effect Rack, chains):
- Dry, 0 dB
- Chain "Pedal": EchoBoy short, feedback 40%, then Live Chorus-Ensemble. Volume about -12 dB.
- Chain "Dark Hall": ValhallaRoom Vocal-HugeDarkRoom, 100% wet. Volume about -14 dB.
- Chain "Wide Double": MicroShift `Double`, 100% wet. Volume about -18 dB.

Source quality: **unverified** for the studio chain. Live gear **partly documented**.

## 3. Fever Ray / The Knife (Karin Dreijer)

### Sources
- [The Fader: The Knife on Silent Shout, 10 years later](https://www.thefader.com/2016/02/12/the-knife-silent-shout-anniversary-interview)
- [OC Weekly: Karin Dreijer Andersson, Fever Ray](https://www.ocweekly.com/?p=89514)
- [Exclaim: Karin Dreijer Andersson](https://exclaim.ca/music/article/karin_dreijer_andersson_of_knife_fever_ray)
- [Perfect Sound Forever: The Knife](https://www.furious.com/perfect/knife.html) and [Crack: Silent Shout](https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/the-knife-silent-shout/) (via search summary only ❓)
- Equipboard Fever Ray page quoting a Pitchfork interview (2009): not readable, search summary only ❓

### What is documented
- Karin hears the vocal in her head, sings it, records it, then pitches to capture that idea. "Sometimes it gets more clear if we pitch, and so we change format." **artist says** (Fader). Note "format" = formant, 🎃 likely.
- Goal: "a character that you can't place", gender unclear. **artist says** (Fader). Voice treated "as any other sound". **artist says** (Exclaim).
- Silent Shout: Olof pitched her voice up, down and sideways on the computer. Title track: voice doubled at different pitches. **forum/press** (Perfect Sound Forever, Crack, via search summary ❓).
- Fever Ray (2009): multi-tracked vocals, some sung high then pitched down. Pitch-shifting software named, no product. **press** (OC Weekly, review snippets).
- Earlier Knife records used "a lot of free software", then they moved to analog gear for Silent Shout. **artist says** (Olof, Fader). Fever Ray: worked in a soundproof box in a shared basement studio. **press** (OC Weekly, Russh).
- Gear claim: a TC-Helicon unit bought for the voice, and Steinberg VoiceMachine plug-in. **forum guess ❓** (Equipboard quoting Pitchfork; I could not read the original). The exact TC-Helicon model is unclear (VoiceLive vs "Live").
- Engineer credit: Christoffer Berg engineered/mixed/produced Fever Ray (AllMusic via search ❓). No interview with him found.

### Chain (documented method, 🎃 for the plug-in steps)
1. Record dry, clear vocal. Sing the idea first.
2. Duplicate. Copy A: pitch DOWN (about -4 to -7 st) with formant down: the "man / monster". Copy B: pitch UP with formant up: the "child". Exact amounts are NOT documented; these are my ranges. 🎃
3. Keep each copy in vocal range, doubled in unison with the original. **press** (Crack, WUWM).
4. Slow tempo, dark space, little reverb sparkle. 🎃

### Plug-in / preset names found
- TC-Helicon (VoiceLive) ❓ and Steinberg VoiceMachine ❓. Not owned.
- No preset names. Closest by sound: Soundtoys PrimalTap `Alien Transmission Voice Effect`, Little AlterBoy `09 Low Octave Double`, `08 High Octave Double`.

### Mapping

| Step | Closest he owns |
|---|---|
| Pitch + formant down | Little AlterBoy (pitch -5, formant down, `09 Low Octave Double` as start); or Waves SoundShifter; or Live Shifter |
| Pitch + formant up | Little AlterBoy `08 High Octave Double` with formant up; Waves OVox ❓ |
| Hardware voice processor (TC-Helicon) | Waves Harmony or Waves OVox; closest idea only |
| Offline clean shift | Melodyne (formant tool) on a bounced copy |
| Dark room | ValhallaRoom `Halls/Vocal-HugeDarkRoom`, ValhallaSupermassive |
| Grit | Decapitator, mild |

### Proposed rack: "FX - Vocal Fever Ray" 🎃
Inserts:
1. EQ Eight: high-pass 80 Hz.
2. Pro-C 2 or LA-2A: 3-4 dB.
3. Waves DeEsser.

Sends rack (chains run PARALLEL, each pre-pitched):
- Dry, 0 dB
- Chain "Low": Little AlterBoy, pitch -5 st, formant down. Volume -3 dB (loud, this is the voice).
- Chain "High": Little AlterBoy, pitch +4 st, formant up. Volume -9 dB.
- Chain "Space": ValhallaRoom Vocal-HugeDarkRoom + ValhallaDelay, 100% wet. Volume -14 dB.
- Optional "Grit": Decapitator low drive after the Low chain.
Tip: record the dry take and print the shifts later, so you can re-tune them per song. 🎃

Source quality: **partly** (method and intent documented, no plug-in names from an engineer, gear claim second-hand).

### What is documented
- Charli sings with Auto-Tune on in her headphones, always, even if it is not printed. **artist says** (Tape Notes via MusicRadar).
- Some Brat tracks go without it on purpose, for a "human mistake" feel. **artist says** + George Daniel (producer) **says**.
- Geoff Swan mixed most of Brat (credit via Discogs, search ❓). A. G. Cook and Finn Keane have vocal engineering credits on some tracks (credits, search ❓). Tom Norris mixed some (AudioTechnology, not read ❓).
- Swan on a Charli vocal (*New Shapes*, 2022, NOT Brat): Black Lion Audio Bluey 1176 "hit pretty hard" for "spit and aggression", then a Buzz Audio EQ, printed back in. Then FabFilter Pro-Q 3 and sends to a reverb, a widener with Eventide H3000, an aux with a Weight Tank compressor, and a Waves H-Delay ping-pong. **engineer says**.
- Swan's mix core: Brainworx bx_console SSL 4000 or Waves SSL E-channel on everything. **engineer says**.
- Swan: breaths automated down, never cut. Wavesfactory Trackspacer ducks backing vocals under the lead. Valhalla VintageVerb + Shimmer for a build. **engineer says**.
- "The production mix is the perfect roadmap." Swan mixes toward the producer's vocal sound. **engineer says**. So the vocal character is made at production (Cook, Keane, Daniel), not in the mix.
- A. G. Cook plug-ins (from the *Lucifer* interview): Soundtoys FilterFreak, Reveal Sound Spire, Sonic Charge Synplant 2, Kick 2, MeldaProduction MBitFunMB, Arturia DX7 emulation, NI Razor, Arturia Vocoder V. **artist says**. Vocoder V's role is described by him; no vocal-chain detail.
- Tutorial (Noise Chest, a fan remake, not the session): EQ, compressors, DeEsser, Doubler. Fast retune speed on Auto-Tune, a touch of distortion or saturation, ValhallaVintageVerb, and one send to Empty Room DimD64 chorus. **tutorial**.
- Tutorial (MusicRadar hyperpop): record slower, speed up; formant +3, or +2 st pitch with formant +1.5 to cut artefacts. **tutorial**, generic, not Charli-specific.
- Mastering: Idania Valencia (Sterling Sound), FabFilter Pro-L 2 into iZotope Ozone Maximizer 7, works in the box. **engineer says**. Not a vocal step.

### Chain (documented parts + 🎃)
Production vocal: pitch-correct hard and audible, formant touch, saturation. Mix: 1176 hard, EQ, Pro-Q 3, then sends (reverb, widener, delay). Backing vocals ducked from the lead.
