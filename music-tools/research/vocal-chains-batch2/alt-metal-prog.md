- Not found: any plug-in preset named for Maynard or Tool vocals. A search for a "Maynard preset" gave only studio history.

### The chain by album
**Undertow 1993** (Massy)
- Mic: **AKG C 1000**, Maynard's own idea, kept as the workhorse. She wanted a U67; it was hard to hang for a singer who sings low.
- Intimate parts: Neumann U67 into a **UREI 1176 "cranked way up"**.
- No preamp or effects named.

**Lateralus 2001**
- Every vocal recorded in Maynard's home loft on his own Pro Tools rig, producer David Bottrill. No mic, preamp or chain named. ❓ (secondary source)

**10,000 Days 2006** (Barresi, Neve 8028 at Grandmaster)
- Main mic: a **Soundelux tube condenser** (out of production), "his favorite".
- Distorted vocals: Maynard sings into the main mic **and a highly distorted SM57 at the same time**. Both run in a parallel chain onto two tracks, so the 57 can be blended up later. Tracked through Barresi's **Helios channel strip**.
- Megaphone vocal: a homemade "pipe bomb" mic (a guitar pickup on copper tube, by B-Band) inside the megaphone.
- Minimal EQ in tracking. He says there was a little EQ on the vocal, and drastic EQ if an effect was planned.
- Tape tricks (Louder): Maynard sang **into snare drums with the heads resonating**; vocals recorded an **octave up or down** and at other tape speeds, then sped up or slowed down.
- Effects owned by Barresi: Echoplex, Hiwatt tape echo, WEM Copicat units.

**Fear Inoculum 2019** (Barresi + Mat Mitchell, home studio in Arizona)
- **Four chains at once** (Barresi): Maynard's mic into a **Neve / 1176 "standard setup"**, plus Barresi's **U67**, an **SM7B**, a **Soyuz tube mic** and handheld mics through other preamps and compressors.
- Afterwards, more creative than on 10,000 Days: filtered with a plug-in, run through amps, or re-recorded on a handheld mic instead of the tube mics.
- Delays, reverbs, distortion and modulation were mostly **printed from pedals, moved live**. He used **Waves GTR3 Stomps** on odd vocal parts.
- Soft parts float in and out. Heavy parts stay aggressive to cut through.

**Live (year ❓)**: main vocal mic Audix OM-6. SPX 990 for chorus and PCM 70 for a short reverb on the vocal channel. Summit TLA-100 and BSS 901 patched on vocal and bass channels (summary tool ❓).

### Plug-in and preset names found

- Mix tutorial level: none found. A forum thread (recording.org) says only "clean pre, try coloured ones" ❓.

### The chain
**Siamese Dream 1993** (Butch Vig)
- Vig did not like the mids in Corgan's voice, so he used a **Shure SM7** (roll-off and boost both engaged) through an **API preamp** and a **Summit TLA-100 tube limiter**, all fed to Corgan's headphones.
- Many takes. Corgan sometimes sang 8 hours to get pitch right. He says his voice is "really hard to record".
- Doubling: Eventide harmonizer at about 30 ms ❓ unverified.
- "Very dry record" claim: an unsourced blog ❓.

**Mellon Collie 1995** (Flood and Alan Moulder)
- Flood found Corgan sang better in pitch without headphones, so he used a **Shure SM58 in front of open speakers**.
- On "Tales of a Scorched Earth" the vocal was recorded over the finished mix and heavily distorted to blend in (Vintage King). **artist says**
- The "1979" vocal sample was recorded through an SM58 (the rest of that line was cut off in my fetch ❓).
- Recorded at Pumpkinland and CRC, Pro Tools and tape. No outboard named for the vocal.

**Machina 2000**: "heavily-processed vocals" (MusicRadar text), no gear named.

### Plug-in and preset names
- **Waves Butch Vig Vocals** (owned). Retailer text: made from Vig's sessions with Nirvana, Pumpkins, Foo Fighters, Green Day and Garbage. ## 5. Steven Wilson (Porcupine Tree and solo)

### Sources
- [SOS: Porcupine Tree](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/porcupine-tree) (June 2010, Tom Flint) · **artist says**
- [MusicPlayers: Steven Wilson, Fear of a Blank Interview](https://musicplayers.com/2007/07/18137/) (2007) · **artist says**

- [Tape Op: Steve Wilson](https://tapeop.com/interviews/73/steve-wilson) (U87 location recording only)
- Not found: a studio vocal chain for *In Absentia* (2002), *Deadwing* (2005), *Fear of a Blank Planet* (2007) beyond the SOS 2010 home-studio facts.

### The chain
**Porcupine Tree home studio (SOS 2010; MusicPlayers 2007)**
- Mic: **Neumann U87**, in the middle of the room, no booth, headphones. "It's the only mic I use." (2007)
- Preamp / converter: **Apogee Trak 2**. He uses its **soft limiter** for peaks and a **70 Hz high-pass**. "I don't even compress on the way in."
- About **90% of final vocals are the demo takes**, recorded at top quality because the first take holds the emotion.
- Processing in Pro Tools: **Focusrite D2 EQ**, **Focusrite D3 compressor** (Bomb Factory), **Line 6 Echo Farm** for tape-style delay, **D-Verb** only occasionally. He prefers delays to reverb on vocals.
- **Megaphone vocal** = the D2 EQ, a very narrow band around 2 kHz (low and high cut very tight).
- Wilson names the D2 and D3 as his favourites (2007).

**Solo, *The Future Bites* (2021)**
- Producer David Kosten: **Chandler Limited REDD mic > Rupert Neve Shelford channel with a bit of compression**. "Pretty raw" before reverb and effects.
- Signature slap: Kosten's **Roland Space Echo** on all vocals ("a halo of slap").
- Wilson's own chain now: **MS47 Mk II (U47 clone) > Neve preamp > UA 1176 > Apollo, 24/96**. He does many takes and **comps**.

**Live (Ian Bond, FOH)**
- Mic: **Audix OM7**. Console dynamic EQ (BSS 901 style): low shelf for pops and proximity, de-ess bands, broadband comp usually off.
- EQ: a few dB out of lows and low-mids (proximity), a narrow cut near 1 kHz (nasal), a very small cut near 4 kHz.
- Delays on the vocal in places, a **Line 6 Pod** for album-style vocal distortion, **no reverb** except one song.

### Plug-in and preset names
- Named: Focusrite D2 / D3 (Pro Tools plug-ins, discontinued), Line 6 Echo Farm, Digidesign D-Verb, UA 1176, Roland Space Echo (hardware).
- No preset named for Wilson found anywhere.
