Mixing two

  • 🎃 Directions can share a song. The documented chains already do this: Illangelo makes the verse small and lo-fi and the chorus open and overdriven; Salogni automates the effects per section.
  • Common pairs: verses 1 with choruses 2 or 4; one chain with section automation like 3.
  • 🎃 For 5: verses close like 1 (only on songs where the take is strong enough), hooks tuned and wide like 5.
  • Write the mix in the note field, e.g. "verses 1, choruses 2".

Hear it before you decide

  • Take one vocal from one song and put it on 4 tracks, one chain each (Live Audio Effect Racks).
  • Match the loudness by ear. The louder version always sounds better.
  • Listen on the monitors and on the phone, then tick here.
