## 2. Five documented chains (singer + engineer + source)

### A. Dave Gahan (Depeche Mode), *Memento Mori* 2023. Engineer: Marta Salogni. **engineer says**
Source: [SOS Inside Track: Depeche Mode 'Memento Mori'](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-depeche-mode-memento-mori) (June 2023)

- Tracking: Neumann U87 > API preamp > LA-2A > Manley Massive Passive EQ.
- Gahan sings into effects that are already set up ("quite extreme"). Tape machines, Eventide H3000, Roland Dimension-D, AMS RMX16, Lexicon. The vocal is recorded **dry** and every effect is printed on its own track.
- Booth: an improvised booth of mic stands and rugs, later a heavy curtain booth.
- Mix insert order (her words): EQ > compression > EQ > de-esser > EQ > Devil-Loc > Radiator > Soothe > EQ. Valhalla Delay on top. A tape slap echo on 'Ghost Again'.
- A lot of volume automation, including the ends of phrases, so the words cut through. Effects are automated per section.

| Step | What he owns (Live / plug-ins) |
|---|---|
| U87 > API pre | His mic ❓ (see open questions) > UADx API Vision Channel Strip (native) |
| LA-2A | UADx LA-2A Silver or Gray (native) |
| Massive Passive | UADx Manley Massive Passive EQ (native) |
| EQ / comp / de-ess | Pro-Q 3, Pro-C 2, Waves DeEsser or RX 11 De-ess |
| Devil-Loc, Radiator, Soothe | Soundtoys Devil-Loc + Radiator, oeksound soothe2: **all owned, exactly the same plug-ins** |
| Valhalla Delay | ValhallaDelay: **owned** |
| Tape slap | UADx Galaxy Tape Echo, EchoBoy, or Live Echo |
| "Sing into effects, record dry" | In Live: monitor through a Return chain, record the dry input, resample the Return onto its own track |

Buy: nothing.

### B. Robert Smith (The Cure). **engineer says** + **artist says**
Sources: [SOS: The Cure, recording *Wild Mood Swings*](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/cure-recording-wild-mood-swings) (1996, Smith's own words + producer Steve Lyon) · [SOS Classic Tracks: The Cure 'A Forest'](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4914807) (2004, producer/engineer Mike Hedges)

- Smith: an **AKG C12** since *Wish*, "much more forgiving". His voice has "a hard edge… which peaks at about 3kHz". The only outboard he knows he used on three albums is a **Massenburg 8200 EQ**, used for "notching out rather than adding".
- Lyon: Smith lives with a vocal for 3-4 weeks, then sings it through; backing vocals take 20-30 minutes.
- Hedges, *Seventeen Seconds* / 'A Forest' (1980): vocal on a **Neumann FET 47**. One take, redone if wrong, very little comping. The mix is reverb-laden. Stereo tape delay with two different delay times, faded between half and double speed. Up to seven flangers following the envelope of the sound. Backwards reverb.
- Compression, de-esser, distortion on Smith's voice (Amir's question, 11-12): **no engineer source names any of these** ❓. Searched SOS (1996, 2004), the Faith production notes ([Hi-Fi News](https://hifinews.com/content/cure-faith-production-notes): only that his vocals sounded "too happy" and were finished at Abbey Road) and Disintegration coverage (no David M. Allen gear interview found). The *Wild Mood Swings* studio gear list includes a Urei 1178 and a Focusrite Red 3 compressor, but nobody says they were on his voice ❓. His own words point the other way: notch the 3 kHz edge instead of adding. The 1980 "distortion" is in the effects (flangers, tape delay, backwards reverb), not the voice chain.
- Live (2007 tour): Shure SM58 for decades and a Fairchild 660 among the FOH vocal plug-ins ❓ (search snippet of [AudioTechnology: The Cure Live](https://audiotechnology.com/?p=59712); the page itself was blocked, not read).

| Step | What he owns |
|---|---|
| "Notch, don't add" around 3 kHz | Pro-Q 3 dynamic band (cut only when it spikes) or soothe2. Massenburg itself: UAD MDWEQ5 (UAD-2, needs the Apollo ❓) |
| Two-time tape delay | EchoBoy Dual mode, or Live Echo (L and R times set apart, modulation on) |
| Envelope flangers | UAD ADA Flanger / MXR Flanger-Doubler (UAD-2 ❓), Waves MetaFlanger, Live Chorus-Ensemble + an Envelope Follower (Max for Live) on its rate |
| Backwards reverb | Live: reverse the clip, print the reverb, reverse back |
| Fairchild 660 (live ❓) | UADx Fairchild 660 (native) |

Buy: nothing.

### E2. Crystal Castles: community recreations (added 11-12, after Amir: "im sure there is some vocal chain suggestion somewhere online")
No engineer source exists, so everything here is **tutorial** or **forum guess**.

**1. YouTube, billyxo: "how to do crystal castles type vocals (free witchhouse/synthpop preset)"** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1XhsLSp_Dk8), Feb 2024, about 31k views, FL Studio). Read from the video's auto-captions while it played muted, plus frames.
- Main vocal: a **harmony layer under it**, plus Auto-Tune with **retune 0**, key set to the song's key (his: G major), and **transpose +1** "to pitch it up, sound more like a female". He sings one note lower so it lands on pitch.
- Insert order: noise gate > compressor > **multiband compressor, only the high band** (attack moved) > gain > limiter > EQ (from a preset) > **flanger** > **convolver ("the ghost effect")** > delay. The whole vocal is **sidechain-gated by Gross Beat** for a rhythmic chop.
- Layer vocal: the same chain, but a reverb instead of the convolver, plus a stereo shaper at about 70% mix.
- His presets are a download link (rekonise). I didn't download them, per the temporary files rule.

(Tutorial 2, Navjaxx / Harmony Engine, removed on Amir's ask 11-12.)

**Convolver** = a convolution reverb (German: Faltungshall). It puts the sound inside an impulse response, a recorded "fingerprint" of a real room, plate, speaker or any short sound. FL Studio's is called Fruity Convolver. In Live it's **Hybrid Reverb**: set the convolution engine and pick an IR, or drag any short audio file onto it as your own IR.

**3. Gearspace thread "Perfect Pussy & Crim3s distorted vocals"** (forum; the page itself is behind Cloudflare, so this comes from a search summary only ❓)
- Distortion, a **short spring reverb on the high end**, and a boost in the upper mids so the yelling has presence. One reply says the oldest Crystal Castles tracks seem to **stack several takes and run the yelled parts through Auto-Tune**.

**4. Internet Tattoo: "Crystal Castles plugin picks"** ([page](https://www.internettattoo.com/blog/crystal-castles-vst-plugins-guide); a weak source, an affiliate blog with leftover AI-prompt text in the page)
- Soundtoys **Little AlterBoy** for pitch and formant, SketchCassette or Vinyl to degrade, Valhalla Delay + VintageVerb, RC-20 for bitcrushing, and "clipping vocals until they distorted into oblivion". It also claims a Casio SK-1 toy sampler was used for distorted vocal textures ❓.

**5. Equipboard (crowd):** SM58, Boss DS-1, Lexicon MX200 ❓.

| Community step | What he owns (Live) |
|---|---|
| Auto-Tune retune 0, key set, transpose up | Auto-Tune Artist (retune 0, key); pitch up with **Little AlterBoy** (owned), formant to taste |
| Harmony layer under the lead | A duplicate vocal through Little AlterBoy +3/+4 st, or **Waves Harmony** (owned) |
| Gate > comp > multiband (high band only) > limiter | Live Gate > Compressor > Multiband Dynamics (high band only) > Limiter |
| Flanger | Live Phaser-Flanger, Waves MetaFlanger, Soundtoys PhaseMistress |
| Convolver "ghost" | Live Hybrid Reverb, convolution side, short dark IR |
| Gross Beat sidechain gating | **ShaperBox 3** (owned) volume shaper, or Live Auto Pan in Gate-like shapes |
| Stereo shaper on the layer | Ozone Imager 2 or Live Utility width |
| Clip / bitcrush | Live Roar or Redux, Decimort 2, RC-20 (all owned), on a parallel chain |
| Short spring on the highs | Waves Magma Springs on a Return, low-cut |

Buy: nothing.

### F. Lana Del Rey (outside his genre, added 11-12 on Amir's ask). **engineer says**
Sources: [SOS Inside Track: Lana Del Rey *Lust For Life*](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-lana-del-rey-lust-life) (Oct 2017, Kieron Menzies + Dean Reid) · [SOS: Laura Sisk, recording *Norman Fucking Rockwell!*](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/laura-sisk-engineer) (Feb 2020)

*Lust For Life* (2017), Menzies + Reid:
- Her favourite mic after years of looking: **Shure SM7B** on a stand, because it has "the dark aesthetic that she likes". It goes to a BAE 1073 > UREI 1176, with a flat API 550B in the path "just for the sound". No EQ on the way in.
- She monitors through the **AIR Spring Reverb** plug-in because she wants to hear herself as she will sound on the record; Menzies calls it one of the characteristic parts of her vocal sound. Valhalla VintageVerb is also used a lot.
- Reid's mix starting point for her vocals: de-esser > EQ (EQ3 7-band or Waves SSL channel) > LA-2A or CLA-2A > Waves C4 (preset 'Pensado Vocal'), then several sends to effects.
- Spoken parts: EQ boost at 1 kHz > CLA-2A > de-esser > McDSP FutzBox (radio sound) > de-esser > C4 > Dynamics 3.
- There is also a vocoder track on Waves Morphoder.
- She records 3-4 takes, takes 2-3 are usually the keepers, and there is very little comping. The stacked backing vocals define much of her sound.

*Norman Fucking Rockwell!* (2019), Laura Sisk:
- Wunder CM7 S (a U47 copy with a small dip at 8 kHz that helps with sibilance) > 1073 > **Tube-Tech CL-1B** on the fixed setting (1 ms attack, 50 ms release), only 3-5 dB at the loudest parts. In the box: barely any EQ, a bit more compression, reverb per song.

| Step | What he owns |
|---|---|
| SM7B | His mic ❓ |
| 1073 > 1176 | UADx Neve 1073 > UADx 1176LN Rev E (or Rev A) |
| API 550B "flat, for the sound" | Waves API-550B or UAD API 550A |
| Spring reverb to monitor + record with | Waves Magma Springs (owned); UAD AKG BX 20 (UAD-2 ❓). He does not own the AIR Spring Reverb |
| CL-1B fixed 1 ms / 50 ms | Softube Tube-Tech CL 1B: **the exact unit** |
| De-ess > EQ > LA-2A > C4 'Pensado Vocal' | Waves DeEsser > Waves SSL channel > UADx LA-2A > **Waves C4 (owned, so the preset should be in it** ❓ not checked) |
| FutzBox radio | Live EQ Eight band-pass + Pedal, or Waves Retro Fi / Lofi Space |
| Vocoder | **Waves Morphoder: owned, the exact plug-in** |
| VintageVerb | ValhallaVintageVerb: owned |

Buy: nothing.

### H. The Weeknd, 'The Hills' 2015. Producer/mixer: Carlo 'Illangelo' Montagnese. **engineer says**
Source: [SOS Inside Track: The Weeknd](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-weeknd) (Dec 2015, Paul Tingen)

- Recorded raw: no EQ, no compressor, 32-bit, he only checks it never clips. The mic is maybe a U67: "it doesn't matter as I ended up treating the vocals so heavily". One take, nothing recut.
- Verse lead inserts, in order: **Auto-Tune EFX** (very subtle: "if you don't pull a vocal through Auto-Tune these days, it almost does not sound normal") > **FabFilter Pro-DS** (the mic was too bright) > Pro-Q2 (low and top rolled off for lo-fi) > **Waves Vocal Rider** > UAD API 560 (gives "a kind of fuzz") > **Pro-MB** multiband ("really pumping").
- Parallel track: **Little Radiator** for overdrive > **UAD 1176 slammed**, blended under the lead.
- 5 effect sends: Altiverb + H-Delay pre-delay + Q2 cutting the top; Waves Doubler > MicroShift > Q2 (so the doubler's esses stay out of the way); long Timeless 2 tape delays, filtered; a second Altiverb.
- Chorus: the same core plus Reel ADT, Decapitator, Volcano and a UAD MXR Flanger. The verse is soft and low and the chorus is high and screamed, so "it had to sound overdriven".
- Backing vocals: Auto-Tune Live (it sounds more natural than EFX), Pro-G gate, two mono LA-2As, Pultecs.
- *Creepin'* 2022 ([SOS Inside Track: Metro Boomin](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-metro-boomin-heroes-villains), Ethan Stevens): his engineer Shin Kamiyama tunes and treats the vocal before it leaves (Pro-MB, Waves Maserati GRP). Long reverb (Eventide Blackhole) > Pro-Q 3 mids only > Saturn 2 > Waves RComp **ducked by the dry vocal**, so the tail opens up in the gaps.

| Step | What he owns |
|---|---|
| Subtle Auto-Tune first | Auto-Tune Artist or UAD Auto-Tune Realtime, fast but light |
| Pro-DS | Not owned: Waves DeEsser / Sibilance, RX 11 De-ess |
| Pro-Q2, Pro-MB, Timeless 2, Volcano, Saturn 2 | Pro-Q 3, Pro-MB, Timeless 3, Volcano 3, Saturn 2: **owned (newer versions)** |
| Vocal Rider | Not owned: ride Live clip/track gain, or Waves MV2 ❓ (not the same tool) |
| API 560 fuzz | Waves API-560 (owned), UAD API 560 (UAD-2 ❓) |
| Parallel Little Radiator > 1176 slammed | Little Radiator + UADx 1176LN: **exact pair**, on a Return or a rack chain |
| Altiverb | Not owned: Live Hybrid Reverb (convolution side), Pro-R, ValhallaRoom |
| H-Delay, Doubler, MicroShift, Reel ADT, Decapitator | **All owned** (Waves H-Delay, Doubler2/4, Soundtoys MicroShift, Abbey Road Reel ADT, Decapitator) |
| MXR Flanger, EP-34, Time Cube | UAD-2 ❓; EP-34 alternative UADx Galaxy Tape Echo |
| Ducked reverb (Creepin') | Live Compressor on the reverb Return, sidechain = lead vocal; Saturn 2 before it |

Buy: nothing needed (Pro-DS, Vocal Rider and Altiverb have owned stand-ins).

### J. Wider sources, added 11-12 on Amir's ask ("not only engineers: Reddit, YouTube, so on")

New label: **tutorial** = a magazine or video tutorial recreating the sound, without the artist's engineer. These lines are not engineer facts.

Reach (11-12): **Reddit is blocked** for both my search and the built-in browser. **Gearspace** sits behind a Cloudflare check, so it was not read; only search snippets were seen. **YouTube** pages give no text (no transcripts), so a video's content was not read; I only saw video titles. **The Weeknd, *After Hours* (2020): engineer says.** This is the biggest find, because it matches Amir's own setup.
Source: [Mix: Illangelo on producing 'After Hours'](https://www.mixonline.com/?p=121713) (June 2021)
- "the vocal chain is really the **Shure SM7B going into the Apollo Twin**, using the pre-amps Universal Audio supplies. They have the **Manley VOXBOX** [Channel Strip plug-in], which is incredible." Sometimes they bypass all emulations and use only the preamp.
- They tried a Sony C800, but the SM7B "hit" better. He bought a Tube-Tech CL 1B, but the small Apollo + SM7B rig was "easier, faster and better". Everything else was done with plug-ins, in **Ableton**.
- Amir has the SM7B, the Apollo Twin and UAD Manley VOXBOX (Unison, needs the Apollo ❓).

**Depeche Mode live, Memento Mori tour (2023-24): engineers say.**
Source: [Mix: Mixing Depeche Mode's Memento Mori world tour](https://www.mixonline.com/?p=143399) (Jan 2024)
- Front of house: a **short plate** (Bricasti M7) for depth, with a TC System 6000 reverb blended in for character.
- Monitors: a UAD **Manley VoxBox on Dave**, plus a 500-series chain of Shadow Hills GAMA pre > RND 542 tape emulator > API 550A. Mic: an SM58.
- Amir's version: UAD Manley VOXBOX ❓ (Unison), Waves API-550A; for the tape stage, UADx Studer A800 or Ampex ATR-102; for the plate, ValhallaPlate or UADx EMT 140.

**Lana Del Rey: tutorial.**
Source: [MusicRadar: How to create drowsy Lana Del Rey-style vocals](https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-create-drowsy-lana-del-rey-style-vocals-531265) (Feb 2012, Born To Die era)
- EQ: high shelf at 10 kHz, boost around 5 kHz, cuts at 1.1 kHz and 272 Hz. Compression at 4.4:1 with a low threshold.
- Send 1: a 2.5 s plate combined with a quarter-note echo, slightly louder on the left. Send 2: a long "very large vocal" reverb, with the send automated up on key lines.
- Tuning: no automatic tuner, because it kills her bends. Use Melodyne or the Auto-Tune graph, leave the bends alone, and don't push notes to perfect pitch: "Del Rey's style is to sing slightly flat".
- Amir's version: Pro-Q 3 > Pro-C 2 at 4.4:1 > ValhallaPlate 2.5 s + Live Echo 1/4 (L a bit up) > ValhallaVintageVerb long on a 2nd Return, automated > Melodyne.
- Forum, not read (Gearspace snippet, 'Brooklyn Baby'): a simple mic > pre > comp, and the sound came from her moving the mic by hand plus real plates and delays ❓.

**The Cure: forum guess.**
Source: [homerecording.com: Vocals - The Cure/The Killers](https://homerecording.com/bbs/threads/vocals-the-cure-the-killers.175117/) (forum posters, by ear)
- One poster, track by track: a normal (not thinned-out) reverb **with a good pre-delay**, so the tail doesn't run over the voice. On other songs, less pre-delay and a quick delay sitting low.
- Another poster: the base is **heavy compression and close-miking a breathy vocal**, with reverse reverb in places.
- A **long ping-pong delay into reverb**, EQ'd thin (less bass and mids), about 3 repeats, fully wet.
- The rest of the thread is jokes ("throw gobs of delay on the vocal").
- This is the only source anywhere that names compression on Smith's voice, and it's a guess by ear.

**The Weeknd and Travis Scott: blogs and preset sellers** (RysUp, Cedar Sound, MixingGPT, Antares blog). Treat these as forum grade.
- The Weeknd: Auto-Tune retune at about 15-25 ms for natural tuning; reverb a short plate or small hall with 30-60 ms pre-delay at about 10-15% wet; highs rolled off on the delays; reverb and delay ducked under the vocal. ([RysUp](https://rysupaudio.com/blogs/news/how-to-sound-like-the-weeknd), [Cedar Sound](https://www.cedarsoundstudios.com/blogs/news/how-to-mix-your-vocals-like-the-weeknd-mixing-guide))
- Travis: retune speed 0 for the effect; Auto-Tune on the dry vocal first, then delays, then reverb; "the delay is doing as much work as the pitch correction" ([Antares blog](https://www.antarestech.com/blog/auto-tune-for-rap-hip-hop-how-trap-producers-use-pitch-correction)). A trailing echo on its own track at about 500 ms with 20-30% feedback ([Cedar Sound](https://www.cedarsoundstudios.com/blogs/news/how-to-mix-your-vocals-like-travis-scott-mixing-guide)). The sources disagree on the reverb (long vs short).
- Paid engineer videos exist but were not watched ❓: [Mix With The Masters, Illangelo *After Hours*](https://www.mixwiththemasters.com/videos/illangelo-the-weeknd-after-hours) and Tom Elmhirst on Travis 'My Eyes'.

**Crystal Castles: still no vocal source.** Forums only discuss the overall overdriven, clipped mix. Tutorials suggest a **parallel** bitcrushed copy under the clean vocal, filtered narrow ❓ technique, not the band's method.

| Idea from the wider sources | What he owns |

### K. Nick Cave, Deftones, Midwest emo (added 11-12 on Amir's ask)

**Nick Cave, *Push The Sky Away* (2013). Producer/engineer Nick Launay: engineer says.**
Source: [Paul Tingen, "Nick Cave & The Magical Fabrique"](https://tingen.org/?p=1171) (first published in AudioTechnology issue 95, 2013)

### G. Auto-Tune in a chain (Amir uses it). **engineer says**
Source: [SOS: Vocal Fixes](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/vocal-fixes) (Oct 2003, Dan Daley). An old article, but the engineers' methods still apply.

- Josh Binder (engineer for Walter Afanasieff): even on singers who are in tune, a **chromatic Auto-Tune with a mellow response** gives "a nice chorus/flanging effect". He prints it to its own track and blends it into the comped vocal: "It's not there to fix the vocal; it's there to be part of the vocal sound."
- Donal Hodgson (Sting, Counting Crows): Auto-Tune in automatic mode with the **bypass and the response time automated**, so only certain syllables get fixed. He has had it on the **monitor path while recording** so the singer stopped worrying about pitch and relaxed. For bigger problems he uses manual (graph) mode.
- Joe Barresi (Queens of the Stone Age): keep the correction light and close to the real pitch; nudge the track 2-4 ms earlier to make up for the delay; tune while the track plays, because a vocal tuned perfectly against a sharp guitar sounds worse.
- Not found: an engineer-documented chain in his genre or Lana's that names Auto-Tune ❓. In Lana's two SOS pieces, pitch correction is not mentioned.
- Seth Firkins (Future, [SOS May 2017](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4916478)): Auto-Tune is always on while recording, but on an **aux the singer hears**, never on the record track. That aux runs Auto-Tune > quarter-note delay > room reverb at 12-15% wet, 12 ms pre-delay. "His confidence is up, and he can experiment a lot more." The Auto-Tune is printed afterwards. Mix bus order: EQ (top lifted above 5.2 kHz) > Waves De-Esser at 5.5 kHz, hitting about once every 10 words > Renaissance Compressor > then effects.
- Illangelo (The Weeknd, see H): Auto-Tune EFX is the **first** insert, very subtle, before the de-esser and EQ.

| Method | What he owns |
|---|---|
| Correction or the effect | Auto-Tune Artist, UAD Auto-Tune Realtime Access/Advanced/X, Waves Tune Real-Time, Melodyne (graph editing) |
| Binder's parallel blend | Live Audio Effect Rack: a dry chain + an Auto-Tune chain (chromatic, slow retune), chain volume low; or print it to its own track |
| Hodgson's live monitor tune | UAD Auto-Tune Realtime (low latency on the Apollo ❓) or Waves Tune Real-Time on the monitoring track, while the dry take is recorded |
| Hodgson's per-word fix | Automate Auto-Tune's bypass + Retune Speed in Live |

## 3. Patterns across A-D (engineer-stated)

- A dynamic mic is fine for loud, hard singers (Talbot, Casey: SM7). Condensers for soft parts.
- One "colour" compressor early (LA-2A, Distressor, CL-1A) and a **parallel dirty copy** blended in (Casey's 1176, Salogni's Devil-Loc + Radiator).
- Remove the harsh band rather than boosting (Smith 3 kHz notch, Salogni's soothe).
- Effects are part of the performance: Gahan sings into them, Hedges builds the delays and flangers while tracking.
- Level riding by hand matters as much as the plug-ins (Salogni, Tolomei).
