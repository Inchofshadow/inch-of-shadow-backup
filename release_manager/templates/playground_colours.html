Smart shuffle uses the rules we collected in the Effects library: 60-30-10 colour (one dominant, one secondary, one accent; R-013), value 70/20/10 (one tone carries most of the picture, the darkest or lightest bit sits at the focal point), and the accent goes on the focal point, the moon. Plus readability: the moon is never the hill's colour and always reads on the sky, the owl stands out from its moon, the trees read against the hill. Rule of thirds and the golden ratio are about placement, so they come in at the text stage. Shuffle stays fully random.
