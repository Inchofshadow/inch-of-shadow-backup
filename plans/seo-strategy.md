## 2. Spotify Strategy

### Profile Setup (Do Once, Update Per Release)

- **Bio (1,500 chars):** Lead with "Berlin-based dark disco and acid producer." Use genre keywords the NLP reads: dark disco, acid, electro, EBM, techno, cinematic, melancholic. @tag collaborators and influences. Update time-sensitive section per release.
- **Artist Pick (140 chars):** Rotate every 2 weeks. Pin current release or curated playlist.
- **Canvas:** Upload for every release. Subtle, atmospheric loops (3-8 sec). Moody visuals matching track mood. Saves +114%, shares +145%.
- **Gallery:** 6 images. Studio shots, live performance, artwork variants.
- **Social links:** Instagram, Bandcamp, SoundCloud.

### Pitch Workflow (Per Release)

**Timeline:** Submit pitch 31-40 days before release date.

**Pitch structure (500 chars max):**
1. Emotional hook -- make the editor feel the track in one line
2. Context -- inspiration, Berlin scene, what triggered the track
3. Sound -- mood-focused: "late-night driving music" not "128 BPM techno"
4. Playlist suggestion -- name 1-2 editorial playlists (Techno Bunker, Warehouse Party, Altar)
5. Promo plan -- video, social strategy, premieres

**Metadata fields (feed the algorithm directly):**
- Genre: 1 main + up to 3 subgenres (be precise -- mislabeling = rejection)
- Mood: 2 selections (mood playlists > genre playlists in 2025-2026)
- Instruments: synthesizer, drum machine
- Music cultures: up to 3
- Location: Berlin

**What to avoid:**
- Generic claims ("this is a hit")
- Biography instead of track description
- Wrong genre tags (top rejection reason)
- Incomplete profile or form fields
- Pitching less than 7 days before release

### Algorithm Signals (Ranked)

1. **Saves** -- strongest signal. 200 saves on 1K streams > 10 saves on 10K streams.
2. **Listener retention** -- play-through rate matters more than stream count.
3. **Repeat listens** -- indicates genuine connection.
4. **Playlist additions** -- both user and editorial.
5. **Skip rate** -- strong negative signal.

**First 72 hours are critical.** Spotify tests with initial audience pools. Strong early engagement = broader distribution.

### Discovery Mode

Not accessible until 25,000 monthly listeners. Long-term tool. 30% commission on recording royalties in Discovery Mode contexts only. Boosts Radio and Autoplay placement.

## 3. YouTube Strategy

**Source of truth for YouTube: [youtube/PLAYBOOK.md](../../youtube/PLAYBOOK.md) (2026-09-13).** Where this section and the playbook disagree, the playbook wins.

### Channel Setup

- **Name:** Inch of Shadow (no "Official", "Music", etc.)
- **Description (1,000 chars):** Genre keywords, bio, streaming links, social links.
- **OAC:** Requested through Symphonic's OAC form (submitted 2026-09-08, ~1 month). Metadata must match exactly (artist name, ISRCs).
- **Playlists:** 5+ organized by release, mood ("Dark Disco Floor", "Late Night Acid"), and content type.

### Video Titles

**Format:** `Inch of Shadow - [Song Title] (Official Audio)` or `(Official Music Video)`

- Keep important info in first 50 chars (safe across desktop, mobile, Shorts)
- "(Official Audio)" / "(Official Music Video)" is the standard music format (the "~23% more clicks" figure is unsourced)
- A/B test up to 3 titles/thumbnails (Test & Compare): winner = watch time share, needs advanced features, low-view videos often end with no winner
- Consistent formatting helps YouTube Music associate with OAC

### Video Descriptions

**Structure:**
```
[Song Title] by Inch of Shadow. [Genre] from [EP name].
Stream on Spotify: [link]

[1-2 sentence track description -- mood, story, instruments]

Written and produced by Inch of Shadow.
Mixed and mastered by Inch of Shadow at Ambertone Studios.
BPM: [X] | Key: [X]

Stream everywhere:
Spotify: [link]
Apple Music: [link]
Bandcamp: [link]
Beatport: [link]

Follow:
Instagram: [link]
SoundCloud: [link]

[Full lyrics if applicable]

#InchOfShadow #[SongTitle] #DarkDisco #ElectronicMusic #Berlin
```

- First 150 chars = most important (visible before "Show more")
- Include lyrics -- major SEO win for lyric-search queries
- Avoid "Out now" / "New 2026" -- dates the content
- 3-5 hashtags at end (first 3 show above title)

### Tags

Minimal role (YouTube's own docs; "~1%" is not an official figure). Fill in, don't over-invest. Never tag other artists' names.
- Specific: "Inch of Shadow", "dark disco", "acid electro"
- Category: "electronic music", "EBM", "Berlin techno"
- Broad: "music", "underground electronic"

### Shorts Strategy

- Separate recommendation system from long-form (on record since 2023; "decoupled in late 2025" is a blog myth)
- 15-35 seconds for hooks/teasers, 45-60 for fuller clips
- Strong first frame: for Shorts it matters more than a custom thumbnail (YouTube)
- Completion and replays help (no official threshold; the "70%" figure is unsourced)
- #Shorts hashtag is not required
- Link each Short to the full track with the related video link (needs advanced features)
- Real funnel to the full track: fans discovered a song on Shorts and watched long-form within a week 700M times (YouTube for Artists)
- Burn in captions (most consumed without sound)
- Content: track previews, studio BTS, sound design, gear footage

### Thumbnails

- 1280x720, under 2MB, mobile-first design
- Simplicity: one subject, one message, 4 words max text
- Dark/moody works if enough contrast to pop at small sizes
- Custom thumbnails and A/B tests (up to 3 variants) need advanced features: verify the channel first
- Drives CTR which is a primary ranking signal

## 4. Bandcamp Strategy

- **Bio (400 chars):** Genre, location, sound identity. Gets cut off after 400.
- **Album descriptions:** SEO-rich. Bandcamp pages rank well in Google. Include genre keywords, production details, mood, equipment, credits.
- **Tags (10-15 per release):** Match Bandcamp Discover spellings. Layer: genre > subgenre > location > mood > instrumentation.
- **Profile-level genre tags:** Auto-apply to all releases. Set: electronic, dark disco, acid, techno, Berlin.

**Core tags:** electronic, dark disco, acid, techno, electro, EBM, Berlin, underground, synth, dark

- Original audio on Reels for algorithmic boost

### Content Types

- Track teasers (5-8 sec hooks)
- Studio/production BTS
- Performance clips (original audio = algorithm boost)

## 6. Beatport Strategy

Not used for new releases since bridge plan v2 (2026-09-13); kept for the back catalogue.

All releases go through Symphonic distributor.

### Genre Selection (Critical)

Each track needs 1 primary + up to 2 subgenres. Genre tagging is the #1 discoverability factor.

| Track Type | Primary Genre | Subgenres |
|------------|--------------|-----------|
| Dark disco (110-122 BPM) | Nu Disco | Indie Dance, Electronica |
| Acid techno (128-135 BPM) | Techno (Peak Time / Driving) | Electro (Classic/Detroit/Modern), Minimal / Deep Tech |
| EBM/industrial (120-130 BPM) | Electronica | Techno (Raw / Deep), Electro |
| Downtempo (<110 BPM) | Electronica | Indie Dance, Melodic House & Techno |

- "Dark disco" is not a Beatport genre -- map to closest match
- Extended Mix versions preferred by DJs
- Less competitive genres = better chart potential

## 7. SoundCloud Strategy

- **Title (100 chars):** Include artist name and track name clearly.
- **Description (4,000 chars):** Front-load keywords. Include BPM/key for DJs. Cross-platform streaming links.
- **Caption (140 chars):** Short "tweet" for the upload. Hook + genre.
- **Tags (5 max, 20 chars each):** Choose carefully. Example: dark disco, acid, techno, Berlin, electro.
- **Custom genre field:** Searchable. Be specific.

## 8. Apple Music Strategy

- **Bio:** Q&A format (answer Apple's predetermined questions). First person, conversational. No press releases, all caps, or exclamation points.
- **Editorial pitching:** Through Symphonic distributor only. No direct artist pitching.
- **Shazam:** Counts are a strong leading indicator for editorial attention.
- **Motion artwork (optional):** 3840x3840 square + 2048x2732 portrait, 15-35 sec, ProRes 422/4444.
- **Title rules:** No version info ("Original Mix", "Album Version"). No explicit markers in title.

## 11. Key Insights (2026)

1. **Saves > streams** on Spotify. Encourage saving, not just listening.
2. **Captions > hashtags** on Instagram. The AI reads your text now.
3. **Tags play a minimal role** on YouTube (YouTube's own wording). Focus on title + thumbnail + description.
4. **Shorts are a real funnel** to full tracks, not a subscriber engine. Our long-form catalog already reaches 94% non-subscribers without them (2026-09).
5. **Mood > genre** for Spotify editorial. Describe what the track is *for*, not just what it *is*.
6. **First 72 hours** determine algorithmic expansion on Spotify. Coordinate release timing.
7. **Bandcamp descriptions rank in Google.** Write them like landing pages.
8. **Spotify NLP reads your bio.** Use exact genre keywords.
9. **Consistency builds editorial trust.** Quality releases on schedule > sporadic output.
10. **All platforms strip EXIF.** Pre-strip for privacy. Your artwork resizer already does this.
