## Platform Deadlines

### Symphonic (your distributor)
- **Ideal submission:** 6 weeks before release
- **Minimum submission:** 4 weeks before release
- **Approval time:** 5 business days (after submission)
- **DSP delivery after approval:** 1-10 days
- **FastLane:** available for fee — jumps queue, speeds up review
- **Release day:** Fridays ONLY (Symphonic explicitly recommends this for playlist placement)
- **Marketing Drivers:** submit via "Market" menu in SymphonicMS for DSP features + playlist pitching
- **Video distribution:** submit at least 3 weeks before release date
- **Key rule:** fully submit release + marketing drivers together — incomplete submissions lose placement opportunities
- Source: [Symphonic Best Practices](https://blog.symphonic.com/2024/02/06/best-practices-for-maximum-release-potential-2/) | [Release Approval Process](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/202944089-Music-Distribution-Release-Approval-Process)

### Spotify Editorial Pitch
- **Hard minimum:** 7 days before release (Release Radar only at this point)
- **Recommended minimum:** 14 days (2 weeks)
- **Industry best practice:** 28 days (4 weeks) — gives editors real review time
- **How:** via Spotify for Artists dashboard — only available after Symphonic delivers the track
- **Rules:** unreleased tracks only, one track at a time, you need Admin/Editor access
- **What to include:** genre, mood, instrumentation, culture/story — metadata determines which editor sees it
- **Insight:** Chartlex data shows pitching 10-14 days out = 3x more editorial consideration vs last-minute
- Source: [Spotify Official](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/pitching-music-to-playlist-editors/) | [Chartlex Guide](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/spotify-playlist-pitching-guide-2025)

### Beatport
- **Not used for new releases** (bridge plan v2); kept as reference for the back catalogue.
- **Minimum lead time:** 2 weeks before release
- **Recommended lead time:** 3-4 weeks
- **Feature consideration window:** editors work 2 weeks in advance — deliver after that = no feature consideration
- **Approval process:** ~2 weeks (separate from submission)
- **Practical total lead time needed:** 4-6 weeks
- Source: [Beatport Lead Times](https://labelsupport.beatport.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415537098004-What-are-Beatport-s-Lead-Times-How-far-in-advance-do-I-need-to-deliver-my-music)

### Traxsource
- **Recommended lead time:** 3-4 weeks before release
- **Approval process:** ~2 weeks
- **Practical total lead time needed:** 4-6 weeks
- Source: [iMusician on Traxsource](https://imusician.pro/en/products/digital-music-distribution/how-to-sell-music-on-traxsource)

### Bandcamp
- **No fixed lead time** — you control it directly
- **Best practice:** release exclusive here 1-2 weeks before DSPs (direct sales window)
- **Bandcamp Fridays:** first Friday of each month — zero commission. Time releases to land on or just before these
- **Revenue share:** 82-85% to artist (vs Spotify's ~$0.003/stream)
- **Strategy:** use as D2F (direct-to-fan) first window before Spotify/Beatport go live

### SoundCloud
- **No lead time required** — upload directly, live immediately
- **Best practice:** upload free preview/clip 2-3 weeks before release to build hype
- **Full track:** upload on release day (free tracks build audience; premium track on other platforms)
- **Underground discovery:** SoundCloud reposts from DJs in your scene = primary organic growth mechanism

## Outreach Deadlines

### DJ Promos (your top priority for underground)
- **Send:** 4-6 weeks before release date
- **Best send days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (DJs prepare sets mid-week)
- **Quality over quantity:** 10 right DJs > 100 random ones
- **Target:** score 4-5 contacts from SI, category = DJ, genre match = acid/EBM/electro/dark disco
- **Follow-up:** 1 week after initial send if no response
- Source: [DJ Promo Best Practices](https://music.loop.fans/blog/dj-promos) | [Label Engine Strategy](https://label-engine.com/news/release-strategy/)

### Blog/Channel Premieres
- **Initial pitch:** 3-4 weeks before release
- **Confirm premiere:** 3-4 weeks before release (leave time if they decline — need backup)
- **Premiere timing:** day BEFORE official release date (builds pre-release buzz)
- **Follow-up:** 5-7 days after initial pitch if no response
- **For general coverage (not premiere):** send on release day with all streaming links
- **Target:** score 4-5 contacts from SI, category = media, sub_type = premiere/press/channel
- Source: [Ditto Music Premiere Guide](https://dittomusic.com/en/blog/how-to-get-a-blog-premiere-for-a-song)

### Label Demo Submissions
- **Timing:** no demo waves for electronic tracks. Song-era labels only, on Amir's call.
- **Best practice:** submit to active labels (discogs_last_year = 2024/2025) when they've recently released in your genre
- **Target:** score 4-5 contacts from SI, category = label, discogs_email available

## Social Media Strategy

### Release Day: Always Friday
- Global industry standard
- Spotify New Music Friday + Release Radar both refresh Fridays
- Electronic/dance music peak consumption = Friday evening through weekend
- Exception: Tuesday if you want to avoid Friday noise (less proven for electronic)
- Source: [House of Tracks](https://houseoftracks.com/page/what-is-the-best-day-to-release-music) | [Tyxstudios](https://tyxstudios.com/blog/best-day-release-music)

### Instagram Posting Times (general best)
- **Thursday 9am** — highest engagement across data sets
- **Wednesday 12pm** — second best
- **Wednesday 6pm** — strong evening slot
- **Best days:** Monday, Tuesday, Thursday
- **Stories:** evening (6-9 PM) — people browse Stories as nightly recap
- Source: [Buffer 9.6M post analysis](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/) | [Hootsuite 2025](https://blog.hootsuite.com/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/)

### Instagram Content Calendar (per release)
| Timing | Content Type | Format |
|---|---|---|
| -4 weeks | Studio/process teaser | Story or Reel |
| -3 weeks | Artwork reveal | Feed post (Thursday 9am) |
| -3 weeks | Pre-save link live | Story with Link Sticker |
| -2 weeks | Audio snippet / clip | Reel (15-30 sec) |
| -2 weeks | Countdown sticker | Story |
| -1 week | Behind the scenes | Story series |
| -1 week | "dropping Friday" reminder | Feed post or Reel |
| -1 day | Premiere live (tag blog) | Story + Feed |
| Day 0 | Release post | Feed (Thursday night or Friday morning) |
| Day 0 | All platform links | Story with Link Sticker |
| +1 week | Engagement post (thanks, DM reactions) | Story |
| +2 weeks | Chart appearance / DJ support (if any) | Feed post |

### Posting frequency during release cycle: 4-5x per week (Stories daily, Feed 2-3x)
### Pre-save campaign: launch 4 weeks out — 70% of pre-savers listen on day 1

## Key Insight for Content Calendar Builder
The critical path is: Symphonic submission → Spotify for Artists pitch window opens → pitch Spotify.
You cannot pitch Spotify until Symphonic delivers the track to Spotify, which takes 1-10 days after approval (5 business days).
So: submit to Symphonic at week -6, expect Spotify delivery by week -5, pitch Spotify at week -4.
