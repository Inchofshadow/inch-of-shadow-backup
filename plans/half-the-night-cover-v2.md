## Lessons from this session (Amir asked to record them, 2026-11-03)

1. The old handoff said "start from scratch"; I still built scenes literally from his sketch line and lost the
   feeling. Start from the FEELING and the LANGUAGE, then the composition.
2. Check our own records first: R-012 (10-14) already said Half the Night's reference is Eli Magaziner /
   Chateau Royal and that he loves 90s Batman comics. I found it two hours in. Read the release's archive refs
   and taste notes BEFORE the first board.
3. image_generator.py prepends a photo core (realism, film grain) that kills poster/illustration styles.
   Concept renders now go raw and short (half-the-night-build/raw_render.py). His 5-second ChatGPT prompt beat
   all my long prompts.
4. No palette at concept stage; colour is our own local step at the end.
5. Do not combine picks unless he asks (I merged B+C unasked).
6. Local code-drawn drafts read as generic; use them only for layout tests, never to judge a look.
7. Workflow he wants: short prompts -> he picks elements -> save as assets -> compose in Canva -> our
   colour / type / export. He guides by voice, Hebrew is fine; take his words literally.
8. Keep chat replies short and focused (he had to ask).
