# Mercor AI interview prep (2026-11-16)

Talking points, not a script. Answer in your own words, out loud.

## How it works (reported by candidates, not official Mercor text)
- About 20 min, video, camera on. An AI asks the questions, you speak, it transcribes.
- Questions come from YOUR CV: it picks the biggest claims first and digs into even one-line items.
- Domain rounds: "listen/read this, what is wrong or missing, why?" Clear reasoning counts more than jargon.

## 1. Your 2-minute intro (points to hit, in this order)
1. Producer + mixing/mastering engineer, 10+ years, based in Berlin.
2. BA (Hons.) in music production, Rimon (Tel Aviv, 2016-19).
3. Studio: Born Studios (recording, mixing, mastering, 2018-19), your own hardware studio now, releases on 4 labels + your own label, Indigo Children Records.
4. Live: 7 years FOH. TEDER.FM, Zappa (Tel Aviv); Renate, Orania, Alte Münze, Prachtwerk, Bucht der Träumer Festival (Berlin/Germany).
5. Why AI work: a trained ear that can explain what it hears; you want to judge and improve AI music and audio.

## 3. Practice questions (say each answer out loud, 1-2 min)
1. Walk me through how you mix a track from the first fader to the bounce.
2. How do you decide a master is finished? (references, LUFS/true peak, small speakers, car)
3. A vocal sounds harsh at 3-5 kHz. What do you try first, and why?
4. Kick and bass fight. Name three ways to fix it.
5. What is phase cancellation, how do you hear it, and how do you fix it?
6. How do you build a sound from scratch in a synth? (Serum, hardware)
7. You hear two AI versions of the same track. How do you say which is better, and how do you explain it?
8. What makes music sound "AI-made" to you?
9. A live show: feedback starts in the monitors mid-set. What do you do, step by step?
10. Explain sidechain compression to someone who doesn't produce.

## 4. How to answer (any question)
- Say the short answer first, then why, then a real example from your work.
- Use numbers when you have them (Hz, dB, LUFS, years, venues).
- If a question is unclear, say what you think it means, then answer. Mercor asks for people who flag unclear tasks.

## 5. Setup checklist
- Quiet room, headphones, the best mic you have, camera at eye level, good light on your face.
- Test camera, mic and internet before you start.
- CV PDF open beside you: Amir Miller CV 2026 Music and Audio.pdf.
