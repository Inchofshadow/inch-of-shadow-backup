# Groover vs SubmitHub — Decision Research (2026-06-15)

Focused deep-research for Inch of Shadow (underground Berlin EBM / dark disco / electro /
darkwave). Verified via adversarial pass: 25 claims checked, 19 confirmed, 6 killed.
Re-check live pricing + genre tag pages before spending — all figures current as of 2026-06.

## Bottom line
Both are **paid feedback/access tools, NOT placement engines.** Neither guarantees a
playlist add — you pay for guaranteed *feedback*, with a placement as an uncertain by-product
(confirmed 3-0, cuts against vendor interest). For a tight-budget niche act, treat spend as
buying curator attention + written feedback, and measure **adds**, not replies.

## Cost (confirmed high-confidence)
- **Groover:** credit-based, 1 Grooviz = 1 EUR incl. VAT. Standard curator contact = 2 Grooviz
  (~2 EUR); top curators 4-6 Grooviz (<3-10% of contacts). Bulk packs up to ~24% off. Of the
  2 Grooviz, Groover keeps 1, curator gets 1 *conditional on giving feedback*. A 25-contact
  campaign ≈ 50 EUR before discounts.
- **SubmitHub:** Premium credits ~0.79-0.98 USD each (bulk: 10 USD/10, 27 USD/30, 80 USD/100).
  Most submissions 1-3 credits. 25-Premium campaign ≈ 40-50 USD. **Deposited funds cannot be
  cashed out or refunded**; accounts inactive 12+ months may incur an inactivity fee.

## Feedback / refund model
- **Groover:** written response in 7 days or Grooviz auto-refunded (credits, not cash).
  Self-reported reply rate >85% (refs 90%); hands-on tests 84-96%. Guarantees a *response*,
  not insight — can be 2-3 generic sentences.
- **SubmitHub:** conditional, Premium credits only (free Standard credits have NO guarantee and
  queue ~20 days at the bottom). Credit auto-returned (reuse, not cash) if curator misses:
  response in 48h (96h labels), >=20s listen, >=10 words feedback on decline. Returned credits
  expire 30 days after notice.

## Niche fit — the decisive caveat for our lane
- Groover's **experimental-electronic pool skews house/techno/EDM** (Orion, BEST EDM 2026,
  Pure Groove, Chris Luno/organic house). Almost none tag dark disco / EBM / darkwave / coldwave.
- BUT Groover hosts the dark niche under **separate tag pages** — a dedicated `/coldwave/` page
  exists (skews post-punk/indie/rock). So coverage exists, just not where you'd first look.
  **Action: evaluate Groover's coldwave / darkwave / EBM / electro tag pages directly before
  paying — do not judge by the experimental-electronic pool.**
- NOTE: a specific "~19 coldwave radio stations" count was REFUTED (0-3). Do not trust curator
  headcounts; eyeball the actual live tag pages.

## Privacy (CLAUDE.md privacy-first flag)
- **SubmitHub:** shares user-submitted feedback data (non-personal) with **ChatGPT/OpenAI** for
  summaries/pull-quotes (privacy policy §16); embeds **Facebook Advanced Pixel** on SubmitHub
  Links, sharing IP + browser/device info with Meta for conversion tracking (§15; auto-deleted
  after 30 days, opt-outs exist).
- **Groover:** no equivalent LLM/ad-pixel data flow surfaced in this research — but this is an
  *open question*, not a clean bill of health. Not affirmatively cleared.

## Recommendation for IoS
1. **Free IG-DM curators first** (zero cost, zero data exposure) — already in motion off
   `playlist_curator_targets.json`. This is the ROI floor everything else is measured against.
2. **If testing paid, prefer Groover over SubmitHub** for our case: per-contact targeting (pick
   the actual dark/coldwave curator), cleaner refund-in-7-days mechanic, and no documented
   OpenAI/Meta-Pixel data flow. SubmitHub's ChatGPT + Facebook Pixel sharing conflicts with the
   privacy-first rule — surface and decide before using it.
3. **First test = small + niche-targeted:** ~20-30 EUR on Groover, hand-pick only curators whose
   live playlists already feature dark disco / EBM / darkwave / electro (Petra Flurr, Soft Crash,
   Silicodisco, Boy Harsher, Zanias adjacency). Do NOT spray the EDM pool.
4. **Measure adds, not replies.** Success = real playlist adds on listener-bearing playlists +
   measurable Spotify save/stream lift in the 7-14 days after. A wall of polite "not for us"
   feedback at 85% reply rate is the expected default, not success.
