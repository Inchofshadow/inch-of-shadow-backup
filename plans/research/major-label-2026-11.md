# Getting signed: majors and major-backed indies (research, 2026-11-05)

## 1. What labels look at
- **No official threshold exists.** No label, MBW or Billboard source publishes one. The common figures come from vendor and blog guides: 5-10k Spotify monthly listeners to get noticed, 50k+ for indie deal talks, 100-300k for majors ([orphiq](https://orphiq.com/resources/a-and-r-in-2026), [artist.tools](https://www.artist.tools/post/record-companies-looking-for-artists)). **Treat these as anecdotal.**
- **Growth matters more than size** (also anecdotal, but every source agrees): steady month-over-month growth (one guide says 30%+ for 3 months running), a streams-per-listener ratio of 3 or more, saves, no drop when a playlist ends, and fans who come to shows.

## 2. Routes that actually happen
- **Found through data.** A&R teams use scouting tools: Chartmetric, Soundcharts, and Instrumental, which runs its own label, Frtyfve ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/ar-scouting-platform-instrumental-strikes-deal-with-uks-official-charts-company)). Warner bought Sodatone for this job ([Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/?p=3599)).
- **Symphonic.** It is independent and has **no public upstream deal with a major**. What it does have: a European A&R client-development team, including a Senior A&R for the German-speaking region ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/symphonic-distribution-expands-european-ar-client-development-team-with-five-new-hires)), and Symphonic NEXT (advances, catalogue deals from about $25k a year in earnings; [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/symphonic-targets-catalog-acquisitions-and-indie-artist-financing-deals-with-launch-of-symphonic-next/)). Upside: pitching help, not a major deal.
- **Major-owned distributors as a feeder.** AWAL (Sony) has tiers: plain distribution at 15%, then selective label-level deals with negotiated splits ([MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/if-you-wanted-to-know-why-sony-music-bought-awal-this-is-why)). Virgin (UMG) now owns Downtown, FUGA and CD Baby; the EU cleared that on 13 Feb 2026 ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/universa-music-downtown-acquisition-cleared-by-eu-competition-regulator/)). ADA (Warner) Central Europe signs Berlin indies ([NIF](https://newindustryfocus.com/articles/ada-central-europe-partners-with-berlin-based-indie-label-aim-music)).
- **Showcases.** Reeperbahn (Hamburg, September) has about 5,000 delegates. Its 1:1 sessions are invite-only; labels and distributors hold their own meetings ([Symphonic](https://blog.symphonic.com/2025/08/20/everything-you-need-to-know-before-attending-reeperbahn-festival/)). Most Wanted: Music (Berlin Music Commission, November) has a live showcase ([mwm-berlin.de](https://mwm-berlin.de)). I found no public "A&R speed meeting" open to artists.
- **Cold demos** work better at genre indies than at majors.

## 3. Deal types
- **Distribution:** you keep everything and pay a fee or a percentage.
- **Licence / label services:** the label holds the masters for a fixed term, then they come back to you. Splits are negotiated ([MIDiA](https://midiaresearch.com/blog/taylor-swift-label-services-and-what-comes-next)).
- **Record deal:** the label owns the masters. New-artist royalties are about 10-25%, and the advance is recoupable ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/bad-deals-are-baked-into-the-way-the-music-industry-operates)).
- **360 deal:** the label also takes a cut of touring, merch and brand income.
- **Red flags:** ownership in perpetuity, no reversion date, cross-collateralised recoupment, a 360 cut without 360 work, upfront fees, no music lawyer.

## 4. Fit for darkwave, post-punk and dark electronic
- **Universal Music Germany (Berlin):** domestic labels are Vertigo/Capitol (rock) and Polydor/Island. The genre lineage sits at **Fiction/Polydor**, UK, home of The Cure's 2024 album ([Line of Best Fit](https://thelineofbestfit.com/news/the-cure-officially-announce-their-fourteenth-studio-album-songs-of-a-lost-world)).
- **Warner Records Germany (Berlin):** led by Lea Londa ([MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/warner-music-launches-warner-records-germany-in-berlin-led-by-lea-londa)).
- **Genre indies:**
  - **Out of Line (Berlin):** EBM and darkwave, signing debut acts in 2025 ([Side-Line](https://www.side-line.com/ghostbells-debut-single-darkness-saves)). Its distribution reportedly runs through Believe/Rough Trade and Ingrooves (UMG). **Unverified.**
  - **Mute:** owned by BMG.
  - **Sacred Bones:** belongs to the indie Secretly Group's All Flowers Group ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/sacred-bones-records-joins-secretly-groups-all-flowers-group-portfolio-of-labels)).
  - **Dais, Felte:** indie, no known major tie.

## 5. Step plan
1. **Songs first:** 3-4 singles, 6-8 weeks apart, via Symphonic. The electronic lane stays light.
2. **Build the data A&Rs see.** Targets (anecdotal): 5k Spotify monthly listeners, then 20k, streams per listener of 3 or more, a rising saves rate, and Instagram growth driven by reels with real comments.
3. **Play live in Berlin:** 4-6 shows a year, the first ones sold out at 100-200 capacity. Then apply to Most Wanted: Music Live and Reeperbahn.
4. **Use the free layers.** Ask Symphonic's German-speaking-region A&R for pitching. Apply to Initiative Musik artist funding, which is in German and gives up to about €30k ([iMusician](https://imusician.pro/de/ressourcen/blog/foerdermoeglichkeiten-musik-artists-2026)).
5. **Step up only when the numbers move.** At about 20k listeners with 3+ months of growth, pitch genre indies (Out of Line, Mute, Sacred Bones) or an AWAL or Virgin label-tier licence deal. Majors only once the curve exists, with a lawyer and manager.
