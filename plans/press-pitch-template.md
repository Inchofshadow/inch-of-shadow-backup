# Press / Radio Pitch Templates (Inch of Shadow)

Refreshed 2026-10-14 (ticket DJ-01) to match `booking-profile.md` and `docs/brand/voice.md`.
Two registers for the press channel: **press** (blogs / magazines / feature + review) and
**radio** (show / mix play). The **premiere** lane is NOT here, it uses
`plans/premiere-pitch-template.md` (one channel at a time, ladder discipline).

Voice: warm, first person, peer to peer, in Amir's voice. Not salesy. Press is an industry
register, not audience-facing clickbait.

Rules (same as the booking emails):
- Under 150 words. First line is never about Amir. One hook about THEM, one ask, one link.
- No bio paragraph, no credential paragraph. Max two credentials, half a sentence of genre.
- One link on its own line. No attachments. The EPK goes out only when they ask.
- No em dash, no double dash, no "Dear team", no "I hope this finds you well", no
  "I wanted to reach out", no three-adjective runs, no closing line that restates the ask.
- Sign "Amir". No signature block.
- Public sound = the electronic lane (dark disco, EBM, coldwave, acid, electro). Songs era
  stays out (positioning.md PUBLIC STATUS).
- Facts only from `docs/brand/proof.md`. Nothing is "released" before its date.

Placeholders: `[OUTLET]` `[HOOK]` `[TRACK]` `[VIBE]` `[DATE]` `[LINK]` `[PRIVATE]` `[SET]`.
- `[HOOK]` = one true, specific thing about the outlet (a piece they ran, a mix in their
  series, a show that fits). Source it. No hook, no email.
- `[LINK]` = the live track's SoundCloud or Spotify URL from `releases/<slug>.json`.
- `[PRIVATE]` = a private SoundCloud stream for pre-release pitches.
- `[SET]` = https://soundcloud.com/shialabiff/blank-wave-guest-mix-130-inch-of-shadow
- `[VIBE]` = a one-line sound hook (e.g. "EBM weight under an acid lead").

## Press / blog / magazine (feature or review)

**Subject:** lowercase and concrete, e.g. `new single from berlin` or `[TRACK]`

```
Hi [NAME or OUTLET],

[HOOK: the piece / mix / section of theirs that made you write, one or two lines.]

I'm Inch of Shadow, Berlin. [TRACK] is out [DATE], [VIBE]. If it fits what you cover
I'd be glad to send whatever you need.

[LINK]

Thanks for the work you do.

Amir
```

Pre-release variant: swap `[LINK]` for `[PRIVATE]` and keep "out [DATE]".

## Radio (show / mix play)

**Subject:** `track for [SHOW]` or `[TRACK]`

```
Hi [NAME],

[HOOK: the show or episode that fits, one line.]

Inch of Shadow, Berlin. New one called [TRACK], [VIBE]. I think it sits well in [SHOW].
Happy to send a WAV or a clean edit.

[LINK]

Amir
```

## Guest mix / mix series ask (magazines and media, ticket DJ-03)

```
Hi [NAME],

[HOOK: the mix or episode of theirs you listened to, one line.]

I'm Inch of Shadow, Berlin. My guest mix for Blank Wave came out in October, dark disco,
EBM and wave. I'd like to record one for [SERIES].

[SET]

Amir
```

## Routing + etiquette

- **email** route: Amir sends from his own mail. Space sends across days, never a
  single-day blast.
- **form** route (website-only contact): flag the submission URL, Amir submits through the
  outlet's own form. Do not invent an email.
- **Instagram** route (DJ-03): warm first, verified handles only, Amir's DM shape
  ("Hi, how are you? :)", warm line on their work, link alone at the end, no feedback asks).
- **premiere** lane: do NOT batch. `plans/premiere-pitch-template.md`.
- If they publish or play it: reshare on IG story within 24h and tag them (verified handle).
