## AI interview prep (topics, not a script: answer in your own words)
- Walk through one mix from start to finish: gain staging, EQ, compression, bus processing, loudness target.
- Mastering: how you judge a master (references, LUFS, true peak, translation on small speakers).
- Critical listening: hearing a problem and naming it (masking, phase, harshness, muddy low-mids, over-compression).
- Sound design: building a sound in a synth (you use Serum, hardware).
- Live sound: FOH work on Midas, Allen & Heath, Yamaha, DiGiCo; a show where something went wrong and how you fixed it.
- Theory: harmony and reading notes (BA at Rimon).
- AI tasks are often "judge and explain": say WHY one version sounds better, clearly and in short sentences.
Tips: quiet room, headphones, good mic, camera on. Speak slowly and give concrete examples.
