# Radar expansion ideas (2026-11-05, proposal only, nothing built)

Cost basis from past runs: an insight run (no competitor check) costs about $0.30-1.05 in Claude.
A crowding check costs about $6-7 per product profile (that is about 90% of all radar spend so far).
HikerAPI only ever gets the leftover of the 300/day cap, after the IG agent.
Inch of Shadow uses come first.
