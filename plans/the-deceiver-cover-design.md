# The Deceiver — Cover Design Recipe

Built in Canva, 2026-07 (from a blank canvas). Recorded for consistency on future releases.

## Concept
- **Image:** Michelangelo's Pietà (cutout), thematically tied to betrayal/deception (Judas = the original deceiver; the Pietà is the aftermath of the ultimate betrayal).
- **Style:** modern **Sisters of Mercy** — cold, high-contrast blue monochrome, analog/xerox grit. Post-punk graphic, NOT fine-art.
- **Pair with Farewell:** carried by *treatment*, not identical art. **Farewell = warm/red + gold title. The Deceiver = cold/blue, blue title.** Two temperatures of one world.

## Format
- **Square, 3000 × 3000 px.** Export high-res PNG.

## Composition
- **Corridor:** two gradient "wall" shapes angling inward (a V), dark center column + dark floor triangle. Reads like a nave / light down a corridor.
- **Figure:** Pietà centered, **scaled up**, anchored low into the floor.
- **Reflection:** figure mirrored below on a "wet floor," faded out toward the bottom (transparency ~50–60% or a gradient fade). This solved the empty-floor problem and is core to the SoM look.
- **Title:** above the figure, centered, with clean headroom above her head.

## Colors
- **Center / background:** `#06090F` (deep blue-black — never pure `#000`).
- **Wall gradient:** `#34506E` (light, top) → `#1E3049` (mid) → `#06090F` (dark, bottom). Light at top = corridor light.
- **Figure:** Canva **Polar** filter, 100 (icy blue, high contrast). Polar beat a custom duotone — kept it. Push **Contrast** if more punch wanted.
- **Title `THE DECEIVER`** (hero — brightest text): icy blue `#C7E5F6` (or electric `#4FB2E8`).
- **Artist `INCH OF SHADOW`** (recedes): dimmer `#8FB2CC` / `#6E90AD`.
- Rule: hierarchy from **size + brightness**, never weight. Text keyed to the figure's light.

## Type
- **Font: Cinzel** (Roman inscription serif — matches the sculpture). ALL CAPS. **Regular weight** (never bold — Cinzel bold gets clunky).
- **Wide letter-spacing** (tracking) for the engraved look.
- `THE DECEIVER` clearly larger than `INCH OF SHADOW`.
- **Spelling watch:** it's **de-CEI-ver** ("i before e except after c") and **SHADOW** — both have bitten before.

## Texture (what makes it feel real, not digital)
- **Grain / xerox** overlay over *everything* (figure + text + walls), on top of the stack, low opacity (~10–25%). Adds analog post-punk grit.
- **Marble texture** on the walls (Soft Light / Overlay blend, or transparency ~25%) — gives the walls a real *surface* instead of a smooth digital gradient.
- **Vignette** low opacity — darkens outer edges, adds depth, pulls the eye in.
- Key lesson: a smooth gradient always reads "digital"; fine grain isn't enough — walls need **mid-scale material texture** to feel like a surface.

## Reusable for the next release
- **Cinzel titles** + the cold-blue / warm-red pairing logic.
- The "**figure + corridor + wet reflection + grit**" system can become a recurring visual language for the project.
