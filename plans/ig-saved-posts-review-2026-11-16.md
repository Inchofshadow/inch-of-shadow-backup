## 8. RAPVILLE trial reel generator (trial-reel-generator.rapville.uk), Amir's test 11-16
Amir commented on a RAPVILLE post and got the link. He tested it with a random Berlin courtyard photo and the brief "melancholic dream pop song". Read from his 16 phone screenshots; the link itself was not opened.
- **What it does, in order:** reads one frame → finds a "text zone" (the free sky) → subject and outfit → location → emotion ("quiet melancholy, feeling stuck, autumn nostalgia") → 6 colour swatches → 6 text hooks → 4 grades → 3 cut lengths → framing moves → fonts → effects (more or fewer) → an **export list of 12 versions, strongest first**. The page also flags a problem: "the bottom 30% is covered by the caption." A "book your next shoot" upsell follows every section.
- **Hooks:** 6 statements, under 8 words, each with a type: confession, callout, before/after, reframe, challenge. Each hook has a set place, a start time, a hold time, a grade and a font.
- **Grades:** A Original Crisp, B Warm Fade, C Grey Story, D Punchy Pop, given as CapCut values plus a one-tap IG filter. **Cuts:** Full (verse to last chorus line), 12 s (2 bars before the chorus to its last line), 7 s (the first chorus line only, cut the second it lands, "never mid-word"). **Framing:** Mirror (flip every second version), 1.4x hook crop, slow zoom 1.0 to 1.3, hold the ending on the sky.
- **Already ours:** batch hook versions from one build (HyperFrames variables, REEL-CRAFT), swap the first 2 s, text hook in a calm area, Lightroom grades (85 recipes), safe zones (`formats.md`), mirror ≈ clone effect, before/after hook (IOS-REEL-CRAFT).
- **New for us:** (a) a text-zone finder per frame that also keeps clear of the caption area; for us it would avoid the face too; (b) one fixed type per hook (confession / callout / before-after / reframe / challenge); (c) the three cut lengths tied to the song (2 bars before the chorus, first chorus line); (d) a ranked export list as the build sheet: hook x grade x length x framing x effects.
- **Not for us:** his fonts (we use Albertus Nova / Marcellus), CapCut and IG filter one-taps (we render locally), Punchy Pop for a moody song, and "stay until the chorus, then tell me" (asks for feedback in the copy). Hook lines are in Amir's voice, so he writes or approves them in the main chat.
- **Second set of screenshots (input, funnel, posting rules):** the input is one screenshot plus one sentence about the song ("Built from 300+ of RAPVILLE's best trial reels"). The funnel is a ManyChat keyword (DREAM), an end card in the last 2 s ("song out now · comment DREAM") and caption line 1. Posting rules: (1) never post the same file twice, change at least 2 of hook, grade, crop or length; (2) trial reels only, the feed is for fans; (3) post 3-4 at a time; (4) let a winner run 72 h, share it to the feed only after views stay flat 2 days in a row, then make 4 more versions of that hook and grade. Mirror crops come in 1.2x, 1.4x and 1.6x.
  - Trials find new people and the feed serves fans: **already ours** (`mosseri-guide.md` trial reels, REEL-CRAFT).
  - The 72 h rule, sharing to the feed only once views have flattened, and 4 more versions of the winner: **new**. We have no timing rule for when a trial goes to the feed.
  - Change 2 things per version: **contradicts** our test design. Our round-one plan (`research-content-language-2026-11-05.md`, experiment 5) changes one thing so we learn which one worked. His version spreads the reach; ours tells us why a reel won. Both are possible: one change while testing, two once we are scaling a winner.
  - ManyChat keyword and the "comment DREAM" end card: **contradicts** the 10-11 decision (no service) and the comment-bait note in `mosseri-guide.md`.
- **Verdict:** the structure is worth copying, the content is not. It is close to what HyperFrames can already render. The missing pieces are the text-zone check, the cut lengths and the export sheet.

- **E.** Build our own version: one frame + one song → text zone (clear of face and caption), 3 cuts from the song structure, Amir's approved hook lines by type, our grades and fonts → 8-12 HyperFrames renders for trial reels. Bigger job = its own ⚙️ ticket and side chat, only on his yes.
