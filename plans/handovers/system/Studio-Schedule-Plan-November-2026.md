## Three options

A day = 2 shifts (for example 10:00-17:00 and 17:00-24:00, times still to decide).

| Option | Amir | Yuval | Anthony | Good | Less good |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Days | 1 fixed day | 1 fixed day | 2 fixed days | Simplest to remember | Anthony may not be free for 2 full days |
| B. Shifts | 1 fixed day | 1 fixed day | 4 fixed shifts | Fits around his job | 4 slots to track |
| C. Mix | 1 fixed day | 1 fixed day | 1 fixed day + 2 fixed shifts | Middle ground | Slightly less simple than A |

All three give Anthony the same amount of time: 4 shifts a week. Only the shape changes.

The week has 14 shifts: Amir 2, Yuval 2, Anthony 4, and **6 shifts stay free** for anyone to book. That is where Amir and Yuval get extra time.

**Amir suggests:** Amir and Yuval each keep one full permanent day (mixing and mastering need long sessions). Anthony picks the shape (A, B or C) that suits him. He feels heard, and Amir and Yuval lose nothing because the amount is the same.

**Yuval:** is one full fixed day a week enough for you? Is 4 shifts a week the right amount for Anthony, or more, or less? Happy to let him choose A, B or C, or would you rather fix one?

## How November would work

1. Build the whole November schedule up front, once Anthony has answered.
2. Every slot is fixed: same days and times each week.
3. Swaps happen in the WhatsApp group (Amir, Yuval, Anthony).
4. A swap counts only after the other person clearly says yes in the group.
5. Whoever asked for the swap updates the schedule right away.
6. Free slots can be booked in the group with 24h notice, first come first served.
7. Every shift ends with the room clean and the gear back to default.
8. End of November: look at how it went and keep or change it for December.

**Yuval:** any rule you would change, add or drop?
