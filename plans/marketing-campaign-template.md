## Campaign Timeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Production (8-6 weeks before release)

- [ ] Final master approved
- [ ] Artwork created and resized (Tool 2)
- [ ] Video/visual content shot or sourced
- [ ] Spotify Canvas created (3-8 sec loop, 1080x1920, <8MB)
- [ ] YouTube thumbnail designed (1280x720; 3 A/B variants only if the channel has advanced features)
- [ ] 2-3 Shorts clips prepared (15-35 sec each, max 1080p, burned-in captions)

### Phase 2: Distribution & Pitch (5-4 weeks before release)

- [ ] Release submitted to Symphonic
- [ ] Spotify pitch submitted (31-40 days before release)
  - Genre/subgenre selections: ___
  - Mood selections: ___
  - Pitch text (500 chars): ___
- [ ] Apple Music pitch through Symphonic
- [ ] Pre-save links generated

### Phase 3: Pre-Release Promo (3-2 weeks before release)

- [ ] Instagram teaser posts (keyword-rich captions, 3-5 niche hashtags)
- [ ] Instagram Reel with original audio (5-8 sec hook)
- [ ] YouTube Shorts teaser published
- [ ] Pre-save link shared across all platforms
- [ ] Story/behind-the-scenes content prepared

### Phase 4: Release Week

- [ ] YouTube video published per youtube/PLAYBOOK.md section 5; verify made for kids = No on the live video
- [ ] Additional Shorts published (1-2)
- [ ] Bandcamp release live (SEO-rich description, 10-15 tags)
- [ ] SoundCloud upload (title, description, 5 tags, caption)
- [ ] Instagram release post (keyword caption, 3-5 hashtags, alt text)
- [ ] Spotify Artist Pick updated (140 chars)
- [ ] YouTube community post (needs advanced features)
- [ ] Monitor first 72 hours (Spotify saves/retention critical)

### Phase 5: Post-Release (weeks 1-4)

- [ ] Weekly Shorts content
- [ ] Engage comments across all platforms
- [ ] Rotate Spotify Artist Pick after 2 weeks
- [ ] Collect platform links for Release Manager
- [ ] Share streaming milestones (if notable)
- [ ] Email outreach to blogs/playlists/DJs (Tool 4 -- when built)
