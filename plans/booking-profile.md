## Who actually books (Amir, 2026-10-14)
In Berlin the parties are usually organised by labels and party organisers who bring their own
lineups. The venue desk rarely picks a single DJ. So for every room: find the night that fits,
find who runs it, and pitch them. The venue email is the fallback, not the plan.
