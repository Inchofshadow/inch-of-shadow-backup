The 4 styles (one idea each, so the test tells us something):

1. **Moon pulse:** the red moon and owl breathe on the kick, gold stars blink on the hi-hats. Clip: Half the Night acid hook.
2. **Through the trees:** slow push through the ivory tree layers (front sharp, back soft, multiplane), the red/cyan edge shimmers on the 303. Clip: Half the Night rolling arp.
3. **Cover builds:** the cover assembles one layer per bar (hill, trees, moon, owl), full picture lands on the drop. Clip: Pinocchio drop.
4. **Acid line:** the cover dimmed, one thin ivory line drawn live by the 303 across the moon (an oscilloscope, drawn locally). Clip: Pinocchio 303 leads.

All local. No AI on any image. If a style ever needs an AI step, I show the exact prompt first and wait for your go.
On-screen hook text (if any) is a draft only, approved by you in the main EP chat before use.

## How each piece is measured

Bar set now, before anything goes out (Mosseri: decide "it worked" first).

- **Baseline to beat** (his own numbers, `plans/ig-content-performance-log.md`): skip rate 75.5%, about 65% gone by 0:02, 6 s average watch, reels reached 341-455.
- **Trial reels (B), read at 72 h:** views, skip rate, average watch time, sends and saves per 1,000 views, follows.
  - Round 1 winner = lowest skip rate; sends per 1,000 views breaks a tie.
  - "Beat the bar" (goes to the grid) = skip rate under 65% **and** views at least 2x the weakest trial of its round.
- **Release-day reel (D):** non-follower share (target: above 55%, his reels' range), views per follower vs 0.77x, profile visits and follows in 7 days.
- **Canvas (C):** Spotify for Artists Monday read. Hypothesis, not checked: S4A may not give Canvas its own number; then we only log that it ran.
- **The songs question:** which style held people longest (skip rate, average watch). Written into this file and the perf log on 12-21. Carrying it into `docs/brand/reel-craft.md` is a rule change: your yes + Fable gate.
