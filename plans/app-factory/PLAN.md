## Open questions
- Who is the seller on the App Store: Amir as a person, or a separate name? (Shown on the store page.)
- Privacy cost of selling in the EU (checked 2026-11-15 at Apple's App Store Connect help, "Manage EU Digital Services Act trader requirements"):
  a trader must have contact details verified and SHOWN on the product page in all 27 EU countries.
  As an individual that means address (or P.O. box), phone and email in public. An alternate address needs proof (a receipt or bill).
  As an organisation, the D-U-N-S address shows automatically. Apps without trader status are removed from the EU store.
  Decide this before the $99 sign-up: a P.O. box plus a separate phone/email, or a company.
