## Short version

1. In the window 20 Sep to 5 Nov 2026 nothing official changed the music basics (library sound, 60 s cap, Personal account, Artist certification, TikTok for Artists).
2. The one confirmed rule change: new Community Guidelines in force since **2026-09-24**. The For You ineligibility wording is now "unoriginal or reused material without anything new". The watermark example is no longer in the text. AI labeling of realistic scenes or people is required.
3. Creator accounts are reported gone; creator tools now sit in the Personal account. Advice unchanged: Personal, never Business.
4. Bigger 2026 music changes sit just before the window and were missing from the 09-20 file: Apple Music "Play Full Song" and "Listening Party" (Feb to Mar 2026), TikTok cutting label-facing music jobs to push SoundOn (May 2026), a reported move to per-play royalty counting (unconfirmed).
5. EU: the DSA addictive-design case is still at preliminary findings (Feb 2026). No final decision or fine found. Age checks across the EU since Jan 2026.
6. US joint venture: no confirmed effect on EU users or EU feeds found.
7. Scene evidence: post-punk and darkwave breakouts on TikTok almost always came from **other people's videos using the song** (fashion, nostalgia, edits, covers), not from the artist's own posts. Molchat Doma, Mareux, Ekkstacy, Wisp (shoegaze, adjacent), NESYA, Sinnerella.
8. MIDiA: TikTok has the weakest follower-to-listener conversion of the big platforms (26% vs 45% for other socials). Use TikTok to make the sound usable and findable, measure saves, keep Instagram and Spotify as the core.

## 4. Entry plan for Inch of Shadow

### What the evidence says [evidence]
- A sound in TikTok's library and a certified Artist Account unlock everything else (base file points 3 and 4).
- Cadence is up to the creator; quality over daily (base file, Creator Academy). Buffer: 2 to 5 posts a week gave the clearest lift, median views flat (base file).
- In this scene, reach came from other people using the sound (section 3).
- MIDiA: TikTok converts followers to listeners worst of the big platforms. Measure saves and Spotify, not views.

### When to open [judgement]
- **Now, before the EP, without posting pressure.** Reason: certification is tied to a delivered track, not to the EP. If Farewell, The Deceiver or Pinocchio were delivered to TikTok through Symphonic, he can certify now and the review clock (up to 30 days) runs before December. Step 0 is a 2 minute check in SymphonicMS: is TikTok ticked as a store for those releases, and is the sound live in the app.
- If no track is on TikTok: open the Personal account now anyway (handle `inch_of_shadow`, same as IG), tick TikTok and set the Clip Start Time for both Half the Night tracks at submission, apply for certification the day the sound appears.
- First post 2 to 3 weeks before the EP, so the account is not empty when the sound goes live. Not 6 to 8 weeks: that number in the 09-20 file came from the certification review time, not from posting.

### Rhythm a one-person act can keep [judgement, inside the decided Formats-04 cadence]
- 2 to 3 posts a week, the same clean files as Reels and Shorts (already decided 09-21). One of the three may be TikTok-only when a format below earns it.
- Library sound picked in the app on every post that uses his music. Batch on one day, schedule up to 30 days ahead in TikTok Studio web (base file, [OFFICIAL]).
- No engagement automation on TikTok. The guidelines ban it, and a ban now reportedly reaches linked accounts.

Each line says where it comes from. All wordless or near wordless, inside the "Never" list in formats.md.

1. **The 60 s Clip Start Time section, one performance take at a strong spot** (cemetery or Tempelhofer Feld). Source: TikTok artist page, performance + scenic spot [OFFICIAL, base file].

4. **Lyric line on screen over a slow face or hands shot**, one line per clip. Source: mute figure [OFFICIAL]; scene clips lean on mood and text [REPORTED, Molchat poet explainer].

6. **Outfit or styling clip to the hook** (sunglasses, the look). Source: Molchat Doma's wardrobe-switch trend [REPORTED]. Within his rules: no dance, no lip-sync.
7. **Neukölln night streetlights walk, the drop as the cut.** Source: nostalgia/aesthetic montages carried the scene [REPORTED]; first-2-seconds rule [OWN].
8. **A short cover of an 80s new wave or post-punk song in his sound**, if he ever wants one (needs a cover licence through Symphonic). Source: Mareux and Sinnerella [REPORTED]. Taste call, his only.

10. **"By Artist" pinned video for each EP sound**: the best of 1 to 9, pinned on the sound page. Source: TikTok for Artists By Artist [OFFICIAL, base file].

### What to measure [mix]
- Per post: 2 s retention and average watch vs his Reels baseline (6 s average, ~50 to 65% gone by 0:02) [OWN].
- Per sound: video creations by others (TikTok for Artists song insights) [OFFICIAL]. This is the scene's real lever.
- Add to Music App saves, Spotify listeners and followers in the same week (S4A Monday read) [process already exists].
- Share of views from non-followers; country split.

### Go / no-go checkpoint [judgement]
- **After 8 weeks of posting** (about 20 posts; for a mid-Dec start that is mid-Feb 2027, before the bridge single).
- GO (keep 2 to 3 a week): any one of these: a post over 2x his median views, at least 5 videos by others using an EP sound, or average watch above his Reels baseline.
- REDUCE to passthrough only (Reels files, no TikTok-only work): none of the three, and saves plus Spotify movement in TikTok weeks indistinguishable from other weeks.
- Never judge on views alone (MIDiA).
