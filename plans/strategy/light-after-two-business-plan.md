## 1. Project Overview

The artist is patient, not chasing fast profit, and willing to invest 12-24+ months into catalog compounding. ## 3. Strategic Read

### What the data actually says
1. **Silver Sky is the whole business right now.** 98% of traction from one track on 32 Asian cafe playlists. This is both a breakthrough and a fragile single point of failure.
2. **The algorithmic neighborhood is correct.** "Fans also like" shows real neighboring lofi artists -- Spotify's recommendation engine has successfully slotted Light After Two into the lofi cluster. This is the hardest thing to achieve and it's already done.
3. **Asian cafe lofi is the right niche and LAT is correctly positioned on it.** The Korean/Taiwanese/Southeast Asian concentration + mature demographic is the highest-value lofi audience that exists.
4. **0% editorial placement = biggest unclaimed lever on Spotify.**
5. **No YouTube presence = the single largest revenue hole in the plan.** YouTube lofi livestream economics are 10-100x more favorable than Spotify for this genre.
6. **Weak retention (3.6 str/listener, 4% super listeners).** Most listeners pass through Silver Sky once and never return because there's no cohesive sibling catalog yet.

### Is this a good business?
**Yes, and stronger than a surface read suggests.** LAT is at a rare inflection point: one track cracked the highest-value corner of lofi streaming on its first 10-track attempt. The next 3-6 months decide whether this becomes a catalog business or a one-hit accident. Given the artist's patience and the existing tooling we can adapt, the time investment is justifiable. Realistic 12-24 month trajectory with proper execution:

- Month 3-6: 1,500-3,000 monthly listeners
- Month 6-12: 5,000-15,000 monthly listeners
- Month 12-24: 20,000-50,000 monthly listeners
- **Revenue:** Spotify payouts immaterial until month 12+; YouTube livestream (if launched) will dwarf DSP revenue from month 2 onward -- potentially $500-5,000/month range.

## 4. 2026 Market Reality (Research-Backed)

### Spotify 2026 payout
- Per-stream rate forecast: **$0.0042-$0.0058** (TuneCore 2026, Ditto 2026)
- US/UK streams pay ~$0.0052+, emerging/Asian markets pay ~$0.0012
- **LAT realistic math:** 15,930 12-month streams × ~$0.0015 weighted = ~$24 gross royalties over the last year
- **Spotify paid $11B to the music industry in 2025**, reported Jan 2026 (half to indies)

### The 1,000-stream threshold (fully enforced in 2026)
- Tracks must hit ≥1,000 streams per 12 months or earn **zero** royalties
- ~87% of catalog tracks fall below this threshold
- Spotify defended the rule in December 2025 against a report showing 85% negative impact on indies
- **Implication:** of LAT's 10 singles, realistically only Silver Sky currently crosses the threshold. The other 9 tracks earn nothing

### AI music compliance (2026 rules)
- **Spotify is NOT banning AI music** -- policy explicitly allows AI-assisted and AI-generated tracks with disclosure (Rolling Stone, Spotify Newsroom Sep 2025)
- **DDEX metadata disclosure is mandatory** at the distributor level -- AI use in vocals, instruments, or post-production must be flagged
- **75M+ tracks deleted** in the 12 months leading into 2026 for "spammy" behavior
- **DistroKid 2026:** mandatory AI disclosure on every upload, automated screening, retroactive enforcement, tiered classification
- **TuneCore 2026:** participates in DDEX; will not distribute 100% AI-generated tracks with zero human creative input
- **Symphonic (LAT's distributor):** accepts AI-assisted music with mandatory DDEX disclosure

### What gets you banned (and it is NOT disclosure itself)
- Stuffed titles like "Lofi Beats Study Music Chill Relax Focus"
- Mass uploads, near-duplicates, SEO metadata abuse
- Artificially short tracks (<30s)
- Undisclosed AI that gets detected via retroactive scans

### Asian market tailwinds
- **South Korea Spotify MAU: ~3.6M** (vs Melon 6.5M) -- Spotify closing the gap fastest in Asia
- **APAC = ~45% of global TikTok users · SE Asia = ~25% of global ad audience**
- **Thailand:** highest TikTok penetration in Asia (matches LAT's Bangkok data)
- **Lofi genre still growing** with Gen Z + work-from-home adoption driving steady demand

### YouTube lofi economics
- Lofi Girl earns an estimated $3.5M/year (~$9,600/day) from its 24/7 stream
- Typical lofi YouTube Music RPM: $5.50-$6.00 per 1,000 views
- **24/7 livestream is the single most profitable format in the genre**

## 5. Does Disclosing AI Hurt the Algorithm?

**Short answer: no, disclosure itself doesn't hurt. What hurts is the spam-like behaviors the filter targets.**

### Mechanics
- Disclosure is a **metadata flag** via DDEX from distributor to DSP -- it lives in machine-readable credits
- Spotify surfaces it in the in-app credits panel (since Sep 2025) but it's not visible on cover art or playlist tiles
- It does **not** feed into algorithmic ranking as a negative weight
- Curators typically don't see a disclosure flag at pitch time

### What actually hurts engagement
1. Spam signatures (stuffed titles, mass uploads, duplicates, sub-30s tracks)
2. Undisclosed AI that gets detected retroactively -- worst case: account termination + royalty clawback
3. Low engagement velocity in the first 14 days (unrelated to AI)

### Risk-adjusted verdict
- **Worst case from disclosing:** no measurable change
- **Worst case from hiding:** account terminated, royalties clawed back, 12 months of work gone
- **Recommendation: disclose everything cleanly via Symphonic DDEX**

### Caveat
There is no public A/B study comparing disclosed vs undisclosed AI tracks on Spotify's algorithm. Confidence is based on stated policy, how DDEX metadata flows through ranking, and the absence of any reported case of a disclosed AI track being algorithmically penalized. Read: "very unlikely to hurt", not "impossible".

1. **Reverse-engineer Silver Sky.** Document tempo, key, mood, length, mix (specifically bass roll-off and drum softness), cover art style. Every future release should match this sonic fingerprint -- not copies, but clear siblings.
2. **Silver Sky EP extension.** Release "Silver Sky (Rain Mix)", "(Cafe Mix)", and "(Korean Version)" as a mini-EP. Rides existing algorithmic momentum and creates 2-3 extra discovery points for returning listeners.
3. **Launch YouTube channel.** Upload Silver Sky as an aesthetic visualizer first. This is the single biggest unforced error to fix.
4. **Defend the 32 playlists.** Monitor weekly, pitch replacement tracks to curators who drop him, DM-pitch the curators directly via Instagram since this cohort is already warm.
5. **Disclose AI on every release via Symphonic DDEX.** Non-negotiable.

### Months 2-3: Catalog + discovery

- 1 release every 7-10 days minimum, all Silver Sky siblings
- Daily TikTok posts using aesthetic loops + Silver Sky audio overlay

- Begin Scene Intelligence adaptation -- ingest the 32 playlist curators as a new "lofi_curators" category

## 9. The Missing Piece -- Video Automation

Networking, outreach, and upload pipelines are largely covered. **Video generation and rendering is the remaining bottleneck** -- and for a lofi artist chasing Asian cafe playlists + YouTube revenue, it is arguably the most important automation to build.

### Per-release video artifacts needed

| Artifact | Length | Dimensions | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube visualizer | full track (2-4 min) | 1920x1080 | Per-track video upload |
| YouTube Shorts | 15-60s | 1080x1920 | Discovery |
| TikTok / Reels loop | 15-30s | 1080x1920 | Daily posting |
| Spotify Canvas | 3-8s loop | 1080x1920 | Mobile Spotify in-app |
| Instagram post | static or 15s | 1080x1080 | Feed |
| 24/7 livestream segment | 1-2 hr | 1920x1080 | OBS concatenation |
| Bandcamp preview | 30s | 1280x720 | Track preview |

**7 artifacts × 1 release/week = 28 renders/month manual work.** This is exactly the kind of thing to fully automate.

### Proposed tool: `lofi_video_engine.py`

A new module alongside video_resizer.py that handles the generation side. Four layers.

**Layer 1: Visual source library**
- Local `video_sources/lofi/` folder with 20-50 pre-rendered aesthetic loops (3-10s each)
- Tagged by mood: `rain_window`, `seoul_night`, `cafe_warm`, `train_window`, `anime_room`, `sunset_city`, etc.
- Rendered at both 1920x1080 and 1080x1920
- Sources: Pexels/Pixabay (free commercial), Kaiber/Kling/Runway (optional paid generation), or custom
- **Reusing loops across releases is a feature, not a bug** -- it builds visual identity (see Lofi Girl)

**Layer 2: Audio analysis / hook detection**
- `audio_analysis.py` using librosa (pure Python, numpy-based)
- Loudness envelope + spectral novelty detection
- Return: `intro_end`, `hook_start`, `hook_end`, `outro_start`
- Cache in release JSON so analysis runs once per track

**Layer 3: Composition engine (pure ffmpeg)**
```
render_full_visualizer(track, visual_loop, title, subtitle_kr) → 1920x1080 full track MP4
render_tiktok_loop(track, visual_loop, title)                  → 1080x1920 30s hook clip
render_spotify_canvas(track, visual_loop)                       → 1080x1920 7s loop
render_shorts(track, visual_loop, title, subtitle)              → 1080x1920 60s hook
render_livestream_segment(tracks[], crossfade=4s)               → 1920x1080 2hr concat
render_instagram_post(track, visual_loop, title)                → 1080x1080 15s
render_bandcamp_preview(track, visual_loop)                     → 1280x720 30s
```
- Each function = deterministic ffmpeg pipeline
- CJK font support for Korean/Japanese subtitle overlays
- All local, no external APIs, uses existing imageio-ffmpeg binary

**Layer 4: CLI + RM integration**
```
python -m release_manager.video_engine silver-sky --all
```
- Reads release JSON, runs hook detection, renders all 7 artifacts into `resized_video/silver-sky/`
- New "Generate Videos" button on RM release detail page
- Progress bar, previews, re-render controls

### Impact

- **Manual time per release before tool:** 2-3 hours
- **Manual time per release with tool:** ~5 minutes (pick visual tag, hit render, verify)
- **Annual savings at 1 release/week:** ~100-150 hours

### Build plan

**Week 1:** Visual source library + librosa hook detection + basic ffmpeg composition. Goal: render Silver Sky's full YouTube visualizer + TikTok loop + Spotify Canvas end-to-end.

**Week 2:** All 7 artifact variants + RM detail-page integration + batch-process existing catalog (retroactively generate videos for all 10 tracks).

**Week 3 (optional):** 24/7 YouTube livestream builder + OBS setup documentation.

**Total effort:** ~5-6 days of focused work for layers 1-4. Livestream builder adds ~1 day.

### Optional upgrade: AI-generated source loops

Hold off initially. If needed later, generate a small batch of custom loops quarterly via Kling ($7/mo) or Kaiber ($29/mo) and drop them into the local library. Still no runtime AI dependency.

## 11. Flags, Caveats, and Unknowns

- **Specific AI-assisted music acceptance policies** at Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed could not be verified from public sources. Query each platform directly before submitting.
- **No public A/B study** on disclosed vs undisclosed AI track algorithmic performance exists. The "disclosure is safe" verdict is inferred from stated Spotify policy, DDEX metadata flow, and absence of reported penalties.
- **Korean cafe playlist curator contact norms** are inferred from general Spotify user-playlist behavior. The "DM curators directly" recommendation has not been verified against a sample of successful pitches.
- **Silver Sky's 32-playlist position is temporarily advantaged** because Spotify's spam filter is demoting competing AI lofi tracks. This is a window, not a permanent moat.
- **YouTube lofi livestream RPM estimates** vary widely ($500-$9,600/day range in published cases). Actual LAT revenue will depend heavily on execution quality and luck.
- **Lofi Girl's $3.5M/year figure** is from 2025 estimates, not verified 2026 data.
- **Spotify Web API Extended Access** is gated at 250K MAU in 2026 -- LAT is far below this, so all Spotify data must come from Spotify for Artists exports, not API.
