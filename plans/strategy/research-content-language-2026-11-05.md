# Content language for the songs era: what the scene does on Instagram (2026-11-05)

Read-only research for the next 2 to 3 months of content. Evidence first, opinion marked.

How it was done: 17 public Instagram profiles read logged out in the built-in browser pane (not Amir's Chrome), on 2026-11-05. For each: the header (followers, bio, highlights), the 12 most recent grid items (date, type, own post or collab post from another account), the 12 most recent reels with their public play counts, and the caption, audio label, likes, comments and date of the 3 best and 3 worst reels. 16 cover frames of own reels were looked at (best vs worst). No file was downloaded; the browser tool kept its own small screenshot captures in the harness folder `/Users/amir/.claude/projects/-Users-amir-Desktop-Inch-of-Shadow/6041907b-315a-44f5-9db9-2e33db577f5c/tool-results/`, nothing in the project, nothing in Downloads, nothing to move.

Limits, stated once:
- Logged out, Instagram shows 12 grid items and 12 reels per profile, not 30. "Posting rhythm" below is computed from those 12 grid items and marked **(est.)**.
- Play counts are the public grid numbers, captured on one day. Older reels had longer to accumulate. The ratio **views / followers** is used to compare across sizes; it is a rough measure, not reach.
- Reels could not be watched (no login, no download). Hook, cut and text-on-screen findings come from captions, audio labels and the cover frame only.
- Stories and broadcast channels are invisible logged out. Only highlight names were seen.

Tags: **[SEEN]** on the real profile page today. **[OUR DATA]** our own insights (`plans/ig-content-performance-log.md`). **[REPORTED]** press or interview. **[CLAIMED]** blog or agency, no method. **[JUDGEMENT]** Claude's reading of the evidence, not evidence. No Wikipedia was used.

All handles below were verified on the real profile page [SEEN]. Three first guesses were wrong and are corrected here: Twin Tribes is `@twin_tribes` (not `@twintribes`), She Past Away is `@shepastaway_official`, Mareux is `@__mareux__`.

## 2. Patterns across the 17 accounts

**Breakout factor = best own reel / followers.** Boy Harsher 2.9x (release day with the music-video clip). Mareux 2.8x (a 4-word caption, the track as audio). She Past Away 1.4x (official video). Dina Summer 6.2x (the final tease before the album, three faces into the lens). She Can't Afford Mascara 5x (an unreleased track with a videographer's clip). Night in Athens 1.2x (close face, violet light). R. Missing 1.26x in 2024, 0.4 to 0.65x in 2025. Curses 0.46x. Twin Tribes 0.25x own (1.2x via a festival collab). Molchat Doma 0.8x. Drab Majesty 1.0x. Infant Sanchos 1.2x. Phantom Head 1.06x. Ekkstacy 19x (a joke clip; a persona built over years, not a format). Inch of Shadow 0.77x [OUR DATA].

1. **The reel that carries the song wins; the reel that carries a date loses.** [SEEN] In 9 of the 12 accounts that post their own reels, the top own reel is the song itself with a picture: a music-video clip, a teaser with the track, a performance. The bottom own reels are announcements made of words: "out on Friday" (Boy Harsher 110K vs 831K three days later), "New track coming out on the 2/10" with a date card (Night in Athens 5.4K vs 20.9K), show announcements (Twin Tribes 10K to 13.7K vs 34.6K to 42.6K), "tour announcement" (Dina Summer 5.9K vs 46.8K), "show tomorrow" (Mascara 3.8K vs 22.4K), "out now everywhere" (Ekkstacy's lowest, 41K vs 2.4M). The only exception is Lebanon Hanover, whose best own reel is merch being printed (34.2K, process).

2. **The cover frame of the top own reels is a face close to the lens; the bottom ones are wide shots with small people.** [SEEN, 16 cover frames] Top: She Past Away 152K (B&W close face and a hand), Dina Summer 46.8K (three faces staring, flash on a wall), Night in Athens 20.9K (close face, violet light), Curses 21.7K (sunglasses, red neon, pointing at the camera), Boy Harsher 831K (two people, warm room, faces readable). Bottom: Boy Harsher 63.8K (stage from the crowd, green light, performer tiny), Curses 5.1K (wide stage, purple wash), Dina Summer 1,856 (pedal boat at sea, people small), Night in Athens 5.4K (gear plus a date card). Counter-example: Mascara's 22.4K cover is a dark corridor with a small figure, but it carried an unreleased track and a videographer's clip. [JUDGEMENT] Face and strong single subject in frame one is the scene's default top performer; it is the same finding as our "slow, distant walk-in opener lost people" [OUR DATA].

3. **Collabs from bigger accounts are the biggest number on most grids.** [SEEN] The top reel on 8 of 17 grids was posted by someone else: BBC 6 Music for The Cure (1.4M), a fan page for Molchat Doma (766K), a festival for Twin Tribes (201K), a gear page for Drab Majesty (115K), Âme for Curses (75.7K), a remix compilation for Skelesys (88.3K), Grauzone festival for Night in Athens (57.6K), a venue for Lebanon Hanover (38.3K). Skelesys posts no reels at all; all 12 reels on his tab are other people's. Lebanon Hanover posted 1 of the last 12 grid items. The sources are venues, promoters, festivals, labels, fan video pages (darkkmusicvideos, gothgigs, postpunk_80) and peers (Night in Athens x Skelesys "WITHR" 9.2K, Curses x Skelesys as Silver Tears). This matches Mosseri's rule that the account which starts the collab sets the reach (`mosseri-guide.md`) and our social-algo research point 8.

4. **Captions in the scene are short, lowercase, and often a lyric line.** [SEEN] Mareux: "Will you blame it on your blood?" (139K), "Ebene Fume by Riki & Mareux" (385K). Curses: "No remorse/control", "Look into his somber eyes / The hunger, the pain". R. Missing pastes the whole lyric under every release reel, plus "Link in bio". Ekkstacy: 2 to 5 words. Boy Harsher is the exception: long, warm, funny, with a full cast and crew list, and the longest caption is on the biggest reel (831K). Twin Tribes and Dina Summer write formal band announcements ("Thrilled to reveal...", "Step straight into the 80s!") and their own reels sit at 0.25x and 1x to 6x respectively; the formal tone did not stop Dina's 6.2x, the picture did the work. Nobody in the sample uses "POV:", "wait for it", listicles or questions as hooks. Fan questions appear only on tiny accounts (Phantom Head "suggest songs!!").

## 3. What works at our size (under 5k) and what only works at 100k

**At our size the default is 0.5x to 1.2x.** [SEEN] Phantom Head 0.45x to 1.06x, Infant Sanchos 0.77x to 1.2x, Mascara's show reels 0.6x to 0.8x, Night in Athens' own reels 0.3x to 1.2x, R. Missing's 2025 reels 0.4x to 0.65x. [OUR DATA] Inch of Shadow 0.58x to 0.77x. We are not behind the scene; we are exactly at the scene's floor.

**Three things pushed a small account above 1x in this sample:**
1. A track teaser with a strong clip, posted before release: Mascara 5x at 4.5K (a videographer's clip, an unreleased track named in the caption), Dina Summer 6.2x at 7.5K (three faces into the lens, flash light, "final tease" 30 days ahead).
2. A collab started by a bigger account: Night in Athens 3.2x via Grauzone, 1.4x via a curation page; Skelesys 8x via a remix compilation; Dina 2.9x via a fan video page reposting her own video as a collab.
3. A close face or a strong single subject in frame one, with the song under it: Night in Athens 1.2x, Curses' best own, She Past Away's best own.

**What only works with 100k+ and should not be copied:** tour thank-you montages of crowds (Molchat Doma 394K, Mareux 242K: they need crowds), merch reels (Lebanon Hanover, She Past Away's merch studio), fan-page history reels (postpunk_80 766K: they need a history), music videos with a cast, crew and a post house (Boy Harsher), the joke persona (Ekkstacy 2.4M, built over years, and his release post was still his lowest). Twin Tribes' 4 to 5 show-promo posts a week is the most posting in the sample and the lowest own ratio for its size: volume of announcements does not help.

**What small accounts in the sample do not do and could:** put the track in the library and use it as named audio (Phantom Head, Infant Sanchos, Mascara all run on Original audio, so no sound page); ask the venue, promoter or photographer to start the collab instead of being tagged in a photo (Lebanon Hanover, She Past Away and Skelesys get most of their reel reach exactly this way, at every size).

## 4. Contrast with our kit

**Where the kit matches the evidence.**
- `reel-craft.md` [CARRIES] "process beat announcement": matches 6 accounts (Lebanon Hanover, Night in Athens, Phantom Head, Curses' camcorder reel, and the announcement floor everywhere).
- `reel-craft.md` [CARRIES] "a slow, distant walk-in opener lost people" and [VERIFIED] "frame one has to arrest: contrast, movement, eye contact": the cover-frame split in pattern 2 says the same (close faces top, wide stages bottom).
- `reel-craft.md` "every Reel carries an audio track": the scene's top own reels run on the song as named audio; the small accounts on Original audio have no sound page.
- `formats.md` object 8 "face or performance reel, no talking": supported by She Past Away, Dina, Night in Athens, Curses. Nobody in the sample talks to camera about meaning.
- `formats.md` "reels find new people, carousels feed the base": the big bands run carousels for live photos and photo dumps, reels for the song.
- Copy rules (no clickbait, no "POV:", no listicles): the whole scene writes this way; the brand is not asking for something unusual.
- `mosseri-guide.md` collab rule (the bigger account starts): the strongest single pattern in the data (pattern 3).
- `research-social-algo-2026-11-05.md` point 8 (one peer-size collab per song): confirmed, and the evidence widens the partner list to venues, promoters, labels and fan video pages, which are the ones actually starting the collabs in the scene.

**Where the kit contradicts the evidence, or needs a change.**
- `formats.md` object 4 and 6: the release reel "winner on release day" plus a 3-frame story set. Evidence: the release-day post wins only when it carries the song with a picture (Boy Harsher 831K with the video clip); a card or a text post on release day is the floor (Boy Harsher's own "out on Friday" 110K, Night in Athens' date card, Ekkstacy's "out now"). The winner reel must be the song with his face or hands, never a card with a date. The story set can carry the date; the reel cannot.
- `formats.md` object 11 "pre-save image at T-14": in this sample the pre-release piece that worked is a video teaser with the track, 30 days ahead (Dina 6.2x) or months ahead (Mascara 5x). A still pre-save image has no evidence either way; a video teaser does. The object should be a teaser reel with the song, and the still image a story.
- `reel-craft.md` [ERA] "his face works with the base; the best reel so far had no face": the scene's evidence leans face. It stays [ERA] until our own song-era reels measure it, but the test should be run first, not last.
- `rapville/INCH-OF-SHADOW-PLAYBOOK.md` "2 to 4 posts a week": the small accounts that posted most (Phantom Head 2.4/week, Twin Tribes 4 to 5/week) did not beat 1x. Rhythm is a container, not the lever; the lever is what the reel carries and who starts the collab. Keep the rhythm, but measure the ratio per piece, not the count.
- Lyric reel (object 9, typographic): nobody in the sample posts typographic lyric reels. The lyric lives in the caption (R. Missing, Mareux, Curses). The kit is silent on lyric-as-caption. It costs nothing and fits "the lyrics are his edge"; it should be tested before the typographic reel.

**Where the kit is silent and the evidence says something.**
- Named audio on carousels and photos: not in the kit (the social-algo research proposes it as change 6). The scene's big accounts do not use it either; no evidence either way.
- Collab sources: the kit names peers; the evidence names venues, promoters, photographers, labels and fan video pages. The kit should list Berlin venues he has played (About Blank, Beate Uwe, Sameheads, Dark Matter) as collab starters for his own clips, not as engagement targets.
- The "post your working" philosophy (memory): the scene's process posts are hands and gear with the song under them, 1x. It is a base format, not the discovery format. The kit should say so, so process is not expected to find strangers on its own.
- Sunglasses as signature: one data point for it (Curses' best own reel is sunglasses into the lens). Not a rule.
