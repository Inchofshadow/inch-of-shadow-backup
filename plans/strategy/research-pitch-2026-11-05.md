## 1. Spotify editorial self-pitch, late 2026

Rules [OFFICIAL, support page "Pitching music and videos to playlist editors"]:
- Pitch from Music > Upcoming. Deliver at least 7 days before release. No other lead time is given.
- One song at a time. Next pitch only after the pitched song is live. Released songs cannot be pitched. Featured-artist songs and compilations cannot be pitched.
- Admin or Editor can edit the pitch until release day, but "editors aren't guaranteed to see the changes".
- Editors may pick a different song from the release.
- Field list is not published by Spotify. Practitioner view of the 2026 form: 500-character pitch, one genre, up to 2 styles, 2 moods, instruments, location/culture. [CLAIMED, Jesse Cannon, 2026-02-17, https://musicmarketingtrends.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-write-a-spotify-editorial-playlist-pitch-in-2026-part-2-of-2] Our June note says "up to 3 moods, 3 styles" (`platform-algorithm-research-2026-06.md:488`); the form changes, read it live.
- Cannon also says Spotify is expanding location-based playlists, so the location field (Berlin) matters. [CLAIMED]

What changed 2025-2026:
- **Video pitching** (rolled out gradually; MBW 2026-07-21). Official music videos or performances, unreleased or released within 7 days; delivered by distributor or uploaded in S4A (upload is beta). Separate pitch from the song. [OFFICIAL + REPORTED] Whether Symphonic Starter delivers video to Spotify: unknown (gap, ask Symphonic).
- **Clips ended** 2026-06-17 for new uploads; Clips tab becomes a Video tab. [REPORTED, MBW]
- **Spotify 2026 plan** (S4A blog, 2026-01-27): new editorial "programs" for emerging artists with "more sustained support", pitching tools being "sharpened", SongDNA (credits exploration), stronger verification against impersonation. No details or thresholds published. [OFFICIAL] https://artists.spotify.com/blog/what-were-building-for-artists-in-2026
- **Artist Profile Protection** (beta from 2026-03-24, limited group): approve or decline releases before they appear on your profile; an "artist key" auto-approves trusted deliveries. Risk: slow approval delays releases. [OFFICIAL + REPORTED] https://support.spotify.com/ad/artists/article/artist-profile-protection ; https://musically.com/2026/03/24/spotify-cracks-down-on-fake-releases-with-artist-profile-protection-feature/
- **Release Radar listener filters** (2026-07-10): up to 5 filters on the playlist; ~9 million users listen every Friday. Spotify says nothing about effect on artists. [OFFICIAL] https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-07-10/discovery-playlists-release-radar-control-updates/
- **Wrapped 2026 artist tools**: a video message under 30 s via S4A > Video & Visuals, may show in fans' Wrapped; open to all artists. Deadline reported as 6 Nov 2026 (Hypebot 2026-10-14 and 2026-11-02); a Spotify creators page says 13 Nov but it is about podcasts. Time-sensitive: check in S4A today. [REPORTED] https://www.hypebot.com/spotify-for-artists-opens-wrapped-2026-portal-with-new-tools/ ; https://www.hypebot.com/5-days-left-for-artists-to-update-for-spotify-wrapped-2026/
- **Countdown Pages**: 3,000 monthly active listeners, albums/EPs only. [OFFICIAL] (Newsroom said 5,000 in 2025, support page says 3,000.)
- **Discovery Mode**: 30% commission on Discovery Mode streams; 25,000 listeners per blogs. [CLAIMED] Out of reach.
- **Marquee/Showcase**: out of reach (see table row 6).
- **AI DJ / prompted playlists**: no artist-side lever found. Spotify only says DJ picks for the listener and learns from feedback. [OFFICIAL, newsroom, general] Any weighting numbers (Chartlex "38% higher save rate") are unsourced. [CLAIMED] https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/spotify-ai-dj-how-it-works-artists-2026
- **Artificial streaming**: no royalties, not counted, does not boost algorithms; per-track charge to distributors on flagrant cases (since 2024-04-01); paid placement prohibited; paid "consideration" = "Be highly skeptical". [OFFICIAL] https://artists.spotify.com/artificial-streaming . FUGA and blogs say the charge is EUR 10 per track and distributors pass it on. ## 2. Release strategy for an unknown songs act

- No outcome study on cadence found. All 2026 guides say a single every 4-6 or 4-8 weeks, waterfall into an EP. [CLAIMED] https://www.collabhouse.com/blog/how-to-release-music-2026-strategy-guide ; https://orphiq.com/resources/how-often-release-music
- What is official and drives the same answer: one Release Radar song per artist per week; one pitch at a time; pitch only unreleased songs; 1,000 streams per track per year for royalties. Together: separate releases give more pitches and more Release Radar slots, and a waterfall (re-using the earlier single's ISRC) keeps streams on one track instead of splitting them. [OFFICIAL mechanics; the strategy itself is derived]
- Spacing: because of the one-pitch rule, release N+1 can only be pitched after release N is live. With 7 days minimum lead, 4 weeks apart is the practical minimum that still lets each pitch sit 2-3 weeks. [derived]
- Pre-save value 2026: no evidence it is a ranking input; Countdown Pages closed to us. Value = a follow ask + day-one saves from real fans. [CLAIMED / derived] See today's tool table in `research-release-fans-2026-11-05.md`.
- Release day: Friday is still the global day and the most crowded. A 2025 SSRN study (via Dynamoi) only shows that mid-tier artists release on Thursdays more often; no proof Thursday performs better. [CLAIMED] https://dynamoi.com/learn/spotify-algorithm/the-best-day-to-release-music-on-spotify-data-study . Release Radar and New Music Friday refresh on Friday. [OFFICIAL] Keep Friday.

## Recommended strategy changes

### 1. Pitch every release, everywhere we are allowed, the day it is delivered
- **Was:** Spotify only, "28 days ideal", "pitching every release can hurt" (`release-guide:204`, `:233`); Apple "via Symphonic" (`platform-research:220`); no Amazon.
- **Change to:** For every release: Spotify pitch the day the track appears in S4A Upcoming; Amazon Music for Artists pitch (claim the profile first); no Apple/Deezer step (closed on Starter). Video pitch when an eligible video exists.
- **Based on:** Spotify pitching + Release Radar pages [OFFICIAL]; Amazon promotion page [OFFICIAL]; Apple Music Pitch page [OFFICIAL].
- **Why:** Free, and the only editorial doors open on Starter. The "hurts" claim has no source.
- **Steps:** Claim Amazon Music for Artists (Owner). Add "Amazon pitch" and "Spotify pitch same day as delivery" to the release checklist. Delete the Apple pitch step.
- **Lane:** both.

### 2. Build releases around the one-pitch slot and the 1,000-stream floor
- **Was:** Half the Night as one 2-track drop; cadence "4-6 weeks" with no pitch-slot rule.
- **Change to:** Release Half the Night as a single first, then the 2-track (Half the Night + Pinocchio) about 4 weeks later with Pinocchio pitched (waterfall, same ISRC for Half the Night). Songs era: single, then the next single 4-8 weeks later, each pitched; never schedule release N+1's pitch before N is live.
- **Based on:** one song at a time, unreleased only, one Release Radar song per artist per week [OFFICIAL]; 1,000 streams per track per 12 months [OFFICIAL]; waterfall practice [CLAIMED].
- **Why:** Two pitches and two Release Radar slots instead of one, and streams stay on one ISRC so a track has a chance to pass 1,000 a year. This firms up today's hypothesis 2 in `review-2026-11-05-research.md` with official rules.
- **Steps:** Decide split with Amir; recompute dates (each release delivered at least 7 days before, pitch sits 2-3 weeks); put the pitch-slot rule in the release guide.
- **Lane:** both (electronic now, songs next).

### 3. Drop threshold tools from planning; spend effort on followers and curators
- **Was:** Countdown Pages, Marquee, Discovery Mode, Apple editorial in plans (`release-guide:257-273`, `platform-research:170-177`).
- **Change to:** Mark them "locked until thresholds" (3,000 MAL Countdown + EP; 1,000 streams/28 days Showcase; 5,000 MAL Marquee; ~25k Discovery Mode). Use Artist Pick, Canvas, third-party pre-save with a follow ask, and warm curators.
- **Based on:** Countdown support page [OFFICIAL]; campaign tools page via today's research [OFFICIAL]; Discovery Mode [CLAIMED].
- **Why:** At ~94 listeners none of these can be bought or used; keeping them in the plan wastes prep.
- **Steps:** One edit pass on the release guide (Amir approves first). Re-check thresholds in S4A each release.
- **Lane:** both.
