**Pattern [JUDGEMENT]:**
1. For small acts, one finished official video per single is still the asset that carries; teasers, unboxings, "out now" and ticket posts sit at the bottom on every channel checked.
2. Two formats beat the act's own baseline by 10x or more: a **cover of an 80s song** (Sinnerella; also Twin Tribes "Lobo Hombre En Paris" cover 328K) and a **real place filmed live** (Molchat Doma hotel; Ultra Sunn studio sessions).
3. Shorts win when they show **the person or the raw work** (first mix, a personal answer about influences), not when they announce.
4. Lyric videos carry when the song itself is already the meme (Mareux). For others the official video wins (Boy Harsher, Pixel Grip).

## 10 language rules the evidence supports

1. **Show the human making it: face, hands, room, gear.** Fans now suspect any new upload is AI and say they want imperfect human work. Source: Goth Dad AI video comments 2026 [FORUM]; NNHMN 2026-06-11 [REPORTED]; Deezer via NPR 2026-10-27 [REPORTED].
2. **Post the raw version, framed as raw ("first mix", "first take").** Ultra Sunn's first-mix Short did 30.5K vs 1K to 3K for their polished Shorts. Source: YouTube @ultrasunn 2026-07-17 [SEEN]; ThxSoMch's first viral post was a session screen recording [REPORTED] Chartmetric.
3. **Talk about your taste in your own words (films, books, records), short.** Twin Tribes' answer on film influences is their top Short at 126K. Source: YouTube @twintribes 2025-08-28 [SEEN]; R. Missing on books, Electricity Club 2025-01-27 [REPORTED].
4. **Daylight and warmth are allowed; do not perform gloom.** Source: Boy Harsher on brightness, Metal Magazine 2026 [REPORTED]; Molchat Doma on LA sun and literal readings, Bend Bulletin 2026-08-25 [REPORTED]; friendlier flyers grew the nights, Substack 2026-03-30 [FORUM]. Matches memory "brand tone not only dark".
5. **Light humour about yourself, full sincerity about the songs.** Source: Hot Hail!, Post-Punk.com 2026-05-02 [REPORTED]; Goth Dad juxtaposition, WDET [REPORTED]; Gannon on honest songs, Asheville Stages [REPORTED].
6. **Use one real place as a set and film the song there live.** Molchat Doma's hotel set (2.9M) beat their official videos; Ultra Sunn's studio sessions reached 627K. Source: YouTube [SEEN]; R. Missing's self-shot underpass video, Electricity Club 2025 [REPORTED]. Fits memory "Studio as content set" and Berlin locations.
7. **One finished official video per single beats many teasers.** Source: Then Comes Silence, R. Missing, Minuit Machine, Sydney Valette channels: teasers and "out now" at the bottom, official videos at the top [SEEN].
8. **Keep a cover of an 80s song as a door opener, recorded your own way.** Sinnerella's cover: 77K vs ~1K for originals; Twin Tribes cover 328K; Mareux's Cure cover. Source: YouTube [SEEN]; Knotfest 2026-10-21 [REPORTED]; Chartmetric [REPORTED].
9. **Name the lineage, but sound and look like now.** Press punishes tribute acts; fans praise "old feel, new song". Source: Treble 2026-07-15 [REPORTED]; Lebanon Hanover, Spotlight Report 2026-09-15 [REPORTED]; Twin Tribes comments [FORUM].
10. **Make the song easy for others to use, and let them carry it.** Every scene breakout came from other people's edits, covers or outfit clips, often years later (Bragolin after 7 years, Sinnerella after 1). Source: Post-Punk.com 2026-08-14 [REPORTED]; Knotfest 2026 [REPORTED]; 11-05 TikTok file section 3.

## 5 things the evidence says to avoid

1. **AI images, AI covers, AI-looking video.** Fans and writers drop an act on sight of AI art. Source: Goth Dad comments 2026 [FORUM]; NNHMN 2026 [REPORTED]. Matches memory "no AI on his face".
2. **Bought or faked engagement, follow schemes presented as fans.** Source: NPR 2026-10-27 [REPORTED]; Boy Harsher, CREEM 2025 [REPORTED]; Casket Cassette 2026 [REPORTED].
3. **Tribute-act look and sound (one-influence worship, Cure cosplay).** Source: Treble 2026-07-15 [REPORTED]; Lebanon Hanover 2026 [REPORTED].
4. **Literal dark clichés (gas masks, skulls, menace for its own sake) and fashion without music.** Source: Substack 2026-03-30 [FORUM]; Globe and Mail 2025-10-25 [REPORTED]; Molchat Doma 2026 [REPORTED].
5. **A feed of announcements: "out now", "out Friday", tickets, unboxing.** Lowest numbers on every channel checked. Source: Section 2 channel data [SEEN].

## Gaps and limits
- Reddit (r/darkwave, r/postpunk, r/gothmusic) and RYM: blocked for the fetch tools today, so no Reddit or RYM claim is made. Fan voice comes from YouTube comments and one Substack.
- TikTok per-video and per-sound data is not readable as text any more; only profile follower counts were read.
- Fonts, b/w vs colour and film grain: no primary 2025-2026 evidence on fan response. Do not treat any of these as decided by evidence.
- YouTube numbers are lifetime views on 2026-11-05; ages are YouTube's rounded labels.
- Files: no file was downloaded. Pages were read as text only. The small read scripts written in the session scratchpad were moved to the Trash at the end.
