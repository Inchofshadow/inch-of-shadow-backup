# Inch of Shadow -- Web3 Music Strategy

**Created:** April 10, 2026
**Status:** Research complete, ready for execution

---

## Overview

Web3 music platforms as a supplementary channel alongside traditional DSP distribution (Spotify, Apple Music, Beatport, etc. via Symphonic). Not a replacement -- an additional layer for direct fan support, collectibles, and positioning in the Web3 music space.

**Why now:** Crypto is in a down cycle. Less noise, platforms hungry for quality artists. Artists who are present when the cycle turns get the first wave of attention. Amir has deep Web3/NFT knowledge -- a real advantage most musicians don't have.

**Key principle:** Same tracks can exist on both traditional DSPs and Web3 platforms simultaneously. No conflict. You own the masters, you decide where your music lives.

## Platforms -- Action Plan

### 1. Nina Protocol -- PRIMARY WEB3 PLATFORM
- **What:** "Bandcamp for Web3" -- music sales with permanent on-chain storage
- **Chain:** Solana (payments) + Arweave (permanent file storage)
- **Why it fits:** Featured on Resident Advisor. Strong electronic/experimental community. Underground music focus
- **Fees:** ~$1.20 to publish + ~$0.003/MB for files. $1 community fee per sale. Artists keep 100% of their price
- **Onboarding:** Need Solana wallet + some SOL (~$5 worth). iOS and Android apps available
- **Partnership:** Merlin (major indie distributor) -- September 2025
- **Discovery:** Community-curated collections, editorial, tag/scene following
- **Action:** Sign up, set up artist profile, upload Farewell + future releases
- **URL:** https://www.ninaprotocol.com/

### 2. Catalog -- PRESTIGE WEB3 PLATFORM
- **What:** Originally 1/1 music NFT marketplace, evolved to include digital downloads and fan endorsements
- **Chain:** Ethereum mainnet (1/1 records) + Base L2 (cosigns at 0.001 ETH/~$3)
- **Built on:** Zora Protocol. Files stored on IPFS + Arweave permanently
- **Why it fits:** Richie Hawtin, Boys Noize on platform. "No correlation between follower count and success." 0% marketplace fees on downloads. Email collection built in. Referral system
- **Three products:**
  - Digital downloads -- 0% fee (free to use, like Bandcamp)
  - Cosigns -- lightweight fan endorsements at ~$3 each (Base L2)
  - 1/1 NFT records -- collectible, ~$50 gas on Ethereum mainnet (optional, per release)
- **1/1 exclusivity rule:** If you mint a track as a 1/1 NFT on Catalog, don't mint same track as 1/1 elsewhere. Song itself can still be on all streaming platforms
- **Catalog Radio:** 24/7 curated broadcast with live chat -- passive discovery
- **Liner Notes:** Editorial blog. Can pitch writers. Artists featured based on music quality, not follower count
- **Genre tags:** Electronic (185), techno (98), experimental (119), ambient (107)
- **Onboarding:** May require approval. Apply and see
- **Action:** Apply/sign up, set up profile, upload Farewell as digital download. Consider 1/1 for special releases
- **URL:** https://catalog.works/

### 3. Audius -- FREE STREAMING PRESENCE
- **What:** Decentralized music streaming (Spotify/SoundCloud alternative)
- **Chain:** Solana (content) + Ethereum (governance/$AUDIO token)
- **Why it fits:** Free, zero friction, better audio quality than SoundCloud free (320kbps/48kHz, lossless available). Underground Trending chart for smaller artists
- **No wallet needed:** Sign up with email. Credit card payments accepted
- **Upload:** Unlimited, free. WAV/FLAC/MP3/20+ formats. 200MB max per file
- **Artwork:** 1000x1000px minimum, PNG recommended, square
- **Monetization:** USDC sales (90/10 split), tipping (100% to artist), gated content, Artist Coins (fan tokens)
- **$AUDIO token:** ~$0.02 (nearly worthless). Top 5 trending = ~$20/week. Not a revenue source
- **PRO royalties:** ASCAP/BMI/SESAC agreements in place. Include ISRC/ISWC metadata
- **Symphonic does NOT distribute to Audius** -- upload directly, no conflict
- **Action:** Sign up, upload all releases, set and forget
- **URL:** https://audius.co/

### 4. Coop Records -- WATCH ONLY (Do Not Sign Up Yet)
- **What:** Hybrid fund/label/incubator with "songcoins" model
- **Chain:** Soneium (Sony L2), previously Base
- **Why watch:** $4.5M raised Sept 2025. Working with Ninja Tune (electronic music label). If they open to indie artists, get in early
- **Action:** Monitor for announcements. No sign-up needed yet
- **URL:** https://www.cooprecords.xyz/

## What Successful Web3 Music Artists Do

1. Don't need millions of listeners -- 100-500 dedicated supporters is the model
2. Use Web3 as supplementary, not primary -- Spotify/SoundCloud/Bandcamp for reach, Web3 for superfan monetization
3. Offer genuine exclusivity -- unreleased tracks, stems, alt mixes, artwork
4. Price realistically -- $5-50, not speculative pricing
5. Follower count doesn't predict success -- music quality and connection do

## Macro Context (April 2026)

- Web3 music is in the "trough of disillusionment" -- NFT trading volume fell 19% in 2024
- Major labels retreated from Web3 (Warner, Universal, Beatport, Limewire)
- Survivors are pivoting to "invisible crypto" -- blockchain rails underneath, normal UX on top
- AI + Web3 convergence is the emerging trend
- Down market = opportunity for positioning. Less competition, platforms want quality artists
