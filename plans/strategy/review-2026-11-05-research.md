# Strategy review research, 2026-11-05

Text-only web research. Official = Spotify/Instagram/SoundCloud own pages or executives. "(weak)" = practitioner, vendor or secondhand claim, no published method. Gaps are stated, not filled.

## 1. What grows Spotify FOLLOWERS under 1k

- Followers are the Release Radar input: Spotify's support page says followers get songs from a new release in their Release Radar (updates Fridays); pitching 7+ days ahead lets you choose which song, otherwise Spotify picks. No reach % given. Source: "Getting music on Release Radar", Spotify for Artists support, current page, https://support.spotify.com/br-en/artists/article/getting-music-on-release-radar/
- Practitioner estimate: only about 3-5% of followers actually see a given release in Release Radar in a week; one track per artist per week. (weak, not attributed to Spotify) Source: Sonosuite blog "Spotify's Release Radar: How the Algorithm Works", 2025, https://sonosuite.com/blog/spotify-release-radar
- Countdown Pages and the Upcoming Releases hub need 5,000 active listeners in the last 28 days (we are at about 94). Pages published 7+ days early get nearly 2x the pre-saves; about 1 in 7 (14%) Countdown pre-savers become super listeners by release week. Source: "Pre-save upcoming releases", Spotify Newsroom, 2025-05-21 (updated 2025-09-29), https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-05-21/upcoming-releases-hub-countdown-pages-charts-discovery
- Below that threshold the only pre-save route is third-party links (Feature.fm, Hypeddit, etc.). Feature.fm's "Multi-Follow" can ask the fan to follow 1-3 profiles right after a pre-save, and "Future Save" adds later releases too. No published pre-save-to-follow rate exists; we would have to read our own link Insights. Source: Feature.fm Help Center, "Activate Pre-Save Multi-Follow Actions", https://help.feature.fm/articles/360042839312 (weak on outcomes, vendor docs)
- Marquee/Showcase are paid and offered per release inside S4A; third-party guides put the floor around 1,000 streams per release (some say 5,000 for Marquee). Discovery Mode needs roughly 25,000 monthly listeners or similar, so it is out of reach. (weak, conflicting summaries) Sources: Symphonic blog "Spotify marketing tools: Marquee, Showcase, Discovery Mode", 2026-07-06, https://blog.symphonic.com/2026/07/06/spotify-marketing-tools-marquee-showcase-discovery-mode/ ; Dynamoi "What is Spotify Showcase and Marquee", 2026, https://dynamoi.com/learn/spotify-algorithm/what-is-spotify-showcase-and-marquee
- Spotify research: the most engaged fans are about 2% of monthly listeners but drive nearly 18% of streams and 50% of ticket sales. Source: RouteNote "Spotify for Artists 2025: A recap on all the new features", 2025, https://routenote.com/blog/spotify-for-artists-2025-a-recap-on-all-the-new-features/ (secondhand of Spotify data)
- Gap: no official Spotify source measures what moves a follower count for sub-1k artists (follow CTA on profile, Clips, artist pick). Nothing found with numbers.

## 2. Release cadence before an era change

- The common advice is one single every 4-6 weeks (Dynamoi), 4-8 weeks (Orphiq), 6-8 weeks (Berklee). All are recommendations, none cite outcome data. (weak) Sources: Dynamoi "How often to release music on Spotify", 2025, https://dynamoi.com/learn/spotify-promotion/how-often-to-release-music-on-spotify ; Orphiq "Music release cycles", https://orphiq.com/resources/music-release-cycles-guide
- Release Radar mechanic favours separate drops: one track per artist per week reaches followers, so a 2-track EP released on one day gets one Release Radar slot, while two singles 4+ weeks apart get two. (weak, derived from the practitioner "one track per week" claim plus the official pitch rule above)
- Countdown data shows lead time matters even at the top: 7+ days of pre-release page = about 2x pre-saves (Spotify Newsroom, 2025-05-21, URL above). The same logic applies to our third-party pre-save links (hypothesis).
- Same name vs new page: distributors say a name change creates a new artist page and loses followers and listeners; Apple does not allow renames. Keeping one name keeps the 68 followers and Release Radar reach. Source: Soundrop support "Changing an Established Artist Name", https://support.soundrop.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047638471 ; Amuse support "Changing artist names", https://support.amuse.io/en/articles/107732-changing-artist-names
- No study found on audience loss when a solo artist moves genre under the same name. A vendor claims 9-18 months to recover after a full rebrand. (weak, unsourced) Source: Orphiq "Rebranding as an artist", https://orphiq.com/resources/rebranding-as-musician

## 3. Owned audience (email, Bandcamp, drops)

- Bandcamp says release notification emails to followers are "one of its biggest drivers of sales" and allow a personal message per release. No open rate published. Source: Bandcamp Blog "New messaging features", 2020-05-27, https://blog.bandcamp.com/2020/05/27/new-messaging-features-up (official but old)
- Laylo claims 75% of all its data capture comes from drops tied to live shows (teasers or presales), and says Instagram converts best from followers to a fan list. Vendor data, no rates published. (weak) Sources: Laylo blog "How festivals and events use Laylo", 2025-06, https://laylo.com/blog/how-festivals-and-events-use-laylo-to-dominate-ticket-sales ; ROSTR stack "Laylo CEO's direct-to-fan tech stack 2026", https://stack.rostr.cc/stacks/laylo-ceo-s-direct-to-fan-tech-stack-7-tools-for-artists-to-build-an-owned-audience-2026
- Feature.fm can capture email on a smart link or pre-save without the fan connecting a streaming account. No published capture rate. Source: Feature.fm Help "Collect fan emails on your smart link", https://help.feature.fm/articles/360051494531
- Practitioner numbers: music newsletter open rates quoted at 20-30% up to 28-50% for a clean small list; one unnamed 2025 pre-order case: 3-email welcome sequence, opens 52% to 41%, $3,094 first-week sales. (weak) Sources: Orphiq "How to build an email list as a music artist", https://orphiq.com/resources/email-marketing-musicians ; one-submit "Email CRM best practices", https://www.one-submit.com/spokes/email_crm_best_practices
- Small-list value case: an indie musician raised $40k from a list under 2,000 subscribers. (weak, 2018) Source: Hypebot, https://www.hypebot.com/how-i-raised-40k-through-my-email-list-of-fewer-than-2k-subscribers.md
- Gap: no independent 2025-2026 benchmark for signup conversion on smart links or Bandcamp follow rates.

## 4. Instagram for an artist who posts rarely

- Trial Reels (launched Dec 2024) show a reel only to non-followers first; Instagram reported 40% of users posted more often and an 80% increase in Reels reach among non-followers after launch. Access was opened to public accounts with 1,000+ followers (we are at about 969). Sources: Social Media Today "Instagram expands access to Trial Reels", 2025, https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/ ; Tubefilter, 2025-01-23, https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/01/23/instagram-trial-reels-growth-hacking-creator-economy/ ; The Keyword "Trial Reels to all public creators with 1k+", https://www.thekeyword.co/news/instagram-expands-trial-reels-to-all-public-creators-with-1k-or-more-followers
- Mosseri (Jan 2025): top ranking signals are watch time, likes and sends; sends per reach weigh more for non-follower reach, likes more for followers. Source: Social Media Today "Instagram shares algorithm insights 2025", https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/ (secondhand report of Mosseri video)
- Buffer, 2.1M posts from 102k accounts (Aug 2025): weekly follower growth +0.12% at 1-2 posts/week, +0.26% at 3-5, +0.44% at 6-9; reach per post about 12% higher at 3-5 than at 1-2. Correlation, not cause. Source: Buffer "How often should you post on Instagram in 2025?", 2025-08-13, https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/
- Mosseri on frequency: no ideal number; he would rather see twice a week for two years than daily for two months. Older 2021 guidance was 2 feed posts/week + 2 stories/day. (secondhand) Source: RouteNote "Mosseri answers: how often should you post", 2025, https://routenote.com/blog/?p=102097
- For accounts under 1,000 followers: median reach Reels 134, carousels 56, single images 23; carousels slightly higher likes (7 vs 6). (weak, secondhand of Metricool/HypeAuditor) Source: Fanpage Karma "The Instagram Reels algorithm 2025", https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/ ; NetInfluencer "Carousels outperform Reels for larger accounts", https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-carousels-outperform-reels-for-larger-accounts-content-performance-benchmarks-show/

## 5. Follow/unfollow automation at small scale

- No study found that links follow/unfollow automation to listeners, streams or sales for musicians. Evidence for conversion is absent, not negative.
- General follow-back rates on social networks fall as account "quality" rises: about 40% follow back for medium accounts, about 20% for high-quality ones. Not Instagram or SoundCloud specific. Source: arXiv 2104.00834 "The Production and Consumption of Social Media", 2021, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00834 (academic, old, generic)
- SoundCloud: removes fake accounts and activity when detected; users who buy engagement risk permanent suspension; services selling inflated popularity "go against our Terms of Use". Source: SoundCloud Help "Fake activity on your tracks", https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003448207 ; Terms of Use 09-2025, https://soundcloud.com/terms-of-use/09-2025
- Instagram: enforcement is described as graduated (action block, then restriction, then suspension), limits unpublished and vary by account age and history. Even automation vendors state their tools may violate Instagram's Terms. (weak, no Meta primary found) Source: Apify "Instagram Follow Unfollow Bot" docs, https://apify.com/dead00/instagram-follow-unfollow-bot ; Spur "Instagram automated behaviour", https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/instagram-automated-behaviour
- Indirect: Spotify ran a 2025 masterclass on avoiding artificial streaming because it leads to takedowns, showing platforms police inauthentic activity tied to music metrics. Source: RouteNote 2025 recap, URL in section 1.

## 6. Where darkwave / post-punk revival audiences are found

- TikTok: Molchat Doma's "Sudno" went viral on TikTok in 2020 and was memeified away from its original meaning; this is the template case. Source: IASPM Journal "It wasn't our song anymore", 2024, https://iaspmjournal.net/index.php/IASPM_Journal/article/view/1233
- TikTok also carries goth-infused alt-pop (Mareux's viral Cure cover), which drives catalogue revival of older post-punk. Source: Hypebot (Chartmetric syndication) "Goth-infused alt-pop emerges from BandLab", https://www.hypebot.com/goth-infused-alt-pop-emerges-from-bandlab-as-a-new-genre-on-tiktok.md (qualitative)
- Virality rarely sticks: about 15% of TikTok-viral songs show long-term streaming growth (30%+ Spotify streams 4 months later). (weak, Chartmetric via vendor) Source: Dynamoi "TikTok music discovery statistics 2025", https://dynamoi.com/learn/statistics/tiktok-music-discovery-statistics-2025
- Bandcamp editorial actively covers the niche: New & Notable picks for darkwave/coldwave/post-punk acts in March, May and October 2025 (Oakland, Ohio, Lebanon, Paris). (qualitative, from Bandcamp release pages)
- Live/club: Gen Z is filling goth nights (Miami report), A Murder of Crows grew from a 4-band bill to a multi-day NYC festival, and WGT 2025 coverage notes a resurgence. Sources: Miami New Times, https://www.miaminewtimes.com/?p=40467843 ; Post-Punk.com A Murder of Crows 2025 review, https://post-punk.com/an-annual-gathering-of-goths-in-nyc-a-murder-of-crows-festival-2025-review/ ; Post-Punk.com WGT 2025, https://post-punk.com/bands-and-artists-to-see-at-wave-gotik-treffen-2025/
- Gap: no numbers found on genre playlists or Discord communities for this niche.

## What this means for us (all hypotheses)

1. Hypothesis: our follower lever is the Release Radar loop itself. Every release pitched 7+ days ahead reaches followers, so more separate releases plus a follow ask (Feature.fm Multi-Follow on the pre-save) beat one bigger drop. Countdown Pages, Marquee and Discovery Mode are not available at 94 listeners.
2. Hypothesis: split the 2-track EP into single, then EP (track 2 as focus) 4-6 weeks later, to get two Release Radar slots before the bridge. Keep the same artist name so the 68 followers carry into the sung era.
3. Hypothesis: owned audience grows from real moments (gigs, DJ sets, releases), not from passive signup boxes. A QR/link capture at every set and an email field on every smart link is the cheapest test; measure our own conversion, since no trustworthy benchmark exists.
4. Hypothesis: IG minimum worth doing is about 2 reels a week sustained, judged by sends, with Trial Reels once we pass 1,000 followers (31 to go). Carousels serve followers, reels reach strangers.
5. Hypothesis: the follow/like automation buys platform risk without proven listener conversion. Its value should be judged by our own data (follow-back rate to stream/save), not by follower totals.
6. Hypothesis: the sung era's audience is found on TikTok/Reels (Cure-adjacent hooks), Bandcamp editorial tags, and Berlin goth/post-punk nights, in that order of reach; live nights likely give the best email capture.
