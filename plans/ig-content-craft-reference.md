## Verified — colour & grade (strongest area)

- **Don't fake darkness with "milky blacks."** Crushing shadows then lifting the black
  point (so nothing is ever true black) is the amateur tell — overused because it's two
  clicks. Real cinematic darkness = correctly-placed deep blacks + balanced contrast +
  selective highlights. [Noam Kroll]
- **Grade first, then add grain — lightly.** Around 30% opacity, "felt not seen." Grain
  cannot rescue a flat or digital-feeling image; contrast/tone/colour must already be
  there. Add grain last, as finishing texture. [Frame.io]
- **Match grade to mood.** Dark/dramatic = deeper blacks, stronger contrast, selective
  highlights (deep shadows make viewers lean in). Melancholic/slowmo = cool, desaturated,
  soft contrast. Caveat: the EBM/techno lane usually wants HIGH contrast — do not apply
  soft contrast everywhere. [aaapresets; consensus colour theory]
- **Bleach-bypass over teal-orange for this palette.** Bleach bypass (desaturate ~10-30%,
  add luma contrast, mute reds/yellows, subtle grain) gives the gritty desaturated
  metallic look native to underground. Teal-orange's warm-skin bias suits a cooler/
  desaturated dark palette less well — keep it as a tool, not a default. [aaapresets,
  Boris FX, ASC]

## Verified — openings & retention

- **The opening FRAME must arrest, not the opening talk.** Verbal warm-ups ("hey guys,
  welcome back") kill retention. Key nuance: this targets dead air, NOT atmospheric
  musical builds. A slow cinematic build works IF frame one is already arresting — high
  contrast (black/white with one colour pop), sudden movement (zoom/pan), or direct eye
  contact. Those three triggers are backed by peer-reviewed attention science, not just
  blogs. [Opus.pro + cognitive-psych literature: direct-gaze, motion-onset, contrast
  saliency]
- **Optimise for loops & shares, not just looks.** Instagram's named ranking signals
  (Mosseri, Jan 2025): watch time, completion, re-watch/loops, sends/shares. Build clips
  that loop seamlessly and invite a share or save. Aesthetics and retention are
  complementary, not opposed. Re-verify the signal list each planning cycle — the
  algorithm shifts.

## Refuted — do NOT believe these (commonly repeated, failed verification)

These were killed 0-3 or 1-2 under adversarial verification. Treat as myths:

- "Hook must land in 1.7s / 2s / 3s." (no evidence for a specific threshold)
- "3-second hold rate = 5-10x reach." (unsupported multiplier)
- "Cut every 1.5-3s; static shots >4s are a danger zone." (no support)
- "Scratch-record / rewind audio hook." (no support)
- "Mid-performance drop-in is the top formula / front-load everything." (no support)
- "Lyric-reveal overlays produce the highest save rates." (no support)
- "Showing yourself on camera beats curated/aesthetic content." (no support — the
  curated/cinematic lane is valid; there is no evidence the artist must be on camera)
- "Specific export settings preserve 60% more quality after compression." (no support)
- "Reddit paid EDM creatives: short loops + hype copy at $10-30/day." (no support)
