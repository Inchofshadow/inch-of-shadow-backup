**R3 concept — LOCKED 07-09 (final): "Becoming the marble" — filmed, cemetery.**
One idea threads the reel: Amir MIRRORS the statues (same pose, gaze, framing) until statue and man blur = "beauty was always the first disguise." Shot on phone at the cemetery next to his flat (nearest, quiet, on-aesthetic). Cold grade, cut to 02:13 hook, ~15-18s. Elevated but beginner-doable.

Superseded: animated-stills version (07-09, AI looked amateur on the stylized graphic cover), "marble & streetlight" (07-08), #2 desk performance.

**Signature techniques (the sophistication):**
- **Match cut statue→Amir:** film a statue's upward gaze, then Amir in the SAME gaze/framing; cut between them — the eye completes the motion across the cut.
- **Foreground wipe:** walk so a headstone/tree/pillar passes close to the lens and blacks the frame; cut on the blackout (hidden edit / built-in transition).
- **Rack focus:** statue detail in foreground, Amir blurry behind; tap on Amir mid-shot to pull focus statue→man.
- **Pose mirroring:** Amir echoes a statue's exact pose (sunglasses on); intercut.

**Shot list (cemetery, phone vertical, lock AE/AF by press-hold, hold 10-15s, several takes each):**
1. Statue upward gaze (match-cut A) — medium/close, centered.
2. Statue detail — hands / drapery.
3. Statue full, symmetrical/centered.
4. Amir, SAME upward gaze + framing as #1 (match-cut B).
5. Amir in a statue's pose (pose mirror), sunglasses.
6. Amir walking, foreground wipe (pass a headstone/tree close to lens).
7. Rack focus — statue foreground, Amir behind, tap to pull focus to Amir.
8. (opt.) Neukölln streetlight walk / silhouette at dusk.

**Edit (~15-18s, CapCut, cut to 02:13 hook):** statue gaze (hook) → MATCH CUT to Amir's gaze → walk → foreground wipe → rack focus (statue→Amir) → pose mirror intercut → text "The Deceiver — out now". Cold grade + grain, centered/symmetrical, let shots breathe 2-3s (few cuts = composed), the track DROP lands on the match cut.
**Canvas:** pull one clean 3-6s clip (a centered statue or the rack focus), no text, seamless loop, center kept clear (Spotify overlays edges).
Publish checklist before Share: location (Berlin) · tag ambertone.studios if relevant · rename audio to the track · custom reel COVER = the match-cut or pose frame (NOT the flat album cover — avoid twinning the carousel thumbnail) · first-comment hashtags (tuned set: #ebm #darkelectro #darkwave #berlinunderground #electronicmusic).
