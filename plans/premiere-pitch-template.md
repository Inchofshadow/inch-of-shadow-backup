# Premiere Pitch Template

Reusable template for pitching SoundCloud / YouTube premiere channels. Battle-tested on THE BRVTALIST pitch for "Farewell" (2026-04-23). Tone: polite, cool, careful, gentle -- not salesy.

## Tone rules (why this template works)

- **"Dear [channel] team"** -- polite, respectful, not over-familiar. Use channel name (Brvtalist, Industrial Complexx, etc.) rather than just "Hi" or "Hey".
- **Intro with artist name first, full personal name at sign-off.** "I'm Inch of Shadow" at top, "Amir Miller" at bottom. Keeps it professional but personal.
- **No "offer"** -- the word reads transactional. Use "host a premiere with you" -- collaborative framing, scene-appropriate.
- **No release distributor mention** (Symphonic etc.) -- irrelevant to the curator, makes it feel corporate.
- **No specific premiere window dates** -- let them propose. Feels less demanding.
- **"Let me know how you usually handle these on your side"** -- the polite scene way to ask about fees/process without mentioning money. If they charge, they'll disclose. If free, they'll just describe the workflow.
- **"Thank you for your time and music"** -- acknowledges their work without sycophancy. Warm but not fawning.
- **Cheers** as sign-off -- friendlier than "Best" without being too casual.

## What NOT to include

- Do not name-drop other artists ("you premiered X so you should premiere me"). Curators find this pushy.
- Do not explicitly ask "is there a fee?". Phrase it subtly via process-question.
- Do not mention distributor (Symphonic) or release platforms unless directly relevant.
- Do not attach a long bio -- the EPK does that. Keep email short.
- Do not offer format flexibility (host vs repost) in the first email -- raise it only after they accept.

## Pitch workflow (one premiere at a time)

1. Pitch ONE channel exclusively. Wait 5-7 days.
2. Decision day: if no reply, move to next channel in tier ladder.
3. Log each pitch in the release JSON `sc_yt_premiere_pitch` notes with date sent.
4. When a channel accepts, mark previous pitches as withdrawn (politely, only if you pitched multiple).

## Follow-up etiquette

- Don't follow up before 5 days.
- If silent after 7 days -- move on, don't nag.
- If they publish -- reshare on IG story within 24h, tag them, acknowledge publicly. Builds the relationship for next release.
