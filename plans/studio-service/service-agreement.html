3Price and payment

3.1

The total price is EUR. No VAT is charged (Kleinunternehmer).

3.2

The Client pays 50% of the price before the work starts. The work starts once this payment has arrived.

3.3

The Client pays the remaining 50% before the final files are delivered. The Engineer may send preview files (e.g. watermarked or MP3) before the final payment.

3.4

Payment is by bank transfer to the account on the invoice.

4Files from the Client

4.1

The Client sends the files as WAV, 24-bit or higher, at the session's sample rate, with clear track names. For mastering: one stereo file per track with headroom (no limiter on the master). For mixing and stem mastering: the stems or multitracks, all starting at the same point.

4.2

The Client sends any reference tracks and notes with the files.

4.3

If the files are missing, damaged or not as described, the Engineer tells the Client, and the delivery time starts again once the right files arrive.

5Delivery

5.1

The Engineer delivers within 10 days after receiving the right files and the first payment. Express delivery (48 hours, up to 4 tracks) is only when agreed in the quote.

5.2

Final files: WAV 24-bit, plus a 16-bit / 44.1 kHz version if asked. Delivered by download link.

5.3

The Engineer keeps the project files for 90 days after delivery. After that, they may be deleted.

6Revisions

6.1

Two (2) rounds of revisions per track are included. One round = one list of changes sent in one message.

6.2

A revision is a change to the delivered mix or master. A new version of the song (new files, new arrangement, new vocal) is not a revision and is quoted as new work.

6.3

Further rounds cost EUR per round per track.

6.4

The Client asks for revisions within 14 days after delivery. After that, the work counts as accepted.

7Rights and ownership

7.1

The Client confirms that they own or have cleared all rights in the music, recordings, samples and vocals they send.

7.2

The Client owns the final mixes and masters once the full price is paid. The Engineer claims no copyright, master rights, royalties or publishing share in the music.

7.3

The Client holds the Engineer free from any claim by a third party about the rights in the material the Client sent.

8Credit

8.1

The Client credits the Engineer where credits are shown (streaming credits, liner notes, release info) as: "Mixed by Amir Miller" and / or "Mastered by Amir Miller", as fits the service.

8.2

The Client also credits the Engineer for mixing and / or mastering in their social media posts about the release.

8.3

The Engineer may name the Client and the release in his list of works and may play short parts of it as a reference, only after the release is public. Before the release, the Engineer keeps the music private.

9Cancellation

9.1

If the Client cancels before the work starts, the first payment is returned in full.

9.2

If the Client cancels after the work has started, the first payment is kept for the work done.

9.3

If the Engineer cannot deliver (e.g. illness or a studio problem), he tells the Client right away and offers a new date. If the Client does not want a new date, the Engineer returns all money paid for the work not delivered.

10Liability

10.1

10.2

The Client keeps their own backup of all files sent. The Engineer is not responsible for loss of the Client's files.

10.3

The Engineer's liability is limited to the price paid under this agreement, except for intent and gross negligence.
