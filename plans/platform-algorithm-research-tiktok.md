# TikTok: how it works in 2026 (platform research for Inch of Shadow)

**Re-checked 2026-11-05:** see `plans/strategy/research-tiktok-2026-11-05.md` (6 of 10 summary points still true, point 7 changed: the guideline text since 2026-09-24 says "unoriginal or reused material", no longer the watermark example).

Date: 2026-09-20. Companion to `plans/platform-algorithm-research-2026-06.md`. Read-only research, no account exists yet.

Tags: **[OFFICIAL]** stated by TikTok (or, where marked, by Symphonic or the EU Commission about their own domain). **[REPORTED]** named press or research. **[CLAIMED]** marketing blogs, no primary evidence. **[MYTH]** contradicted by a strong source. Every link was opened on 2026-09-20. Dates are the page's own, where shown.

## Summary: what matters for this artist

1. Watch time is the one signal TikTok itself calls a key driver. Follower count is officially not a direct factor.
2. Open a **Personal** account, never Business. A Business account cannot use his own distributed track.
3. A track live on TikTok through Symphonic comes first. The Artist tag, Music Tab, TikTok for Artists and the pre-save tool all depend on it.
4. Apply for the certified Artist Account once a track is live. Review takes up to 30 days, no follower minimum, so 6 to 8 weeks ahead fits.
5. Post with the **library sound picked in the app**. Audio baked into the render gives no sound page link, no usage count, no royalties.
6. Library music is capped at 60 seconds per video, his own songs included. Set the Clip Start Time in SymphonicMS.
7. Upload clean files. Reused clips showing another platform's watermark are ineligible for the For You feed. No penalty found for the same clean file on Reels and Shorts.
8. TikTok's own material names performance clips, a scenic spot, the story behind a song and text for muted viewers. It calls "all dances and lip-syncs" a myth.
9. TikTok reports 6 billion "Add to Music App" saves in a year. MIDiA finds social discovery often does not become listening. Measure saves, do not assume them.
10. No official page gives test batch sizes, a "first hour" rule, a warm-up or an ideal length. Those numbers are blog folklore.

## 1. How the For You feed ranks videos

Long-standing:

- **[OFFICIAL]** Three factor groups: user interactions (likes, shares, comments, watching in full or skipping), content information (sounds, hashtags, views, country), user information (language, location, device). Interactions, "which may include the time spent watching a video", weigh most. Undated. [TikTok Support: How TikTok recommends content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Finishing a longer video is a strong signal. "Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors." (2020-06-18) [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Same support page: ranking is by predicted interest, unrelated content is mixed in on purpose, and "Not interested" reduces similar content.
- **[REPORTED]** Leaked "TikTok Algo 101" (New York Times, authenticity confirmed by TikTok): the system optimises for retention and time spent, scoring predicted likes, comments, play time and plays. (2021-12-06) [Gizmodo on the NYT report](https://gizmodo.com/leaked-tiktok-doc-reveals-its-obvious-secret-to-an-addi-1848166901)
- **[REPORTED]** Audit, Boeker and Urman (Web Conference 2022): follows shaped a viewer's feed most, then likes, then watch share. [arXiv 2201.12271](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12271)

Recent (2025 to 2026):

- **[OFFICIAL]** "Watch time is a key driver for our content recommendation system." Updated 2026-08-18. [Creator Academy: high-quality videos](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Creating-high-quality-videos-on-TikTok)
- **[REPORTED]** Audit, Baumann et al. (2025): interest-matched content is amplified within the first 200 videos a user watches. [arXiv 2503.20231](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20231)
- **[CLAIMED]** "A new video is tested on a small batch, then bigger ones." Plausible, but no TikTok page opened here describes it. Buffer gives no source; another blog says "50 to 200 accounts", unsourced. [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-algorithm/), [cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/how-to-warm-up-tiktok-account-2026)
- First hours: **NOT FOUND** in any official source.

## 2. New accounts

- TikTok documents the cold start for new viewers only. Anything official on a new creator's first videos: **NOT FOUND**.
- **[OFFICIAL]** Cadence: a steady stream of quality "is always better than rushing to push out daily updates", and "your exact posting cadence is totally up to you". Updated 2026-09-11. [Creator Academy: 8 tips](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/8-tips-for-becoming-a-successful-TikTok-creator)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Repeatable formats, batching, one editing style, scheduling up to 30 days ahead in TikTok Studio web. Updated 2026-08-18. [Creator Academy: posting routine](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/building-a-sustainable-posting-routine)
- **[REPORTED, vendor data]** Buffer, 11.4 million posts: 2 to 5 posts a week gave the clearest lift over one. Median views stayed near 500 at any frequency; more posts mainly raise the odds of an outlier. (2025-10-08) [Buffer study](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
- **[CLAIMED]** "Warm up" the account for days before posting. No TikTok source cited. [cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/how-to-warm-up-tiktok-account-2026)
- **[OFFICIAL]** TikTok "strictly prohibit[s] automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems". Effective 2025-09-13; a guidelines update is announced for 2026-09-24. [Community Guidelines: Integrity and Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)

## 3. Music: sounds, Symphonic, artist tools, streaming conversion

- **[OFFICIAL, Symphonic]** Direct TikTok deal since 2019, renewed 2024 with CapCut. Select TikTok as a delivery partner. The clip sent is 60 seconds from the per-track "Clip Start Time", editable later. (2025-06-24) [Symphonic: TikTok Overview](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042330411-TikTok-Overview). Starter includes TikTok delivery, with a 30% share on UGC revenue. [Symphonic Starter page](https://symphonic.com/get-your-music-on-tiktok/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Symphonic]** Royalties follow views of videos using the delivered sound. A song uploaded as an original sound earns nothing. (2025-04-09) [Symphonic: Royalties](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/4420785552525-TikTok-Streaming-Royalties)
- **[OFFICIAL, Symphonic]** Licensed music is capped at 60 seconds per video, own songs included, or the video is muted. Audio added outside the app avoids the cap, but the video gets no sound page link, no "Use this sound", no usage count. Advice: always pick the track from the Sound Library. (2026-06-16) [Symphonic: FAQs](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/46696058370445-TikTok-Common-Issues-FAQs), [Symphonic: TikTok for Artists](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/39356909849485-TikTok-for-Artists)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Adding a sound in the app replaces all previous background music and voices. [TikTok Support: Sounds](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/sounds)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Artist certification: TikTok Studio, More tools, Artist Hub, Apply, claim the profile, prove ownership with distributor or Spotify for Artists screenshots. Answer within 30 days, then the Music Tab and Artist tag appear. Updated 2026-08-18. [Creator Academy: music tab](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/music-tab-introduction). Symphonic adds: mobile only, no follower minimum, exact name match, Music Tab needs three delivered tracks, plus a "New" release tag and a "By Artist" pinned video per sound. [Symphonic](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/39356909849485-TikTok-for-Artists), [By Artist](https://artists.tiktok.com/help-center/by-artist)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Recent: TikTok for Artists launched 2025-06-03: song and post analytics with completion rate, follower data, a pre-release tool with pre-save to Spotify or Apple Music. Germany is listed (2025-08-26). Needs a certified Artist Account. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/tiktok-for-artists-launches)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Add to Music App: in Germany since 2023-12-18, an "Add Song" button beside the track name in the feed and on the sound page. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/addtomusicapp?lang=en-ZA). Recent: over 6 billion saves from April 2025 to April 2026. (2026-04-24) [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/6-billion-tracks-saved-w-tt-add-to-music-app?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok-commissioned]** Luminate: US TikTok users are 68% more likely to pay for streaming; 84% of 2024 Billboard Global 200 entries went viral on TikTok first. Correlation. (2025-02-13) [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/tiktok-and-luminate-release-latest-music-impact-report)
- **[REPORTED]** MIDiA, "All eyes, no ears" (10,000+ consumers): 52% streamed something after social discovery; 19% of 16 to 24s who found a new artist listened to more; 31% of them had used Add to Music App. [MIDiA, 2025-09-23](https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog/gen-z-social-habits-spell-trouble-for-music-discovery), [Hypebot, 2025-09-17](https://www.hypebot.com/hypebot/2025/09/all-eyes-no-ears-new-midia-data-shows-why-virality-is-not-building-fandom.html)
- A per-view or per-video streaming conversion rate from TikTok or trade press: **NOT FOUND**.

## 4. Format facts

- **[OFFICIAL]** Length: record up to 10 minutes, upload up to 60 minutes. [TikTok Support: camera tools](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/camera-tools). Some sounds are unavailable on long videos, set length to 15s or 60s to use them. [TikTok Support: Sounds](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/sounds)
- Recommended length for music content from TikTok: **NOT FOUND**. **[CLAIMED]** "21 to 34 seconds", traced by blogs to an old WIRED report. [sociality.io](https://sociality.io/blog/tiktok-video-length/). His practical ceiling is the 60 second music cap.
- **[OFFICIAL]** Hook within the first 3 seconds with something "different, original, or attention-grabbing". [Creator Academy: 8 tips](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/8-tips-for-becoming-a-successful-TikTok-creator)
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Over 30% of TikTok users" watch on mute. Simple fonts, consistent colours, do not block the face, a logo at start or end is fine. Updated 2026-08-18. [Creator Academy: common mistakes](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/avoid-common-creation-mistakes)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Search weighs how well content matches the query, plus hashtags and the sound used. Creator Search Insights shows searched topics and content gaps. [TikTok Support](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content), [Creator Academy, 2026-08-18](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/detail-page-creator-search-insights). On-screen text and spoken words being indexed: **[CLAIMED]**, not on an official page.
- **[REPORTED]** Recent: a five hashtag limit per post appeared in August 2025, without official explanation. [Social Media Today, 2025-08-17](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-implements-five-hashtag-limit-per-post/757857/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Photo Mode carousels with music since 2022-10-06; captions up to 2,200 characters (not re-verified for 2026). [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/editing-tools). How carousels rank against video: **NOT FOUND**.
- **[OFFICIAL]** Watermarks: For You ineligible is "reused or unoriginal content posted without creative edits, such as clips that show someone else's watermark or logo". The rule targets reused content, the watermark is its example. Effective 2025-09-13. [Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)
- Cross-posting the same clean file: no penalty statement found anywhere. **[REPORTED]** Instagram makes Reels "visibly recycled from other applications" (logos, watermarks) less discoverable. [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/instagram-to-make-reels-recycled-from-other-apps-like-tiktok-less-discoverable/). YouTube: not verified here.

## 5. Business vs Personal account

- **[OFFICIAL]** TikTok's own guide: a "creator, artist, or public figure" should use a Personal Account. Updated 2026-06-17. [Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/TikTok-Business-vs-Personal)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Business accounts cannot use music not licensed for commercial use. TikTok's licences outside the Commercial Music Library do not cover commercial use. [TikTok Support: Sounds](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/sounds), [TikTok Support: commercial music](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok)
- **[OFFICIAL, Symphonic]** Distributed tracks sit in the general library, which Business accounts cannot use, his own included. Commercial library opt-in needs 100% control of recording and composition and opens the track to every brand. Linking TikTok for Artists does not make the account Business. [Symphonic: FAQs](https://support.symdistro.com/hc/en-us/articles/46696058370445-TikTok-Common-Issues-FAQs)

## 7. What TikTok's own material says works without dancing, trends or talking

- **[OFFICIAL]** "Myth #1: It's all dances and lip-syncs." Every genre has a place. Updated 2026-08-18. [Creator Academy: getting started as an artist](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/get-started-as-an-artist-on-TT)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Same page: pick a performance style, "is there a scenic spot you can record from?", go LIVE for longer performances. It also suggests telling your story, as advice, not a ranking rule.
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Behind The Song": a video on how the song was written or recorded, with the official sound and #BehindTheSong, can be added to the sound page via the distributor. [TikTok for Artists help](https://artists.tiktok.com/help-center/behind-the-song)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Music Tab: the artist can "select specific parts of your songs" for creators to use. [Creator Academy: music tab](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/music-tab-introduction)
- Lyric text and visual loops as named formats in TikTok's artist material: **NOT FOUND**. Rewatches are not named as a signal on any official page opened, so loops stay **[CLAIMED]**.

## Myths to ignore

- **[MYTH]** "You need followers first." TikTok: follower count and past hits are not direct factors.
- **[MYTH]** "TikTok is only dances, lip-syncs and pop." TikTok's own artist page calls both myths.
- **[MYTH]** "Post daily or disappear." TikTok says quality over daily updates, cadence is up to you.
- **[MYTH]** "A Business account is more professional for an artist." It blocks his own released track.
- **[MYTH]** "Upload the full song as an original sound." No royalties, no sound page link, no usage count.
- **[CLAIMED]** Warm-up rituals, "50 to 200 viewer" test pools, "the first hour decides", "21 to 34 seconds". No primary evidence.
