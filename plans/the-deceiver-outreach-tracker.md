**The rule of thumb to reuse next release:**
- **Curators → Spotify link** (they add it to a Spotify playlist).
- **DJs → SoundCloud link** (listen + WAV on request).
- **Personal friends → smart link** (they open in their app; Spotify saves = the metric).
- **Reddit / Facebook → SoundCloud link** (inline player; Reddit is anti-DSP).
- **Message platform = wherever the real relationship already is** (SC for the SC-inbox crowd, IG for IG mutuals, email where they emailed). Cold IG DMs barely deliver.
