# Genre cover board (kept by Amir, 2026-11-03)

Image: artwork/edits/half-the-night-board-genre-kept.jpg. For looking only, never for publishing.
Name: "Half the Night board, genre kept". Leans to dance wave and darkwave. Real covers from our genres (dark disco, acid, darkwave, EBM, minimal wave) in a flat, ink, noir or poster
language. Found by a research pass on Bandcamp label pages (read-only).

Amir's notes (quick answers, not 📌 taste rules): likes Frigio Ecdisis 4 for how the art is divided; likes
Dark Entries / Wolfsheim for the red moon with silhouettes inside (not the type). Pinkman: kept the snake,
the face and the falling man; other Pinkman covers removed. Removed as not his style: Lustpoderosa Jack
Pattern, Kontravoid Live From The Void. Lustpoderosa Decada 2: not his style, kept on file.
