Inch of Shadow, a Berlin artist: light and shadow held together in one frame.
Hand-made print look: brush and India ink, dry brush, or old copperplate engraving. Lines, hatching and solid black carry all the shading.
Strong contrast: deep blacks next to clean paper white, a few fine white lines scratched into the black.
Black ink by default; colour only where the subject asks for it, as one loose wash.
Quiet and a little mysterious, tender rather than grim.
