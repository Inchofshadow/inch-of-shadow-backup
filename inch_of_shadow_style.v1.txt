Cinematic still in the visual world of Inch of Shadow, a Berlin underground artist. The identity is the duality of light and shadow: one inch of love is one inch of shadow. Mood: melancholic, romantic, mysterious, obscure, sexy, nocturnal, drenched in atmosphere.

Light: one dramatic source, deep shadow, long cast shadows, silhouettes and obscured or hidden faces, a single point of light glowing in darkness. High contrast, noir.

Texture: analog 35mm film grain, soft halation, the look of old film and engravings, matte not glossy.

Undertone: gothic and romantic with a quiet occult edge (shadow horns, yin and yang duality, ritual symbols), never cartoonish.

Settings when relevant: foggy gothic nature, ancient ruins, abandoned Berlin at night, empty streets under a single streetlight, tunnels and stairwells.
