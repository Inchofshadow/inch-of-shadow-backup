# Inch of Shadow - Style library (Look-11, decided 2026-09-20)

Named looks a release can borrow. Amir knows what he likes by eye; the names and their
history are here so a good direction does not get lost and can be ordered by name.

**The borrowing rule:** the brand stays constant (the brand face from `frame.md`, Albertus Nova for now, the colour moods,
text placement, safe zones, one whole, one light idea, the face rules, the pop rule). A
release borrows ONE named look on top of it, for its cover, reels, thumbnails and posts.
The library is broad and open. No single influence becomes the whole direction.

**How the looks are made:** `python3 print_looks.py <photo> --look <name>` (or `--sheet` to
see all of them on one photo, numbered). Local, no AI, no cost. The photograph is put
through a print or darkroom process, it is never redrawn, so this is a safe path for
photos of Amir. For generated images the same looks exist as image-tool options
(`--medium xerox`, `--medium cyanotype` ...).

## The looks (S1-S7 picked by Amir on his own photo 2026-09-20; S8-S10 picked 2026-10-13)

| Code | Name | What it is | Where it comes from | Feels like | Use it when | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Xerox** | hard black and white, photocopy grain, toner streaks | punk flyers and fanzines of the late 1970s: Jamie Reid for the Sex Pistols, Linder Sterling's Buzzcocks sleeve, Gee Vaucher for Crass | raw, urgent, handmade | a song with anger or speed; a flyer or gig post | it works against luxury, so it suits a single, not a whole era; faces blow out, check the eyes survive |
| S2 | **Halftone** | the newspaper dot blown up until you see it | press printing; Warhol's silkscreens from press photos; Factory Records sleeves | pop, familiar to everybody, a bit distant | a big pop moment, posters, a face made iconic | fine detail goes; never combine with small type over the dots |
| S3 | **Riso** | two inks, slightly off register, paper grain | the Risograph duplicator (Japan, 1980s) and the art-print and zine culture around it | warm, romantic, handmade but refined | love songs, warm moods (blush + plum, champagne + oxblood) | choose the two inks from the release mood, not the default rose and blue |
| S4 | **Engraving** | line and cross-hatch, like a banknote or an old book plate | intaglio printing; Gustave Dore's plates; currency portraits | classical, expensive, timeless | covers, the mark, anything that should feel like an heirloom | needs a large output, the lines turn to mud when small; test at 64px |
| S5 | **Cyanotype** | one colour, Prussian blue, soft paper | the sun print, John Herschel 1842; Anna Atkins' botanical books | night, mystery, memory | night songs; pairs with the moon violet + midnight mood | only one colour on the page: type goes in the paper white |
| S6 | **Solarisation** | silver print where the highlights fold back to dark, a dark line on the contours | the Sabattier effect; Man Ray and Lee Miller, Paris around 1930 | strange, elegant, electric | songs about doubles, deceit, transformation; echoes the flash and shadow ideas of the motion library | strongest on a picture with one clear light; flat light gives mud |
| S7 | **Pictorialism** | soft focus, warm platinum tone, glow in the highlights, dark corners | art photography around 1885 to 1915: Stieglitz, Steichen, and Julia Margaret Cameron before them | romantic, intimate, expensive | the closest look to "love and shadow, romance, luxury"; ballads, portraits with the sunglasses off | keep the eyes sharp enough to read at phone size |
| S8 | **Dither** | fine dots from error diffusion, two colours (platinum on warm black) | early computer graphics and printers (Floyd and Steinberg, 1976); today Dither Boy and the zine/net scene | digital, grainy, a bit cold, like a found screen image | electronic-lane covers, flyers, a reel frame | at small sizes the dots turn to grey; check at phone size |
| S9 | **Scanlines** | horizontal lines that get thicker where the photo is bright | TV scanlines, modulated-line dithering (Dither Boy's "modulated" styles) | screen, signal, night broadcast | a moody frame, a title card, a still from a video | it loses fine detail first; keep the face large in the frame |
| S10 | **Pixel** | big pixels in a small palette (12 colours), keeps the photo's colour | 8-bit and 16-bit computer and game graphics | playful, retro, warm | a lighter single, merch, a sticker, a profile variant | needs a clear subject; busy backgrounds turn to noise |

History lines are from general knowledge, not checked against sources on the day; verify
before quoting them in public copy.

## Rejected

- **Screenprint in flat colours** (2026-09-20): it breaks a face into blotches, which comes
  too close to the face rule "never cartooned". Do not use it on portraits.

## Candidates (Amir liked them, not yet seen on his own photo)

None right now. The dither family (Amir 2026-10-13, "keep this pixel style", then "1 3 5 can be cool"
on his photo out-037) became S8 Dither, S9 Scanlines and S10 Pixel. Dither Boy (Studio AAA,
49 GBP once, stills and video) stays a special-job tool for when a job needs more than these;
see `shadow-archive/fakeplasticbrands/sites-checked.md`.

**S11-S20, added 2026-10-14 on Amir's word ("add all 10"), still candidates:** S11 Halation (red glow from bright edges), S12 Dirty window (old glass, Lucas Garcete's texture), S13 CRT (phosphor stripes), S14 Softclub (Gen X Softclub: cold blue, soft, archive grain), S15 Emboss (pressed relief, like the APC cover), S16 ASCII, S17 VHS, S18 Anaglyph 3D, S19 Thermal, S20 Night vision. In `print_looks.py`, the playground and the Effects page (examples on his photo out-037). Each becomes a kept look only once Amir has judged it on his own photo.

**S21-S23, added 2026-10-22 from Shadow Archive R-019 (_kodam4_), Amir picked group A, still candidates:** S21 Pixel sort (bright runs dragged sideways into stripes; on a portrait it smears across the face), S22 Blowout (highlights clip to white, the rest one violet silhouette with line bands), S23 Monotint (one periwinkle wash, a little original colour left; the same tint across a set). The fourth pick, Chromatic Split Cutout, needs no new look: S18 Anaglyph on a local cut-out. Same rule: kept only once Amir has judged it on his own photo.

**S23b, S24-S25 and a VHS tune, added 2026-10-22 from the R-019 second pass (Amir: "all"), still candidates.** What he named: the big flashes on the face, the colours, the vintage vibe, VHS, "melancholic gothic witchy ... dark and mysterious, ominous", a vintage different from the Lana Del Rey one (cold and cheap, not golden). Numbers from 19 of his liked slides: 13 of 19 green-teal mids; night pictures one hue on black (saturation 0.7 to 0.9, black floor 5 to 21); day pictures milky (saturation 0.1 to 0.3, black floor 28 to 82).
- S23b Monotint night: the night setting of S23, teal (12, 138, 146) on deep black.
- S24 Night flash: the subject near the lens blows out to white with a warm yellow flash and glow (Amir: the cold version was "too fluorescent"), fast falloff, the dark around it stays cold. Bright smooth sky is kept out of the flash. A fake; the real one is the Night Flash (camera) shoot record on /effects.
- S25 Camcorder: blacks lifted to ~40, a third of the colour, green-teal mids, soft, a rainbow light leak top right.
- S17 VHS tuned: colour bleed halved, teal instead of neutral, faint vertical screen lines. Render before the tune kept as `effects/examples/look-vhs-before-2026-10-22.jpg` (old values: 4 px bleed at 1000 px, tint 1.0 / 0.97 / 1.03, no screen lines).
- `--mosaic X,Y,W,H`: pixel mosaic over a box after any look, for other people's faces only, never his.

## Not looks but subjects (open, no decision yet)

These came up on 2026-09-19 and cannot be made by putting a photo through a process; they
need new imagery, so they are shown to Amir as generated or shot examples when a release
calls for them: Art Nouveau botanical, memento mori still life, torn paper collage,
stencil lettering. The pop rule decides: would a stranger feel something in one second.

## Adding a look

A look enters the library only after Amir has seen it on his own photo and said yes. Then:
a function in `print_looks.py`, a row in the table above with its history and its rule, an
option file in `options/medium/`, and a test. The reference render goes in
`references/styles/`.
