# Social Media Skills plugin, mined 2026-10-06

What this is: Amir saw the "Social Media Skills" plugin (106 skills, Anthropic
directory, community tier, by Frank Heijdenrijk of WoopSocial, MIT) and asked
whether to have it. Decision: NOT installed. The skill files are public on
GitHub (social-media-skills/skills), so seven of them were read at source and
compared against our own playbooks. This file is the only thing that changed.
Nothing else in the repo was touched. Revert = delete this file.

Skills read: instagram-growth, instagram-seo, hashtag-strategy,
engagement-routine, profile-optimization, youtube-shorts,
viral-reverse-engineering. The other 99 are LinkedIn/Pinterest/X/Threads,
monetization, lead magnets, AI video tools, and writing templates in a
generic creator voice. Not read, not wanted.

Verdict in one line: nothing in it beats what we already have on Instagram;
a handful of numbers and two methods are worth keeping as reference.

## Worth keeping as reference (new or sharper than ours, no clash)

1. Hashtag SIZE tier: prefer tags with ~10K-500K posts over mega-tags; rotate
   2-4 reusable sets per content pillar; never paste the identical block
   twice. We say "niche" but never gave a size or a rotation rule.
2. Instagram SEO placement: primary keyword inside the first ~125 characters
   of the caption; on-screen text in the first 3 seconds carries the keyword;
   alt text must MATCH the caption (mismatch is treated as a signal);
   auto-captions on. We have keyword captions and alt text, not the placement
   or the match rule.
3. Reels eligibility gates: original, no other-platform watermark, HAS AUDIO,
   under the length cap, clear niche. "Has audio" is the one we never wrote.
4. Format-to-surface: Reels = discovery, carousels = saves/authority,
   Stories = retention, photos = low discovery. We treat Reels this way; the
   carousel-for-saves idea is new.
5. Diagnostic: views high but follows low = the profile is the leak, fix the
   profile, not the content.
6. Profile: a pinned-posts plan (first impression: start here / proof /
   current release) and a broadcast channel for superfans. We have
   highlights; nothing on pinned posts or broadcast channels.
7. YouTube Shorts numbers (we have "no own data yet"): target 70-75%+
   average viewed; lengths are bimodal, under 30s for completion or 50-60s
   for total views; 3-7 per week; the first 30-60 minutes decide spread;
   Shorts are deprioritised after ~28-30 days; AI-made Shorts need the
   "Altered Content" disclosure (since May 2025); Content ID risk over 1 min.
8. Viral reverse-engineering method, usable on our OWN best posts: deconstruct
   hook / share driver / retention / format / angle; counterfactual test
   (remove a feature, does it still work?); only 1-2 drivers are
   load-bearing; name the share-trigger from the top comments; check for
   luck and sample size before copying anything.

## Rejected (clashes with our rules, or not for us)

- Caption length 150-300 words. Our voice: half his captions are under 85
  characters, length follows mood. Keep ours.
- "Follower count is a vanity number". Followers are our KPI. Keep ours.
- "No automation, no bots" in engagement-routine. Their product rule, not
  ours; our automation has its own guards.
- Everything that routes to WoopSocial scheduling/publishing. Amir publishes
  by hand, always.
- Lead magnets, monetization, trend-jacking, meme/culture, LinkedIn,
  Pinterest, X, Threads, Facebook. Off-brand or off-platform.
- 106 auto-triggering skills competing with /ig, /promo, /release. The reason
  it was not installed.
