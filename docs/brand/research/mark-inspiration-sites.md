# Where to dig for the mark (Look-02, asked by Amir 2026-09-21)

Amir: do for the hand-drawn logo what was done for the fonts: he digs through good sites,
sends what he likes, Claude records it and reads the pattern only when he asks. The working
direction so far (Look-02): the RING, thorn ring and halo, engraving feeling, symbols a
stranger feels in one second, and it has to be POP. Start from `../mark/mark-drafts-v4.png`.

Every site below was opened and checked on 2026-09-21. Free to browse unless said.

## Logos and marks

| Site | What it is | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Logobook, https://www.logobook.com/ | curated archive of logos, symbols and trademarks, sorted by what the mark SHOWS | `/shape/rings/`, `/shape/circle/`, `/object/crowns/`, `/nature/sun/`, `/nature/plants/`, `/nature/hands/`, `/nature/eyes/`, `/object/crosses/` |
| Logo Archive, https://logo-archive.org/ | calls itself the world's largest historical logo book | browse; the page gave no detail on how it is sorted |
| Fonts In Use, Branding/Identity, https://fontsinuse.com/in/2/formats/9/branding-identity | the site he already knows, 9,157 identities; the Music topic is https://fontsinuse.com/in/1/topics/43/music (6,797 uses) | combine the two in Advanced Search |
| BP&O logos, https://bpando.org/logos/ | reviews of new identities, a logo section | free; a paid tier only adds bookmarks and a gallery view |
| Reagan Ray, https://reaganray.com/2018/04/27/record-logos | about 150 record label logos, 1910s to 2000s, mostly mid-century | also `/2020/09/22/jazz-lettering` (musicians' names as lettering) |

## Old hand-drawn emblems (the engraving feeling)

| Site | What it is | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Printers' Devices, University of Barcelona, https://marques.crai.ub.edu/en | 3,871 printers' marks from 1476 to 1907: the hand-drawn logos of their day, many with a motto around a symbol. CC BY 4.0 | browse the devices; filters for motto and ensign |
| The Public Domain Review, https://publicdomainreview.org/collections/ | curated public domain images | theme "Religion, Myth & Legend", also "Ghosts & Occult" and "Design & Typography": `/collections/all/theme/<name>/` |
| Old Book Illustrations, https://www.oldbookillustrations.com/ | engraved book illustrations, by subject, artist, technique | `/illustrations/subjects/`, `/illustrations/techniques/`; check `/terms-of-use/` before using an image |
| Letterform Archive Online, https://oa.letterformarchive.org/ | lettering and graphic design by people, decades, formats | was offline for maintenance on the day it was checked |

## Could not be checked on the day (do not count on them)

Are.na search (needs a browser to show results), The Met open access prints (the site

## Rule

An old engraving that is in the public domain may be traced or redrawn. A living company's
logo is reference only: look at how it is built, never copy it.
