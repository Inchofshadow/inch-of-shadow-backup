# Inch of Shadow - Font library (potentials, started 2026-09-20)

Amir's own research, stored for future use. **There will not be one font for everything**
(Amir, 2026-09-20): a release can bring its own face, picked from here or from new finds.
Everything below is a POTENTIAL unless the status says otherwise. Nothing here is a rule.

- The face in use right now, its file and its fallback: `frame.md`, block `face`.
- Where each reference came from, with links: `research/type-references-2026-09-20.md`.
- **Where to buy or download each font, with prices and trials: `font-sources.md`.** That
  file replaces the "Get it" column below, which is kept only as a quick hint.
- How a candidate gets tested: `references/fonts-look12/render_font_settings.py` renders any
  face in eight settings on his own photos, next to the others. Local, free.

Tags like [NOT in Canva] record a check of Canva's font search on 2026-09-20; a face Canva
has can be used in a Canva design with no purchase, the rest must be bought and uploaded.
What is set in his Brand Kit: `canva-setup.md`. The Canva faces were then SET on real type
and exported, not only searched by name: `references/fonts-look12/canva-fonts-specimen.png`.
A name in a search box is not a match; always look at the letters, and at the STYLE, because
a family's default weight in Canva can be the wrong one (Cooper BT defaults to Light).

**Before any font is used in something public:** buy the licence, and read its clause about
logo use first (Creative Market, for one, allows a font in a logo only if it is modified and
not the dominant element). Trial files and the Letterhead bundle on disk are for LOOKING
only. "Get it" below is where the Fonts In Use page or a search pointed on 2026-09-20;
"not checked" means nobody has confirmed a seller yet.

Status words: **default now** / **fallback** / **guest** (kept by Amir for titles and
posters) / **potential** (from his references) / **set aside** / **out as main**.

## Two voices (Amir, 2026-09-20)

Setting a loud face next to a quiet one (Madonna's tall capitals with a thin script, The
Human League's bold sans with a thin lowercase) "is really my style". It belongs to the
ARTWORK OF A RELEASE: the pair is chosen per release, together with its cover, from the
groups above (a display face from A to E, a partner from F, G or H). It is not a fixed brand
pair.

## Adding to the library

A font enters when Amir points at it (a link, a screenshot, a photo of a sign). Identify it,
put it in the right group with where it was seen and where to get it, and leave the status
"potential" until he has seen it on his own photos.
