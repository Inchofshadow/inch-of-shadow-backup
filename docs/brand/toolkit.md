# Inch of Shadow - Reference toolkit (decided 2026-10-12)

The few reference sites we use every time, one first stop per job, and how a reference
search runs so Amir does not have to browse. Decided with Amir on 2026-10-12 after every
site from @bywaviboy's lists was opened and the strongest were tested on searches from his
world. Full checks of all 29 sites: `shadow-archive/waviboy/sites-checked.md`.

Rule (Amir): few tools, permanent, super quality, understood before use. A job is reopened
only when a tool clearly beats the kept one.

## The daily kit (all free)

| Tool | What we do with it |
|---|---|
| [FILMGRAB](https://film-grab.com) | Pick a film per release or shoot; its stills teach the light, framing and colour. Feeds a shoot brief, a grade, or an AI background plate he is composited into. About 66 stills per film, cinematographer credited; 37 of 40 films from his world were there (Wings of Desire, Only Lovers Left Alive, The Hunger, In the Mood for Love, Fallen Angels, Cat People, Nosferatu, Possession, Lost Highway, Querelle, Control...). Reference only. |
| [Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so) | "More like this": search by feeling, colour or an uploaded image; a Pinterest without ads, with a filter that hides AI images. Builds a mood board fast. Reference only. |
| [Are.na](https://www.are.na) | Scene archives built by designers: record sleeves, flyers, posters (6,600 boards for post-punk design). Covers, flyers, logo. Reference only. |
| [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org) | Old art that may go ON a cover: engravings, symbols, moons, angels, each collection labelled with its rights. First pearl: Kay Nielsen's 1922 illustrations for "East of the Sun and West of the Moon". |
| [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com) | Fonts on real record covers and posters. Font buying stays in `font-sources.md`. |

## By job

| Job | First stop | Second stop | Only when needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover art | Cosmos (the mood) | Public Domain Review (usable art), Fonts In Use (title) | Getty Open Content (more rights-free) |
| Flyer / gig poster | Are.na | Fonts In Use | Letterform Archive (type history) |
| Photo shoot | FILMGRAB (the light) | Cosmos (poses, places) | ShotDeck, one month (one exact light) |
| Reel / music video | FILMGRAB (a film's language) | Cosmos | Directors' Library (how a campaign was shot) |
| Logo / wordmark | Fonts In Use | Are.na | Letterform Archive |

**Paid, only for a job that needs it:** [ShotDeck](https://shotdeck.com), $12.95 a month: FILMGRAB searchable by light, colour and lens. One month when a shoot needs one exact light.

**Saved for special jobs:** [Directors' Library](https://directorslibrary.com), [Letterform Archive](https://oa.letterformarchive.org), [Library of Congress free to use](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/), [Getty Open Content](https://www.getty.edu/projects/open-content-program/), [Magnum Photos](https://www.magnumphotos.com), [Atlas Obscura Berlin](https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/berlin-germany) (location scouting for a shoot: 173 unusual Berlin places; picked by Amir 2026-10-13 from @fakeplasticbrands' list, R-007).

**Checked and dropped:** Huemint (the colours are decided in the brand), Same Energy (cheaper results than Cosmos), CARI (consumer-design eras, not his world), Savee, Kosmik (shut down May 2026), Godly (now a tech-design feed), AIGA Eye on Design (nothing new since 2022), Archive Design (an empty page), Mograph Encyclopedia (a 7-entry blog).

## Rights, in one line

Film stills, Cosmos, Are.na, Magnum and most images found online are for LOOKING. Only
public domain or rights-free sources (Public Domain Review with its labels, Library of
Congress free to use, Getty Open Content) may go into published work. A film still is a
blueprint, never material: no AI turns him into a character inside a still (his face is
never changed by AI, `frame.md`), and a near-copy of a frame is never published.

## How a reference search runs (Amir does not browse)

1. **Claude asks, Amir answers (Amir, 2026-10-12: "ask me questions each time to point me
   and direct me").** Claude draws the brief out with short questions, ONE OR TWO AT A
   TIME, each with 2 to 4 example answers he can pick or replace. Never a long numbered
   list. Stop as soon as the brief is clear (usually 3 or 4 questions). No search starts on
   Claude's guess of a song's feeling. The questions per job are below.
2. **Claude searches** the first and second stops for that job, in the app's own browser
   (never his accounts), and builds ONE board of 8 to 12 references: small images, the
   source link, one line each on why it is there.
3. **What the 8 to 12 are picked on:** the brief; what he already chose (Shadow Archive
   entries, direct influences, tags for that release, the release's colour mood); what he
   excluded; the brand rules (one light idea, the pop rule, never only dark, familiar
   symbols handled with taste, nothing that needs explaining). A spread, not twelve of the
   same: about 4 close to what he likes, 4 that stretch, 2 or 3 wild cards.
4. **Amir picks by number** ("3, 7, 9") or says what is wrong, in about 5 minutes.
5. **Claude turns the picks into the work:** a shoot brief, a grade, a cover direction, a
   flyer layout. His picks and rejections go into `taste.md`, but only through a question
   marked 📌 and a read-back line he confirms (see that file), so the next board is sharper.

When Amir finds something himself, he sends it in the chat; it goes into the Shadow
Archive.

## The questions, per job (asked one or two at a time)

Every job starts with: **which song or which event is it for?** Then:

**Cover art**
1. If this song played behind a movie scene, what is on screen? (a place, a time of day, who is there)
2. Is he in the picture, or only the world around him? (him / a silhouette / no person / an object)
3. One word for the feeling a stranger should get in the first second? (romantic / restless / mysterious / warm ...)
4. Anything from the archive or a film it should feel close to? Anything it must NOT look like?

**Flyer / gig poster**
1. Where and when, and who is it for? (a club night / a live show / a guest mix)
2. Loud or quiet? (a big title leads / a big photo leads)
3. A flyer or sleeve he has seen and liked, or an era it should belong to?

**Photo shoot**
1. Which location? (cemetery / Tempelhofer Feld / Koernerpark / Neukoelln streets / the studio)
2. Day, dusk or night, and what light? (sun / blue hour / streetlight / flash)
3. Sunglasses on or off, and which outfit? (tailoring / leather / other)
4. A film whose look it should borrow?

**Reel / music video**
1. Which part of the song, and what should a stranger feel in the first two seconds?
2. Is he performing, walking through a place, or not on screen?
3. A film or a reel whose movement or cut it should feel like?

**Logo / wordmark**
1. Which feeling should the name carry? (carved and old / sharp and modern / hand-drawn)
2. Where must it work first? (cover corner / profile picture / stage screen)
3. A logo or sleeve he admires, and one he dislikes?


## Copy tools (local, added 2026-10-15)

Two scripts at the repo root, written in house, no network, no API. Both run by
themselves as the last pass inside the Release Manager copy writers (SEO Copy,
Bio Manager), and from the command line on any draft.

| Tool | What we do with it |
|---|---|
| `copy_clean.py` | Clean a draft before it is pasted anywhere: invisible characters out (zero-width spaces, soft hyphens, byte-order marks), odd spaces to plain, em and en dashes and double hyphens to commas, curly quotes straight, the ellipsis character to three dots. Emojis, the middle dot, handles, hashtags and links are untouched. `pbpaste \| python3 copy_clean.py - --report \| pbcopy` |
| `caption_check.py` | Show an Instagram caption the way the feed cuts it (the first 125 characters in a box), then check it against voice.md: length, first sentence before the cut, no greeting opener, no dashes, no feedback or engagement asks, no clickbait or filler, 3 to 5 precise hashtags at the end, emojis used lightly, no URL, the name and handle written right, the exact photo credit, and any `--keywords` inside the first 125 characters. Exit code 1 on a FAIL. `pbpaste \| python3 caption_check.py - --keywords "berlin,dark disco"` |
