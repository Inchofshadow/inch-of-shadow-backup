# RAPVILLE playbook (his method, not yet adapted)

What this is: one merged summary of the method taught on the RAPVILLE music-marketing channel (Niall/Nile and team), built only from the per-video notes in `videos/`. Not adapted to any artist.
Coverage: all 38 long YouTube videos read 2026-10-11: 34 from YouTube captions, 4 transcribed locally with speech-to-text because they had no captions (G-Eazy, Drake's producer, D Smoke, Eric Bellinger strategy; names in those may be mis-heard). All numbers he states are labelled "claimed".

## 1. The big picture

**His roadmaps (several versions of the same idea)**
- **Fanbase Growth Roadmap, 4 stages:** (1) Brand and Music, (2) Go Viral, (3) World Building, (4) Conversion. Getting brand and music right first gives 10x better campaigns (claimed). [4 secrets](videos/the-4-secrets-to-growing-a-fanbase-in-2026-full-guide--B4wbAR7-bPg.md)
- **4-step content system:** branding -> testing formats -> doubling down -> conversion. Streams are planted at the branding step, not at the end. [Content into streams](videos/how-i-turn-content-into-streams-not-views--vPULwokgVqY.md)
- **30-day launch:** brand -> experiment -> double down -> convert, all inside the first 30 days; first 48 hours = branding. [Desiree 9M](videos/her-first-ep-ever-and-how-she-hit-9m-views-in-30-days--po6P7I1gM6U.md)
- **3-stage content system:** branding -> building momentum (test formats, then double down on data) -> conversion. He relabels the stages inside the same video. [DeCarlo 4.2M](videos/how-i-got-decarlo-4-2m-views-in-5-posts--nGk2OVbyn9o.md)
- **4-phase repeatable system:** (1) lead with emotion, (2) manufacture demand, (3) pivot when formats die, (4) build a repeatable system, then repeat. [1M streams EP](videos/helping-my-bro-get-1m-streams-on-his-ep--69EbCOIqhIw.md)
- **3-month plan:** brand -> music vision -> competitor research -> tailored strategy; month 1 = 40 posts testing 10 stolen formats; month 2 = double down, reach the younger trend-driving audience; month 3+ = you own an audience. [15M in 30 days](videos/getting-an-unemployed-artist-15-000-000-views-in-30-days--LviwMK-GUVU.md)
- **Three needs:** branding with a unique edge, hooks built on psychology, a world built on emotions people return to. [Arjuna 8M](videos/getting-an-artist-8m-views-in-first-8-posts--RrcXl8bcKXg.md)
- **Attention first, conversion second:** don't reject viral content because it "doesn't convert"; viral hooks bring people into the world, different content moves them down the funnel. Pushing streams or Meta ads before anyone is hooked is the common mistake (first-date analogy). [Bianca 16M](videos/how-we-took-an-unknown-singer-from-0-to-16m-views--96-zC5Vt51g.md) [DeCarlo 4.2M](videos/how-i-got-decarlo-4-2m-views-in-5-posts--nGk2OVbyn9o.md)
- **Funnel direction:** go viral on short-form first, which then feeds YouTube. [Jason Derulo secrets](videos/i-stole-jason-derulo-s-marketing-secrets-to-get-130m-views-f--RUlebhsGt7k.md)
- **Top / middle / bottom of funnel:** RAPVILLE first sold virality, then realised artists want streams and a full-time career, so they built middle-of-funnel (trust, personality) and bottom-of-funnel (converting) content. [Guru vs 10 artists](videos/music-guru-vs-10-upcoming-artists--v8H8EQQD4D0.md)

**The 5 artist levels** (each with a diagnostic and one exit move) [5 levels](videos/the-5-levels-of-music-artist-which-level-are-you--tGtIyxkCmSg.md)
1. **The Vault Artist:** finished songs on a hard drive, only posts announcements, says "I'm an artist, not a content creator". The vault is armour against rejection. Decide hobby or impact. Exit task: one sentence per finished song: who it's for, where they live online, what video would stop them scrolling.
2. **The Frozen Artist:** has a plan and ideas but posts nothing; consuming marketing content feels like progress. Fear hides as (a) the rebrand (redecorating a house nobody visits) and (b) timing (waiting for a release). Diagnostic: you haven't posted this week. Exit: post something today, unedited.
3. **Lost in the Noise:** posting but not growing, stuck in one of three traps: (a) "the 2019 playbook" (playlist pitching, paid ads, expensive music videos: borrowed attention, a leaky bucket, listeners not fans), (b) "the salesman strategy" (every post sells), (c) "the begging formats" (smallness as content). Diagnostic: 90 days of posting and you can't explain why your best video worked. Exit: borrow whatever stops the scroll, but what follows must be your own world.
4. **The One-Man Army:** has a working format but executes everything alone. Fixes that fail: work harder, cut back, hire people (you "bought a second job"). The problem is architecture. Exits: people who buy into your vision (Citizen's brother, friend, girlfriend) or a professional team.
5. **The Artist CEO:** does less, grows faster; owner who says yes/no. Monthly loop: branding call -> base document -> approve/kill ideas -> pre-production call -> shoot day (directed, focuses on energy) -> review edits -> assistant posts -> end-of-month report -> new direction. Once this runs, music videos, paid ads and PR start to work.

## 2. Brand and world

**What brand is (his frameworks)**
- **The three A's: Art, Artist, Audience.** [4 secrets](videos/the-4-secrets-to-growing-a-fanbase-in-2026-full-guide--B4wbAR7-bPg.md) [Next phase](videos/you-re-not-ready-for-the-next-phase-of-music-content--OYaYVf7XKuc.md) [RB 100k streams](videos/getting-an-artist-100k-streams-in-14-days--7zpipZe0Wy8.md) [Eric Bellinger strategy](videos/creating-eric-bellinger-s-viral-content-strategy-in-25-minut--k-_qK6svdho.md)
  - **Art:** what problem the music solves; the sound/feeling you're known for; engineer it for virality; the **consistent emotional anchor** (the one feeling of the song, in every concept); the **lyric-to-hook visual**.
  - **Artist:** **unique angle** (the one thing nobody can replicate); **character, values, personality** (how you show up on camera); **visual identity** (clothes, background, characters, world).
  - **Audience:** demographic from your posting data; the **ideal fan profile** (look like them, sound like them, make what they already watch); who it's for and why they care; **your relationship** with them (icon/hard to reach vs best friend); **something to belong to** and what your music is *for*.
- Worked example of the three A's: the anchor read straight from a lyric that names the feeling; the transition moment placed on that lyric after a build-up; unique angle = the artist's real partner (real chemistry beats hired actors and models); character = maturity, the wisest rather than the coolest, hard to build and so hard to copy (a "one-of-one" brand); audience split from data (70% women, claimed) while still reaching Gen Z. Write the anchor and the unique angle on the board so every idea carries them. [Eric Bellinger strategy](videos/creating-eric-bellinger-s-viral-content-strategy-in-25-minut--k-_qK6svdho.md)
- Alternate split: **art, content direction, visual identity.** [Arjuna 8M](videos/getting-an-artist-8m-views-in-first-8-posts--RrcXl8bcKXg.md)
- Brand is "the DNA" of the content; not a logo, bio or page aesthetic, but a stranger feeling a video was made for them. [DeCarlo 4.2M](videos/how-i-got-decarlo-4-2m-views-in-5-posts--nGk2OVbyn9o.md) [Anyone to stream](videos/how-to-get-anyone-to-stream-your-music--y1Hu1xpf-x8.md)
- Brand the **song**, not the artist's long-term philosophy; ask only content-relevant questions. [Stranger in 21 min](videos/building-a-viral-music-content-strategy-for-a-stranger-in-21--Yw_SG6SlR1o.md)
- The core message is already in your songs; build the brand around existing songs and check it against the music (one-hour branding session). [Guru vs 10 artists](videos/music-guru-vs-10-upcoming-artists--v8H8EQQD4D0.md)
- Brand acts as a "tinted lens" for taking viral videos and rebuilding them. [Next phase](videos/you-re-not-ready-for-the-next-phase-of-music-content--OYaYVf7XKuc.md) [Content into streams](videos/how-i-turn-content-into-streams-not-views--vPULwokgVqY.md)
- Watch the artist's best existing videos to see the brand visually; set concrete goals (listeners, home market, show size). [Cult fanbase](videos/how-to-build-a-cult-like-fanbase-for-your-music--3Mj9YnSz8EI.md)

**One person / ideal listener**
- **"Your one person":** the specific listener who feels the content was made by someone who already knows them. Precision makes them share, sharing drives virality. Ask "how do I get my one person to share this?" not "how do I get the algorithm to push it?". [First 10M views](videos/how-to-get-your-first-10-million-views-as-an-unknown-artist--TcGo7-CNxXM.md)
- **One-person paragraph:** age; what their environment feels like; what they scroll; what they share; what they'd send a friend at midnight and why; what that says about them; why they listen to you. Can't write it = "one person problem". [First 10M views](videos/how-to-get-your-first-10-million-views-as-an-unknown-artist--TcGo7-CNxXM.md)
- Ask who the music will actually connect with, not who you want; may show a gap between the artist you are and want to be. For a dormant audience: what would make existing followers feel the account came back to life for them. [First 10M views](videos/how-to-get-your-first-10-million-views-as-an-unknown-artist--TcGo7-CNxXM.md)
- Ideal-listener questions: what they love, hate, what's trending for them, what emotions they want, why you over famous artists. [DC3 strategy](videos/i-stole-dc3-s-content-strategy-to-make-my-artists-go-viral--kY-65EpXwsc.md)
- Your audience decides visual choices, not your taste; build the character to be admirable to them; pick locations that person thinks are cool. [How to go viral](videos/how-to-go-viral-as-a-music-artist--isBn6cUo9pw.md)
- With an existing audience, build the visual identity from what they already love about you. [How to go viral](videos/how-to-go-viral-as-a-music-artist--isBn6cUo9pw.md)

## 4. Hooks

**Timing and the decision window**
- The first 3 seconds decide performance; viewers decide "is this for me?" in ~3.5 s; curiosity must start in the first second. [Irresistible hooks](videos/how-to-create-irresistible-hooks-and-blow-up-your-music--od7XTyFJ9-I.md) [Stranger in 21 min](videos/building-a-viral-music-content-strategy-for-a-stranger-in-21--Yw_SG6SlR1o.md) [RB 100k streams](videos/getting-an-artist-100k-streams-in-14-days--7zpipZe0Wy8.md)

**Viral hook formula (three steps + two hacks)** [Irresistible hooks](videos/how-to-create-irresistible-hooks-and-blow-up-your-music--od7XTyFJ9-I.md)
1. Pick **one anchor emotion** before shooting from the **six emotions**: love, heartbreak, cringe, shock, nostalgia, relatability. Use them in the song too.
2. **Dopamine tease:** storytelling clarity in the first seconds, derived from the song's subject; wrong viewers leave, right ones stay.
3. **Solidify the scroll stop:** progress within milliseconds; stun, establish the scenario, tease the next beat.
4. **Dopamine blaster** ("the sledgehammer"): the emotion hits at full force, ideally via a twist. Also works for slides ("three slides are always the key").
5. Hack: **text hook and captions** (claimed difference between 200 and 20,000 views); naming a nationality doubles as audience targeting.
6. Hack: **familiarity** = comfort = watch time; unfamiliarity = confusion = scroll.

**Three parts of a hook:** spoken, visual (context that lets you predict), text. People see before they hear; subtitles are not a text hook. [Stranger in 21 min](videos/building-a-viral-music-content-strategy-for-a-stranger-in-21--Yw_SG6SlR1o.md)

**Audience prediction**
- Make people wait and predict; the payoff surprises or satisfies. "Predictability" is the key word; the reacting person must be on screen from the start. [Stranger in 21 min](videos/building-a-viral-music-content-strategy-for-a-stranger-in-21--Yw_SG6SlR1o.md) [Eric Bellinger strategy](videos/creating-eric-bellinger-s-viral-content-strategy-in-25-minut--k-_qK6svdho.md)
