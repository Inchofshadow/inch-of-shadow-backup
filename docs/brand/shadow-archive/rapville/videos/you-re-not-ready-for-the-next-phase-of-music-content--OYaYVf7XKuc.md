# You're Not Ready For The Next Phase of Music Content
URL: https://youtu.be/OYaYVf7XKuc | Length: 5:48 | Views: 886 | Date: 2026-05-12
Chapters: The problem with content / Defining your brand pillars / Content ideation framework / Variation and scaling

## Core idea
Batch-shooting 50 lip-sync reels from different angles (the "mini music video format") is pointless if none of it goes viral. Instead: define three brand pillars, then plan 15-30 ideas that each tick five boxes, use a variation strategy and BTS, and keep building an "artist universe" world by world each month.

## Lessons and tactics
1. (00:00) The common advice (rent a space, film lip-syncs from different angles, 50 reels in a day) is "the most stupid thing", because volume without virality is pointless.
2. (00:32) Problem: going to a shoot with no plan beyond performance reels from different angles. He calls this the **"mini music video format"** and says every artist does it. The goal is a "one-of-one" brand nobody can copy.
3. (00:53) Three brand pillars: **the art** (music), **the artist** (you), **the audience** (who the content is for).
4. Art pillar:
   - (01:08) **Consistent emotional anchor**: the one feeling the song gives, used in every concept (Eric Bellinger: the moment he knew he loved his wife).
   - (01:21) **Lyric-to-hook visual**: act out the lyric (Citizen: "I caught you falling for me", he catches his girlfriend).
5. Artist pillar:
   - (01:34) **Unique angle**: what sets you apart (La Gigi: "extreme girl boss" brand).
   - (01:49) **Character and values**: Jason Derulo has earned a license to be mysterious as an icon. An up-and-comer like Audri doesn't lip-sync or "stunt for the gram". He is low-key, which he credits as one reason for the EP's success.
   - (02:11) **Visual identity**: clothes, props, location, overall aesthetic, all consistent and congruent (Citizen's "Time" rollout: blue-tinted glasses, blue Mustang, Miami; Mike Manos (spelling unclear): same masked character, breaking props, same archway location).
6. Audience pillar:
   - (02:38) Audience demographic from your existing posting data (skip it if you have never posted).
   - (02:46) **Ideal fan profile**: who exactly the content is for. Look like them, sound like them, make content they already watch.
7. (02:59) The brand acts as a "tinted lens" for ideas, aimed at your song and brand rather than generic trends or performance reels.
8. (03:12) Biggest lesson: never shoot without a plan. Have **15-30 viral content ideas** ready, all carrying the emotional anchor and visual identity so the content is congruent and memorable.
9. (03:29) Five boxes each idea must tick: **format, concept, hook, transition moment, performance**.
   - (03:38) Format: e.g. the transition format, which opens a question in the viewer's mind and then surprises them with the answer.
   - (03:45) Concept: research music marketing AND wider niche inspiration, e.g. movies and familiar scenes everyone knows (catching your girl in the rain, nearly falling off a cliff). That is how you "tap into the culture".
   - (04:04) Hook: must be believable, with a storyline and a reason for being there. Not a BTS-of-a-shoot "what is bro doing" setup.
   - (04:14) Transition moment: must be smooth and needs a **connector** that bridges hook to performance (Eric Bellinger: a hug; City Girls: the catch).
   - (04:28) Performance: engineer it with **re-hooks** so people don't get bored and scroll.
10. (04:38) **Variation strategy**: same setup, slightly different ideas, without changing room, lighting or camera.
11. (04:53) Record the **behind the scenes** of the shoot too. With Eric Bellinger it cost no extra time and was the most viral video of the shoot.
12. (05:06) **Artist universe**: every month, enter a "new world with a new character and a new emotional angle". Each world builds on the previous one (Citizen's "Time" rollout = the blue car, Miami world).

## Examples he uses

- La Gigi: extreme girl-boss angle.
- Jason Derulo: icon, so can be mysterious.

## Process / checklists
- Brand pillars (art: emotional anchor, lyric-to-hook visual / artist: unique angle, character and values, visual identity / audience: demographic data, ideal fan profile) -> 15-30 ideas, each ticking format / concept / hook / transition / performance -> variations on the same setup -> film BTS -> monthly new world in the universe.
