# how to shoot 30 VIRAL music reels in 1 day
URL: https://youtu.be/lNzsuhqemWA | Length: 6:29 | Views: 13,318 (uploaded 2026-01-20)

## Core idea (2-3 sentences)
His workflow for shooting 30 reels in one 6-8 hour day: mindset and branding first, then heavy pre-production (strategy from music + brand document + viral video bank, props, extras, videographer vetting and training, location route), then a run-and-gun shoot led by the artist, then editing with attention to subtle details and daily posting. "Fail to prepare and you prepare to fail."

## Lessons and tactics
1. Mindset: learn to love the process and the creativity of content; enthusiasm is the difference between millions of views and 500 (00:43-01:01).
2. Step 1, "rehearse in the mirror", literally and as branding: who do you want to come across as on camera, what do performances in viral videos look like, what is your unique angle, what should you wear; worst thing is looking like you are faking it on shoot day (01:01-01:30).
3. Step 2, pre-production includes: strategy, videographer, shot list, props, extras, locations, timing - after branding and choosing the music focus (01:36-01:49).
4. Content strategy is the highest-leverage point; get it wrong and results are gone (01:50-01:58).
5. Ideation setup: three things in front of him - the music, the branding document, and a viral video bank (01:58-02:06).
6. Do not copy from the bank; "rebrand": strip the viral video's principles to bare bones (e.g. normal situation -> unexpected turn -> he falls -> music), then re-skin for the song (heartbreak song: trying to break into an ex's house, thrown out, hits the deck, music) (02:06-02:27).
7. Make only 15 ideas; get 30 reels by recording each idea with two different songs of a similar vibe (02:27-02:38).
8. After strategy: order props and hire extras as needed (02:40-02:46).
9. Videographer: use a network; vet camera, lens, equipment (know what you are looking for), portfolio; short-form experience is key in 2026; biggest mistake is hiring a music-video specialist, who is not automatically educated on marketing and short-form (02:48-03:27).
10. Train the videographer: send a ~15-minute Loom walking through the ideas in depth; then a pre-production call with artist and videographer, aimed at getting the best out of the shots, not learning the ideas (03:29-03:44).
11. Plan locations and a route on that call; run-and-gun needs locations very close together, or hire one studio for the whole shoot; a far-off location can burn 1.5 hours (03:46-04:07).
12. Step 3, production: 30 pieces in a 6-8 hour run-and-gun; the artist is the "mission driver" and their passion predicts the day's success (04:14-04:28). You can usually tell how the day will go in the first 45 minutes (04:36-04:40). Four things to watch:
    a. Have the song timestamps on your phone, hooked up to a speaker (04:40-04:45).
    b. If you feel lost or scrambled, call off the shoot and do it tomorrow (04:45-04:50).
    c. If the videographer lacks confidence and the first couple of clips are bad, call off and find another (04:49-04:57).
    d. Team chemistry: make friends and lead the team; if performance energy is not there, pick it up ("just be cringe"); know the plan, set the pace (05:02-05:18).
13. One day of full effort covers the whole month of content (05:18-05:22).
14. Step 4, post-production = editing and posting. Editing quality depends on pre-production and production (bad clips/strategy cannot be saved) (05:31-05:42). Then the subtle things count: transition timing, lyric font and colour, colour grading; do an editing review against the vision (05:43-05:59).
15. Posting: hashtags are inconsequential; description = whatever first comes to mind (funny, motivational); because content is batch recorded and edited, never miss a day (05:59-06:11).

## Process / checklists
Shoot-day pipeline: 1) mindset + branding (mirror rehearsal, wardrobe, unique angle) -> 2) pre-production: music focus, strategy (music + brand doc + viral bank, rebrand to bare-bones principle), 15 ideas x 2 songs, shot list, props, extras, videographer vetting (camera, lens, kit, short-form portfolio), Loom training, pre-production call, location route / single studio, timing -> 3) production 6-8 h: song timestamps + speaker, 45-minute go/no-go check (artist state, videographer clips), team chemistry, energy -> 4) post: edit review (transition timing, lyric font/colour, grade), post daily, no hashtags focus.
