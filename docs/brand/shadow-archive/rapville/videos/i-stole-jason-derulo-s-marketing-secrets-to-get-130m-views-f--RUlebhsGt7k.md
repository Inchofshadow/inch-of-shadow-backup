# I stole Jason Derulo's marketing secrets to get 130M views for my artists
URL: https://youtu.be/RUlebhsGt7k · Length 9:17 · Views 541

## Core idea
Artists who only sell their music ("song out now") never build a brand; the best artists entertain and build a "universe" so people go stream on their own. From meeting Jason Derulo he takes three lessons (entertainer mindset, don't do everything alone, focus on short-form), and says virality depends on a viral format personalised to the artist.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Problem: being focused only on selling the music in every post means you never build a brand (01:19-01:32).
2. The best artists never look like they are selling; they create a universe in their content that viewers want to tap into, then go stream (01:36-01:50).
3. Entertain instead of sell; that is "selling subliminally" (01:54-01:58).
4. Build a consistent, intentional brand made of several content pillars that don't feel forced. DC3's pillars: comedy content with transitions, entertainment/performance content, and Christian faith content (02:06-02:22).
5. Off-music topics (e.g. comedy) aren't unfocused: begging people to listen reaches only the small share who support upcoming artists; brand topics attract the masses and a much larger group interested in those topics (02:35-02:53).
6. Natural content pulls viewers across platforms (Instagram -> Spotify) and to shows/merch (03:01-03:21).
7. Derulo lesson 1, "the Derulo mindset": when recording, you are an entertainer; "put on the mask", start with a straight face and snap into character on record (03:43-04:04).
8. On camera it may not feel like the normal you; everything on social is engineered; every viral video has a reason, so study content with that eye (04:06-04:27).
9. Derulo lesson 2: don't do everything alone; systemise by giving people responsibilities (research, posting consistently, recording, scripting), or you burn out and become inconsistent (04:29-04:55).
10. Derulo lesson 3: put most energy into short-form (TikTok, Instagram Reels), not a music video. A music video is one moment; for the same time/money you can make ~100 short videos of ~20 s = 100 chances instead of one (04:57-05:38).
11. Audiences act after repeated exposure (5th, 6th view), not the first: "consistent touch points" (dating analogy) (05:40-06:06).
12. Funnel direction: go viral with short-form first, which then funnels into YouTube; "it starts from the top and then goes into the YouTube" (06:37-06:46).
13. Viral content depends on one thing: a viral format personalised to you and your brand; otherwise no one remembers you and you look like you're copying trends (06:52-07:06).
14. Tie the format to the song itself (title/lyrics): see examples below (07:10-07:46).

## Examples he uses
- Jason Derulo: 50 billion streams across his tracks; met at his $20M house (claimed) (00:10-00:26).
- His own client results before: 700K, 150K, 600K views; after: 15M, 22M, 8.2M views on a single 18-second music-promo video; 22M while filming in LA (claimed) (00:38-01:07). Title claims 130M views total (claimed).
- DC3: example of a brand universe (comedy transitions, performances, faith); the speaker found his music via Instagram and streamed it on Spotify (01:43-03:14).
- Adriano: told no point pushing a music video; viral videos got millions of views -> 54,000 monthly listeners, promoted song 190,000 streams; started at 200 followers; his music video only 13K plays (claimed) (06:08-06:35). Format: slideshow posts with paintings + on-screen text visualising an old-school, romantic, nonchalant vibe (07:52-08:10).
- Jo (spelling unclear): "disaster videos" because the song is called "Worst Case Scenario" (07:12-07:20).
- Gary Gray: "king of TV transitions"; 40M views on one video; a 15M-view video with an ocean background where he pours sand, because the song is "Ocean" and the lyrics match the visual (claimed) (07:22-07:46).

## Process / checklists
- Brand: pick consistent pillars (e.g. comedy / performance / personal belief) -> entertain, don't sell.
- Derulo's three: entertainer mask on record; delegate roles (research, posting, recording, scripting); short-form over music video.
- Format: build one viral format personalised to you and to the song's title/lyrics.
