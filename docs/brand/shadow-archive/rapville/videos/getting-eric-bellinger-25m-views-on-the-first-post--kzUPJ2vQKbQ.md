# Getting Eric Bellinger 25M views on the first post
URL: https://youtu.be/kzUPJ2vQKbQ | Length: 3:46 | Views: 2,588 | Date: 2025-10-18

## Core idea
Eric Bellinger, a songwriter with a big catalog, had "2010 marketing syndrome": photo posts, no hooks, low views. The team worked backwards from the comment section they wanted (debate, love vs hate). They took a proven controversial format (the TV transition) and adapted it to his song and brand, then repeated variations of it.

## Lessons and tactics
1. (00:10) Diagnose "2010 marketing syndrome": too many picture posts, not enough hooks, no wow factor.
2. (00:32) Understand the brand before any strategy. Ask the artist one key question: what do you want to be remembered for? (Eric said "a marketing genius".)
3. (00:48) "Reverse engineer virality": first imagine the comment section you want. He wanted controversy and debate, because comments show what people really think of you.
4. (01:03) Then look for trends that already sparked that same kind of controversy in their comments.
5. (01:13) Format used: the **TV transition** (previously done with the artist Gary).
6. (01:19) Spin the trend for the artist's brand and song so it becomes a memorable moment for that song.
7. (01:30) Read the song's feeling first. Here it was joy, dancing, a good time with loved ones, and that shaped the execution: Eric kneels on a chair, a woman underneath spins him around, then it transitions to the shot.
8. (01:45) Visibly faking something (clearly not filmed in the garden but pretending it is) drives "this is genius" vs "this isn't real" comments.
9. (01:59) You need both haters and lovers: "You can't do it with just one."
10. (02:24) The lesson he says most artists miss: once a format works, make it yours and "rinse and repeat" with variations (again with a woman in it, different variations).
11. (02:52) The secret: steal trends and formats, but spin them for your brand and your song.
12. (03:02) Variation examples: Gary's song "Ocean" put the TV background in an ocean while he holds sand, so it all fits the song.
13. (03:15) If the TV format feels overused, use a **lyric-matched action** instead (Citizen: lyric "I caught you falling for me" while his girlfriend falls off a ladder).

## Examples he uses
- Eric Bellinger (hits with Chris Brown, Justin Bieber; Grammy-nominated): claimed 1.1M followers but consistently under 30k views before.
- First post: claimed ~600k views overnight, 25M views after about a week, 8,000 comments. (Elsewhere he says "22 million"; the caption has both 25M and 22M.)
- Follow-up variations: claimed went viral again, often 3-4x his previous views.
- Gary: TV transition claimed 100M+ views; "Ocean" variation.
- Citizen: ladder-fall lyric visual.

## Process / checklists
- Strategy steps: ask the brand question -> picture the target comment section -> find trends with that comment profile -> adapt to the song's emotion and brand -> post -> repeat the winning format with variations.
