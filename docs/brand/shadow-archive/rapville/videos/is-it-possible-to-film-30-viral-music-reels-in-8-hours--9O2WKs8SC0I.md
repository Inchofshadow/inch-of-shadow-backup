# Is It Possible To Film 30 Viral Music Reels In 8 Hours…
URL: https://youtu.be/9O2WKs8SC0I · Length 2:16 · Views 383

## Core idea
A client testimonial plus a short explanation of how the agency films a month of content in one day: ideas are fully planned before the shoot, the videographer is briefed on them, and editors handle the finishing details.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Having someone else film you and manage time, ideas and outfits is described by the artist as very useful, "even compulsory" (00:21-00:33).
2. A pre-made plan compressed the shoot to about 7-8 hours vs an estimated 2-3+ days alone (00:42-00:55).
3. Do not show up to a shoot asking "what are we going to record?"; prepare ideas first ("if you fail to prepare, you prepare to fail", said backwards in captions) (01:04-01:16).
4. Research viral ideas specific to the artist, their music and brand before the shoot; gather a month's worth of ideas (01:16-01:32).
5. Brief the videographer on that idea list so on shoot day they know exactly what to record and how (01:32-01:38).
6. In the edit, get "the little things" right: sound design, transition timing, colour grading, lyric fonts and colours (01:43-01:51).
7. Shoot locations named by the artist: golf course and a pool (cold, but the shot looked good) (00:03-00:12).

## Examples he uses
- Unnamed artist on shoot day (golf course, pool); estimates 7-8 hour shoot vs 2-3 days alone (claimed).
- Says they have done this with Eric Bellinger and Jason Derulo (claimed) (01:51-01:55).

## Process / checklists
Shoot-day pipeline: strategists research a month of artist-specific ideas -> train/brief videographer -> one-day shoot -> in-house edit (sound design, transition timing, colour grade, lyric fonts/colours).

## Sales pitch / filler
Most of the video is a sales piece: testimonial, then booking slots for May, "book a call with a senior growth executive" (01:56-02:14).
