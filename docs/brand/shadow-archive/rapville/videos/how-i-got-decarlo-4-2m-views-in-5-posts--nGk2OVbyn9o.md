# How I got DeCarlo 4.2M views in 5 posts
URL: https://youtu.be/nGk2OVbyn9o · Length 7:08 · Views 1,424 · Speaker: Nile (21)

## Core idea
Artists push conversion (streams, Meta ads on dull performance videos) before anyone is hooked on their brand. His three-stage content system is branding -> building momentum (test formats, then double down on data) -> conversion. Case: DeCarlo, branded as the "reverse rapper archetype" with nature as the visual, tested five formats in one shoot.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Common pitfall: focusing on conversion/streaming before people are "addicted" to your music and brand; running Meta ads on boring, low-production performance videos hoping for stragglers (00:28-00:47).
2. Fans need trust and love before they stream (first-date analogy) (00:47-00:58).
3. Three-stage content system: Stage 1 branding (understand the artist and music, research competitors, get creative), Stage 2 building momentum (content strategy + execution), Stage 3 conversion and meaningful growth (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, wherever you send viewers) (01:00-01:44). Later in the video he relabels the steps as stage 1 = content strategy + format testing, stage 2 = data/double down, stage 3 = conversion (02:47-02:53, 05:02, 06:17).
4. Branding is "the DNA behind your content", possibly the most important thing, what makes you unique (01:04-01:15).
5. Use an archetype and define the brand by its opposite: DeCarlo = "reverse rapper archetype" (no women, supercars, flexing money or drugs, since those don't make you rich) (01:48-02:11).
6. Second brand pillar: avoiding distractions (modern social-media world), living a life of peace (02:11-02:20).
7. Find the visual that is the opposite of what the artist rejects; for DeCarlo, nature was the "eureka moment"; don't overcomplicate with abstract concepts, go back to basics (02:20-02:47).
8. Encode the brand subtly in colour: not just green, but saturated greens as a visual sign of the brand (02:57-03:20).
9. Format tested, "clone effect": multiple versions of the artist in one frame; more to look at; on-brand green; lots of depth (~100 m) so it is a familiar concept executed in a way not seen before (03:21-03:48).
10. Format tested, "TV transition": kept the green branding; he says it is very viral and you should try it (03:48-04:02).
11. Format tested, "split screen: setup vs the shot": behind-the-scenes on top, polished colour-graded version below; works best when something about the production is impressive (e.g. brownish greens vs ultra-saturated result) (04:04-04:27).
12. Format tested, "zoom hook": zoom in within the first 1 second; a simple pattern interrupt, easy to shoot on an iPhone (04:27-04:46).
13. Shoot in Log on iPhone to get those colours (details not given) (04:47-04:52).
14. Fifth post: "TV transition V2", repeating a known-working format to "put our chips in the right place" (04:53-05:02).
15. After testing, gather data per format to decide what to double down on at the next shoot (05:02-05:10).
16. Platforms differ: split-screen and zoom hook did better on TikTok than Instagram (05:24-05:43).
17. Next-round decisions from data: repeat clone effect with a slight angle change and a different song; repeat TV transition; park the lowest cross-platform performer (setup vs shot); repeat the zoom hook (easy and performs) (05:48-06:12).
18. Next shoot strategy: the opposite of the nature visual, putting him inside the mess of distractions, disoriented and in pain, to tap audience emotion (06:17-06:50).

## Examples he uses
- Agency: 130M+ views for artists in the last 90 days (claimed) (00:00-00:11).
- DeCarlo: 4.2M views in first 5 posts (claimed). Clone effect 1.2M views; TV transition "millions" (exact unclear); setup-vs-shot weaker on IG, better on TikTok; zoom hook far better on TikTok; last TV transition nearly 500K; a re-done zoom hook got another 150K (claimed) (05:10-06:09).

## Process / checklists
1. Branding session: understand artist + music, define archetype and pillars, find the simple visual (opposite of what the brand rejects), research competitors.
2. One shoot testing ~5 formats (clone effect, TV transition, setup vs shot split screen, zoom hook, TV transition V2), all in brand colour.
3. Review per format and per platform; double down on winners with small variations; park the weakest.
4. Only then focus on conversion.
