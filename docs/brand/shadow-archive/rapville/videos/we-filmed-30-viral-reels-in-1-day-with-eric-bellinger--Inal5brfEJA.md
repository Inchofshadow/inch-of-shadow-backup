## Core idea
A testimonial interview with Eric Bellinger after a full-day content shoot. Almost no tactics; his points are that a batch shoot before a release gives a month of content and that making content that "makes people stick" is a professional job.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Batch content right before a release: he needed lots of content quickly because the song was about to drop; one shoot day gave content for the rest of the month (01:18-01:31).
2. Shoot ran a full day (10:00 a.m. to sunset) (00:34-00:40).
3. On set, trust the plan and focus on "hitting everything right" in your own performance (00:46-01:05).
4. Anyone can switch on a camera and make videos in a day; making videos that make people stick is a different skill, comparable to hiring a songwriter (01:46-02:02).
5. Plans for his brand: more quality in the music and more quantity of presence: content, performance content, short-form music video (02:29-02:52).

## Examples he uses
- Eric Bellinger: shoot day from 10am to sunset, content for the rest of the month (claimed). No numbers given.

## Process / checklists
None beyond "shoot a month of content in one day before the release".
