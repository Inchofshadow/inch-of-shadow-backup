# How I Turn Content Into Streams (Not Views)
URL: https://youtu.be/vPULwokgVqY | Length: 9:35 | Views: 5,445 (uploaded 2026-01-08)

## Core idea (2-3 sentences)
A four-step content system: branding -> testing formats -> doubling down -> conversion, plus two song-trend strategies. Streams are planted from the start: branding lets viewers feel the song's emotion through the visuals, testing is for song response not just views, doubling down strings viral moments together, and conversion uses pinned comment replies and comment-keyword DM automation.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Streams start at branding, not at the conversion step (00:08-00:23).
2. Good branding does two things: makes a viral moment memorable because it is built around the world of your music, and (most important) lets the viewer feel the song's emotions through the visuals; extreme emotions make people switch platforms and stream/save (00:34-01:00).
3. Amplify the song's emotion instead of plain performance reels: for KP's sad track they took a viral video built for high-energy rap (funny intro + energetic performance) and shifted it to an emotional angle, so the viewer is in the right emotional state when the music starts (01:11-02:27).
4. Branding gives you a lens to take a viral video and rebuild it for your brand (02:37-02:43).
5. Format = a repeatable structure, same concept and psychology, different angles/spins; e.g. reaction video in the car with your crush or mum vs at a house party with friends (02:56-03:15).
6. Testing: 30 videos in a month as four formats x seven videos each, so you can see which formats and which individual videos outperform ("shotgun shells", not "spraying in the dark") (03:17-03:38).
7. Test for song response, not only views (avoid "empty virality"): look for comments and DMs saying the track is fire; for big artists the streaming increase shows immediately (03:38-04:03).
8. If many formats all fail (~25 videos), suspect the song, not the formats: switch the track and keep the format (04:05-04:21).
9. Going viral = multiple moments strung together in a congruent, branded way that makes you memorable and respected; one viral video will not change a career (04:30-04:48).
10. A viral video's true value is data on what to push next: analyse what worked, tweak the angle, do it again, relentlessly (04:50-05:12).
11. Conversion via comments: reply to all comments asking for the song with the song name and release date, even if it takes hours (05:18-05:31).
12. Context for Adri's success: very unique micro-genre and a language barrier (Italian) that targeted the right audience; you cannot always rely on viewers begging for the song (05:55-06:12).
13. Conversion strategy 1 (easiest): plant comments from other accounts asking when the song is out / what it is called, reply with the date or name, and pin that comment to the top; people who have seen you two or three times get the final nudge to switch platforms (06:23-06:57).
14. Conversion strategy 2, "attention farming" (more aggressive): tell viewers to comment a specific word (e.g. the song name) and use ManyChat to auto-DM them the song link while they are still hot (06:58-07:23).
15. Making the song itself go viral, method 1, "trend domino effect" (once you have momentum and fans): a contest prompt, e.g. best sunset video using my song wins free tickets to my next show (07:33-07:59).
16. Method 2, "soulless page strategy": start a separate account posting relatable influencer-style content with the song always in the background; can spark a trend and others copy the videos; multiple such accounts possible (07:59-08:31).
17. Patience in the testing phase; content rewards patience (08:58-09:11).
18. Delegation: as a musician you are an entrepreneur; focus on your expertise and delegate the rest (09:11-09:24).
19. Music matters most; he rejects the claim that only attention matters (08:48-08:56).

## Examples he uses (numbers claimed)
- KP: first video before New Year, 250k views, +12% streaming on that song (02:28-02:37).
- Desiree: ~25 videos, none popped; switched track, same format -> 5M views and 10k streams overnight; the planted+pinned comment helped get 10k streams from the post (04:05-04:23, 06:54-06:57).
- Adri: same winning concept with tweaked angles posted 336 times, tens of millions of views; 2k -> 74k monthly listeners, Spotify editorial playlists, song "Easy" 300k streams in six months, EP 1M streams (05:00-05:48).
- J O (name unclear): uses ManyChat flow, "millions of streams" (07:25-07:29).
- Michael Bibi: sunset contest, 3k videos made with the song (07:43-07:59).
- Camille: soulless page grew to 10k followers, multiple 1M+ videos, 3k creators used the song, campaign ended at 200k streams (08:01-08:35).

## Process / checklists
System: 1) Branding (world + emotion of the song; rebuild viral videos through that lens). 2) Testing: 4 formats x 7 videos in a month; judge song response (comments/DMs), not only views; if all fail, switch song. 3) Double down: analyse winner, tweak angle, repeat many times. 4) Conversion: reply with song name/date to all comments; pinned reply comment; comment-keyword ManyChat DM. Extras: trend domino contest; soulless page accounts.
