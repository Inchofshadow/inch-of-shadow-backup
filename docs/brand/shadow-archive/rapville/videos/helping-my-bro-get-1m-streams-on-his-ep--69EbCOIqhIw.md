# helping my bro get 1M streams on his EP
URL: https://youtu.be/69EbCOIqhIw · Length 5:57 · Views 897 · Speaker: Nile (runs the channel's agency; captions say "Rap Film")

## Core idea
Views only build careers if they create fans, so content should chase emotional connection rather than trends. He lays out a four-phase system used on Italian jazz-rap artist Adriano: lead with emotion, manufacture demand, pivot when formats die, build a repeatable system.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Chase emotional connection, not trends; views matter only if they turn into fans (00:17).
2. Being "too unique" is an advantage, especially when most artists still post "song out now, link in bio" and music-video snippets (00:53-01:02).
3. Two problems to solve for a song: (a) geo-target the audience (captions say "Geoargetians", likely "geo-target Italians") through captions and visuals, not hashtags; (b) amplify the song's emotion, because people have to feel it to stream it (01:04-01:19).
4. Borrow a visual world the target audience already recognises: Jack Vettriano paintings (nostalgic, romantic style; he calls him Italian) for an Italian audience (01:19-01:27, 01:55).
5. No filming needed: use the TikTok slideshow (photo carousel) feature (01:30-01:35).
6. Slideshow concept = a story told as a conversation between the man and woman in the painting that leads into the song: slide 1 a teasing line from him, slide 2 her reply about the lyrics, slide 3 the lyrics appear (01:35-01:53).
7. Use the song's emotion to build a storyline rather than just selling the music (01:56-02:00).
8. When a format works, do not celebrate and move on; "double down ruthlessly" (02:18-02:23).
9. Phase 2 "manufacture demand": make many variations of the winning format (more paintings from the archive, plus AI variations of them), all using the same proven song snippet (02:25-02:36).
10. Run this before the song is released, so comments ask "when is this dropping?" (02:36-02:45).
11. Reply to every single comment with the song name and release date; the tedious, unscalable work is where most campaigns die (02:47-02:59).
12. Release only after demand is built (here about 2 weeks into the campaign) (02:59-03:03).
13. "Algorithm change" really means the audience gets numb to a repeated format; the brain seeks novelty (03:14-03:33).
14. Phase 3 "pivot when formats die": don't panic when engagement drops; often a slight tweak is enough (03:36-03:50). Example tweak: swap the Vettriano paintings for new Pinterest photos with a similar vibe (03:51-03:56).
15. Diversify content styles/formats like an investment portfolio, in case one format dies completely (03:56-04:07).
16. Added videos alongside the new slideshow strategy once it popped again (04:10-04:16).
17. Phase 4 "build a repeatable system": volume matters; they posted 263 times (04:36-04:52).
18. The repeatable system restated: lead with emotion -> when it goes viral, manufacture demand with unscalable work -> adapt and pivot when formats die -> repeat; consistency is rewarded over anything else (04:54-05:17).
19. Mindset: it never works the first time; do so many reps that failure becomes unreasonable; artists run out of will, not talent or songs; commit for another two years (05:20-05:53).
