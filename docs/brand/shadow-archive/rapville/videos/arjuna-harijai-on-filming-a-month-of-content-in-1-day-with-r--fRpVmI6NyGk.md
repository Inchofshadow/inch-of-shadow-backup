## Lessons and tactics
1. Self-shooting as a "one-man army" means running back to camera without seeing the frame; a team helps (00:00-00:15, 00:36-00:55).
2. The artist's only job was to give an input/prompt; the agency wrote the scripts (00:21-00:31).
3. Scripts that make you uncomfortable may be the ones that work; an outside push helps you get out of the comfort zone and play characters (02:19-02:39). Framing: being uncomfortable means you are growing (02:53-02:58).
4. Finding the right collaborators takes the most time; once found, collaborate (01:54-02:12).
5. A recurring gag in his content: getting slapped by his wife (said 10-15 times on the shoot day) (00:58-01:04).
