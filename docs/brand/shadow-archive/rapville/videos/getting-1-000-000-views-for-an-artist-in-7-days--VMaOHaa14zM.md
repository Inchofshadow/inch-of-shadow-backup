# Getting 1,000,000 Views for an Artist in 7 Days
URL: https://youtu.be/VMaOHaa14zM | Length: 7:13 | Views: 3,643 (uploaded 2025-05-11)

## Core idea (2-3 sentences)
Adriano, an Italian jazz rap artist stuck at a few hundred views, blames his "too unique" music; the host argues uniqueness is an advantage. He builds a 7-day strategy: define the audience, build content pillars, then a few ideas that mix as many pillars as possible, with the slide post the standout result.

## Lessons and tactics
1. Unique music is not the reason content flops; use it as an advantage (00:07-00:16).
2. Start by listening to the music (00:24).
3. Start the strategy with the audience: who will like this? Here: people who love chill vibes, want to find their next car-playlist artist "that nobody knows about", and live life nonchalantly as if it were a movie/art piece (00:59-01:19).
4. Do not start with ideas (you run out fast); first build content pillars = a theme/style to base all ideas around (01:20-01:41). Pillars for this song: romanticising life; chill, nostalgic vibes; discovering underrated artists (01:41-01:50).
5. Best ideas mix as many pillars as possible, to grab the relevant audience (01:50-01:59).
6. Idea 1: artist in a train station looking nostalgic and nonchalant, lip-syncing, on-screen text "POV: bringing a new sound to Italy"; a "cheat code" because it mixes all three pillars (02:01-02:19).
7. Idea 2, slide post (popping off for very unique sounds in the UK) (02:20-02:29). Do not use your own face when nobody knows you; use a well-known painter from your country whose paintings visually represent the music (02:31-02:45). Structure: slide 1 = man and woman looking at each other romantically, text "Calm down, babe. It's just a chill jazz rapper in Italy"; slide 2 = zoom in on the woman, text "But listen to the lyrics"; slide 3 = an image of you with a grainy old-looking effect, or old pictures of past artists, with your text on top (02:48-03:23). It looks like you are not promoting; the audience feels they found you, which makes them feel special (03:23-03:30).
8. The slide strategy only works for music in a micro-genre; not for generic club music; you must be developing your own genre/path that stands out (03:30-03:47).
9. You never need tons of ideas; aim at the target many times with ideas you are confident in, in slightly different variations (03:49-04:00).
10. Idea 3: nostalgic vibes + night-time city. Artist stands still mid-frame while cars race past: record ~10 minutes of the street, speed it up hugely to 30-60 seconds; record a second clip of the artist lip-syncing; in edit, remove the artist's background and composite over the sped-up street so he moves at normal speed (04:02-04:45).
11. Consistency in posting is part of it (05:01-05:02).
12. You do not need to spend thousands on ads; you need the strategy (06:21-06:27). If you get at least 200 views a video you are not shadowbanned (06:43-06:49).

## Examples he uses (numbers claimed)
- Adriano before: content stuck at 500 views (also said 800 before; under 100 likes), 200 followers (00:09, 00:39, 05:08, 06:13).
- After 7 days: first video 68.2k views, 8.4k likes, 1.8k saves; second video (slide post per context, "laziest content idea ever") 631k views, 89.5k likes, comments asking for the song name; train station 33k views, 4.5k likes; city video 6.5k views, 1.1k likes; followers 200 -> 4.6k (05:27-06:36). Title claims 1M views (not stated as a total in the transcript).

## Process / checklists
1) Listen to the song. 2) Define the audience. 3) Build 3 content pillars. 4) Ideas that combine pillars. 5) Few ideas, several variations each. 6) Post consistently for 7 days, then check results vs prior views.
