# getting an artist 100k streams in 14 days
URL: https://youtu.be/7zpipZe0Wy8 | Length: 7:54 | Views: 431 | Date: 2026-02-28

## Core idea
A case study of RB, a Norway-based Punjabi artist. The team swapped his "selfish, performance-driven" content for entertainment-first skits built from a three-part brand definition (audience, artist, art), then shot them in one day. He credits organic Instagram virality, with no Meta ads, for the jump in monthly listeners.

## Lessons and tactics
1. (01:02) Diagnose the old strategy: content that is too "selfish and performance-driven" (just showing the artist performing). Flip it to entertainment value for the viewer.
2. (01:11) Branding comes before ideas, in three parts: **the audience, the artist, the art**.
3. (01:18) Audience: define the exact market. For RB that means Asian markets plus "global Indians" (diaspora in Canada/US/UK/Norway) who know the language and culture but also live Western lives (listen to Taylor Swift).
4. (01:41) Trend-arbitrage idea: the UK/US pioneer trends, Europe follows closely, Asia lags. Bring Western trends into a market that hasn't seen them yet. (He says the same move worked for an Italian artist with UK slide posts.)
5. (02:04) Artist: find the real backstory and character. RB quit being a lawyer to become an artist ("had everything except the one thing that mattered"). He is a "born entertainer, no limits, no shame". That is not "cringe artist", it means doing whatever it takes to entertain the viewer.
6. (02:33) Derive brand concepts from the artist story, e.g. "struggle to victory".
7. (02:40) Art: name the emotional core of the music (here love songs, heartbreak, some inspiration; authentic Punjabi sound with a modern Western feel) and let it come through in the visuals. He says this "pretty much decides the concepts".
8. (03:00) Keep emotions extreme. Lesson of the day: "avoid no man's land", meaning don't sit in a middling emotional zone.
9. (03:09) Pick concept themes from the brand: struggle to victory, heartbreak, love, existing Western trends.
10. (03:17) Each concept follows the same beat structure: **Curiosity (first second) -> Tension builder -> Transition moment (the hook cuts in) -> Music video section (with a stated energy)**.
11. (03:19-05:26) The five concepts, each in that structure:
    - **Tequila slap**: curiosity = at the bar staring at a tequila shot; tension = does the shot (salt, throw back); transition = barman splashes water and slaps him, snap cut to where he was looking; music video = singing with water dripping, "emotional yet still empowered".
    - **Proposal disaster**: curiosity = gives girlfriend flowers; tension = she throws them back and walks off, he looks shocked; transition = he bends to pick them up, hook cuts as he stands; music video = he re-enters frame from below, heartbroken energy.
    - **Ice skating fail**: curiosity = girlfriend skating; tension = she loses balance; transition = she starts to fall; music video = close-up of him catching her, singing while skating, passionate/in love.
    - **Unexpected turn**: curiosity = filmed in public like a celebrity ("who is this?"); tension = he skips, runs across the street, someone holds up his jacket, he runs to the girl; transition = hook cuts as they hold hands; music video = walking-the-streets montage.
    - **Jumped and beaten**: curiosity = a group kicking someone in the street; tension = kick one, kick two, they lean down to punch; transition = snap cut to a high-quality close cinematic angle of them surrounding him; music video = they scatter, revealing him lying there singing, emotionless performance, slow zoom down for the whole video.
12. (05:29) "Strategy is nothing without execution": all concepts shot in one shoot day, edited by their editors.
13. (06:58-07:31) Things he says go on behind the scenes: shoots run behind schedule, some videos flop that were expected to do well, lots of back-and-forth on transition timing and color grading.

## Examples he uses
- RB (Punjabi, Norway, ex-lawyer): claimed 30,000 monthly listeners while spending thousands of pounds/month on ads; goal 100k; claimed 126,271 monthly listeners 60 days later with no Meta ads, "purely" from Instagram virality.
- Results per reel (claimed, organic, no push): Tequila slap 400k views; Proposal disaster 107k; Ice skating fail 1.1M; Unexpected turn 163k; Jumped and beaten 3.7M. Claimed 5.5M+ views in the first month of content.
- Song "Galamukadi" (spelling as captioned, may be off): claimed jump to 100k streams in 14 days, now claimed 238k streams.
- Adri (Italian artist): UK-style slide posts; claimed passed 1M streams on his EP.

## Process / checklists
- Strategy steps: listen to the music -> brand (audience / artist / art) -> concept themes -> ideas, each in curiosity / tension / transition / music-video form -> one shoot day -> team edits -> post and track.
