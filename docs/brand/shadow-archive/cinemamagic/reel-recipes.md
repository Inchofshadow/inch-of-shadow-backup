# Six reel recipes from @cinema.magic (R-009)

Picked by Amir 2026-10-13 ("all"). Each recipe takes a technique from a film scene he reposted and turns it into a reel Amir can shoot himself.

These are recipes, not plans: which song, which location and when are decided per release. They follow the brand rules:
- 9:16 at 1080x1920;
- text stays inside the safe zone (270px top, 672px bottom, 65px sides) and never on his face;
- sunglasses on is the normal state;
- the studio is a mixing room, with no instruments in shot;
- the title font comes from `frame.md`, not from the film.

## 1. Vertical triptych

*From: The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (three faces, then three hands, 795K views); Three Colours (blue, white, red strips, 41.9M views, 7s).*

- **Frame:** three horizontal strips stacked in the 9:16 frame, each about 1080x560, with thin black gaps of 8 to 12px. Each strip is a 16:9 or 2.39:1 shot cropped to a band.
- **Shots, version A (one moment, three views), all filmed at the same moment:**
  1. top: his face with sunglasses, a tight close-up;
  2. middle: hands on the mixing desk or the mic;
  3. bottom: a wide shot of the place.
  
  Cut all three strips together on the beat, from faces to hands to the wide shot, as Leone does.
- **Shots, version B (colour):** the same shot three times in three grades, taken from the release's colour mood (frame.md). Or three locations, one colour each.
- **Camera:** horizontal, locked on a tripod, eye level for the face, a high angle for the hands, low and wide for the place.
- **Light:** the same light in all three strips, so they read as one moment.
- **Length:** 7 to 15 seconds. The 41.9M reel is 7 seconds.
- **Edit:** HyperFrames, three video layers in strips. The strips change at the same time, on the downbeat.
- **Text:** none, or one short line in the black above the top strip.

## 2. One shot, held

*From: Once Upon a Time in America, the Manhattan Bridge street (1.3M views, 8s, no cut).*

- **Frame:** a symmetrical wide with strong verticals on both sides (buildings, trees, a gate) and a vanishing point in the middle. He is small in the frame, walking in from the side or toward the camera.
- **Where:** Tempelhofer Feld (horizon and sky), the cemetery path between trees, a long Neukölln street with the streetlights in a row.
- **Camera:** locked on a tripod, never moves. Lens about 35 to 50mm equivalent, from far away, at eye or chest height.
- **Light:** flat, hazy daylight or blue hour. The bridge shot is soft and warm, with steam. Mist or a smoke machine helps if the place allows it.
- **Length:** 8 to 12 seconds, no cut. The song carries it, so pick its strongest 8 bars. At 100 to 120 BPM, 8 bars is about 16 to 19 seconds; 4 bars is about 8 to 10.
- **Edit:** none. Only the grade.
- **Text:** none in the frame. The caption carries it.

## 3. Night drive in widescreen

*From: Drive, the opening credits (647K views; letterboxed with one line of text on top).*

- **Frame:** a 2.39:1 band across the middle of the black 9:16 frame, with one line of plain white text above the band. The text sits inside the safe zone and never on the picture.
- **Shots, in Drive's order:**
  1. the city from above at night (a high window or a rooftop), held, with the title appearing on it;
  2. his profile at the wheel at night, from the passenger seat, sunglasses on;
  3. the road ahead through the windscreen, with streetlights as soft blurred lights;
  4. a close-up of hands on the wheel.
- **Camera:** horizontal; shoot wide open (low f-number) for the blurred lights. Rest it on the dashboard or the headrest, not handheld.
- **Light:** only the street. The streetlights passing over his face are the effect. No interior light.
- **Length:** 15 to 30 seconds, slow cuts of 3 to 6 seconds.
- **Edit:** HyperFrames, letterbox mask, slow fades. The title is set in the release's title font; Drive's pink script is the idea of "a title that feels like the song", not the font to copy.
- **Text:** one line above the band. The song title can appear on the city shot, small, lower third of the band.

## 4. Close-up in rain light

*From: Blade Runner, Tears in Rain (418K views; one close-up, wet, cold light).*

- **Frame:** a tight close-up, face and shoulders, slightly off centre. Behind him a blurred bright light: a neon sign, a shop window, a screen.
- **Rain:** a spray bottle or a garden hose in a light mist, aimed between him and the backlight, so the drops catch the light. Wet hair and face.
- **Light:**
  - a cold cyan or blue backlight from behind and to one side, for the rim on the wet skin;
  - a weak warm fill or none;
  - the background light out of focus.
- **Camera:** locked, eye level or slightly below. Shoot 50 to 85mm equivalent for a soft background, wide open.
- **Performance:** he sings or mouths one line, slowly. This works with sunglasses too: the rain on the lenses catches the backlight.
- **Length:** 10 to 20 seconds, one or two cuts at most.
- **Text:** one line in the black above, or none.

## 5. Macro of hands at work

*From: Goodfellas, garlic sliced with a razor (1.8M views; extreme close-ups, one face cut).*

- **Frame:** an extreme close-up of one precise studio move:
  - a fader pushed up slowly;
  - a patch cable going in;
  - a knob turned a few degrees;
  - a finger on a key of the mixing desk.
- **The cut:** 3 to 4 macro shots, then one slow shot of his face (eyes closed or sunglasses, listening), then back to the hands. That is the Goodfellas structure: hands, face, hands.
- **Camera:** as close as the lens focuses, on a tripod or resting on the desk. A phone in macro mode works. Shoot vertical for this one.
- **Light:** one hard light low from the side, so every ridge on the fader cap and the skin shows; a dark background. Warm.
- **Sound:** the song, with the real click of the fader or the plug kept in, mixed under the music.
- **Length:** 15 to 22 seconds, a cut every 2 to 4 seconds.
- **Fits:** "post your working, not your work" (memory: process over product). The studio is the Ambertone mixing room; no instruments.

## 6. Still in the chaos

*From: Forrest Gump, Lt. Dan at New Year (4.1M views; one static shot, he does not move while the party falls around him).*

- **Frame:** a medium shot, him at the centre, completely still, looking into the lens or past it. Around him and in front of him, things move:
  - confetti falling;
  - a crowd passing;
  - smoke;
  - traffic lights streaking (with a longer shutter).
- **Where:** a party or a club (with permission), a busy Neukölln crossing at night, a station.
- **Camera:** locked on a tripod, eye level, 35 to 50mm equivalent.
- **Light:** the scene's own light, colourful and messy around him, with him a little darker or a little cooler than the crowd.
- **Length:** 8 to 13 seconds, one shot. The 4.1M reel is 13 seconds and square; he lets it run.
- **Text:** one line or the song's line as a subtitle in the black under the picture, like the film's subtitle in the original.

## Things that hold for all six

- He picks one moment and holds it. No flicker, no effect stack; the film's own sound (here, the song) carries it.
- One line of plain text outside the picture is the most text these get.
- Each recipe is one idea. If one gets used for a release, its song, place and date go in that release's plan, not here.
