What it is: eight @bywaviboy reels that rebuild a photo in another material, era, machine or print medium, with the tools and settings each one names.
Read 2026-10-12 from local files only (captions, transcripts, frames). Part of Shadow Archive R-006.

Notes:
- Prompts are her work. They are described here by purpose, structure and named settings, never copied. Open the post link at the slide or second given to read the original.
- The on-camera presenter differs between reels (beanie and white glasses in the CRT and VIBE reels, a blond man in black glasses in the print reel, a man in a beanie holding a boot in the shoe reel). "She" means the account, as Amir uses it.

## 4. CRT screen with ChatGPT and Kling

- Link: https://www.instagram.com/p/DbQgTWlIfTW/
- Date: 2026-07-26 (205 likes)
- What it is: a 25.6s phone screen recording with subtitles. Opens on a before and after split (a logo on a plate, a portrait, a globe icon), then the ChatGPT steps, the Kling steps, the animated result, and bonus cards with the portrait inside a TV in a dark room.
- The effect, her rule in my words: ChatGPT rebuilds the whole picture from phosphor dots and scanlines instead of putting a filter on top.
- Quote: "Those three settings are the difference between a CRT and a cheap VHS overlay."
- Pipeline:
  1. ChatGPT image edit: upload any image, paste prompt 1. Screen shows model "5.6 Medium" and "Create image".
  2. Kling 3, image to video: AI Video, Frames tab, start frame = the CRT image, end frame optional, VIDEO 3.0.
  3. Kling settings (caption): Quality 4K, duration 6 seconds, multi-shot off. The screen shows the 4K "Cinematic Quality" option, a duration slider at 7s and Smart Multi-Shot off.
- Prompt 1, ChatGPT image (partly legible; read it at 11.4s and 12.3s in the reel):
  - For: baking the CRT texture into the photo itself.
  - Structure: look and feel / texture source / negatives.
  - Ingredients: harsh, high contrast, dense, tactile; close-up of a broken colour terminal; damaged screen-printed halftone made of CRT phosphors; the raster texture must build the whole image.
  - Negatives it names: no TV or monitor frame, no rounded screen, no vignette, no visible hardware, not a clean green night-vision look, no smooth skin or realistic shading.
- Prompt 2, Kling motion (legible across 19.0s and 20.6s; the end is cut off at the Mood line):
  - For: revealing that the image is playing on an old TV.
  - Structure: start frame / camera move / subject / signal faults / pace and end frame / mood.
  - Ingredients: extreme close-up filled with phosphor dots, scanlines and signal noise; yellow-green highlights, cyan contamination, red and blue pixel speckle, slight horizontal tearing, rolling analogue interference; slow controlled zoom out to a vintage TV in a dark room with curved glass, rounded corners, charcoal plastic casing, dust, light scratches, faint reflections, glow spilling out; face stays recognisable, near still, a slow blink at most; flicker, split-second signal loss, static, tracking wobble, brightness pulse, colour fringing; last frame is the whole TV alone in the dark; mood of an eerie intercepted broadcast, not a clean modern screen effect.
- Bonus: the same two prompts on a product shot make a brand asset.
- Technique worth keeping: split the job. The image step bakes in the texture and forbids the TV frame; the video step reveals the TV with a pull-back. Telling the image model what not to add is half the prompt.

## 5. Kill the polish (texture)

- Link: https://www.instagram.com/p/DbGkmpvIkDR/
- Date: 2026-07-22 (843 likes)
- What it is: a 28.8s talking head. The presenter is cut out over grainy editorial images, with red-highlighted subtitles. Opens on a "how to make AI images with taste" title, then a "do not" example (clean skin close-up) against "do" examples (grittier editorial portraits). Shows Midjourney parameters, Magnific panels and a node compositor with a dust layer.
- Quote: "Imperfection is the new luxury."
- The five steps, in my words:
  1. Never write "cinematic". Name a real camera, lens and film behaviour instead: 35mm grain, halation, ARRI Alexa.
  2. In Midjourney: chaos 10, ar 4:3, style raw. Other phrases on screen: atmospheric haze, diffused softbox.
  3. Never add grain in post; added noise always reads fake. The screen shows a Noise and Grain effect as the thing to avoid.
  4. Use a generative upscaler instead: Magnific, Sharpen 28, Smart grain 17. Screen: Magic spell menu (Magnific Upscaler, Mystic v3, Skin Enhancer, Relight), Version v1 (flexible), Optimized for Enhance everything, Sharpen 28%, Smart grain 17%.
  5. Lay a 35mm dust mask on top in Screen blend mode at 100% opacity.
- Tools: Midjourney (text to image), Magnific (generative upscale), an unnamed node-based compositor for the dust layer.
- Prompts: only the fragments above. The full texture pack is behind the comment VIBE.
- Technique worth keeping: bake texture in through the upscaler's grain, never post noise, and name lens and light instead of a mood word.

## 6. Dreamcore halation

- Link: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd6gWR6HHm-/
- Date: 2026-09-30 (3 likes)
- What it is: a 39.3s sponsored tutorial for invideo (tag and discount code). Opens on a backlit smoker in a dim room with a Dreamcore title, then a screen recording of the invideo editor and its agent, with results on a howling dog, an octopus and the smoker. End card asks for the comment INVIDEO.
- The effect, her rule in my words: the nostalgic cue is halation, the soft red glow film makes around bright lights, not grain. Clean highlights are what give AI clips away.
- Quote: "The glow needs bright edges against shadow to show up."
- Tool: invideo editor and its agent (video, not stills). Steps: drop the clip in, open the agent, ask it to apply halation; it builds a custom shader per shot rather than a preset; then fine-tune with sliders.
- Settings (caption): Highlight threshold 0.9, Halation 0.6, Bloom low, Scatter 1.2. On screen the effect is "Film Halation and Bloom": Highlight Threshold 0.89, Halation Intensity 0.62, Bloom Intensity 0.29, Scatter Radius 1.20 (an earlier state shows 0.80 and 0.35).
- Best sources: backlight, practical lamps, smoke, dark backgrounds.
- Uses she lists: music videos and visualisers, night fashion and product shots, making AI clips look less AI, one look across brand b-roll.
- Technique worth keeping: halation, not grain, is the nostalgia signal. Pick or shoot frames with bright edges on black so the glow has something to catch.

## What repeats across the 8

- Not a filter: the look must build the image (eras, scans, CRT; the halftone prompt rules out an overlay).
- Lock everything but one thing: material and scan reels keep pose, framing and background and change one property, shown with a sliding wipe.
- Process words beat style words: cast, machined, moulded, woven, carved, folded, tooled, grown; deposited metal, engineered knit; real lens and light names instead of "cinematic".
- Effects that recur: CCTV (eras, scans), Night Vision / Phosphor (eras, scans, and named as a negative in the CRT prompt), Datamosh (eras, print), Wireframe / Mesh (eras, scans), ASCII (eras, print), halation (texture reel, dreamcore reel).
- Analogue flaws as luxury: dust mask on Screen, Magnific smart grain, halation, CRT signal loss, xerographic decay.
- Motion pipeline: image edit first (Nano Banana Pro or ChatGPT), then image to video (Kling 3) with a camera move that reveals the medium.
- Mostly image edit, not text to image: stated for materials, implied for eras, CRT and print.
- Every list reel gates its prompts behind a comment word (TASTE, MOOD, SCAN, CRT, VIBE, PRINT, SHOE, INVIDEO); only the CRT and print reels show real prompt text.
