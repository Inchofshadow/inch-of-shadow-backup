# R-003 · Creator · @fancienton (Lightroom colour grading)

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/fancienton/ (photographer, 90.2K followers, 181 posts). Amir saved his Gothic reel and thinks he may be better than @alexandergrades. Read 2026-10-11: all 180 posts listed in a picker, Amir picked 30, each read frame by frame.
- **Recipes:** all 30 in [recipes/fancienton.md](../recipes/fancienton.md) (index + full settings). Two different Gothic reels are in there: "Gothic (the one Amir saved)" and "Gothic style".
- **What it is:** the same "How to make photos look like [film / series / game / vibe]" format, but wider: films and series (Twin Peaks, The Dark Knight, Interstellar, Joker, Sherlock), moods (Melancholy, Dreamy, Heavenly, Cozy, Romantic), eras and film stocks (80s, Vintage, Old Vibe, Polaroid). Panels shown small in the centre over a blurred still, about 0.5s each, then before/after.
- **How he differs from @alexandergrades (seen across the 30):**
  - He grades **each colour channel's curve** (red, green, blue), not only the RGB curve.
  - **Faded ends:** the RGB curve often lifts the black point and lowers the white point, which gives the matte, filmic look.
  - **Flatten first, then shape:** big highlights-down / shadows-up moves (Gothic: highlights -75, shadows +77), then contrast back through the curve.
  - **Dehaze goes negative** for haze and softness (Heavenly -72, Gothic -35) instead of always positive.
  - Finishes with **grain, sharpening and noise reduction** on most looks, and sometimes a 4th "Global" grading wheel.
  - Some looks share one base preset (80s and Yellowstone have many identical values).
- **Tip:** for a soft film feel, lift the bottom-left point of the RGB curve a little (blacks never reach pure black) and pull the top-right down slightly.
