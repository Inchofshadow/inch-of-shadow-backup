## What is in it (measured on the files)

1. **JVculture part.1 (CHR003, PRZ & Rapha, acid EP for the Roland JV and the TB-303).**
   - Warm grey paper (~#E5E1DD), mustard ochre ink (~#E0C185 to #D9A630), black line, one red accent (the blood). Three inks on paper, like a screen print.
   - A big figure drawn in loose black line, wrapped in a burst of ochre brush rays.
   - **Small engraved elements scattered around it:** ~16 small eight-point stars, two daggers, a bat, a cracked egg. Each is a separate little stamp in thick-thin black line, never touching the main figure. This is the "engraving" he likes.
   - **The acid sun:** a small smiley in a circle at the top, inside ~70 uneven single-stroke rays with broken dashes in the outer ring. The acid-house smiley turned into an old engraved sun.
   - Type: "JVculture" huge, bold grotesk, rotated 90 degrees down the left edge; "château royal" in a blackletter stamp at the bottom right.
2. **Blue Box (CHR001) and Room 353 (CHR002).** Black sleeves with framed engraved panels: a woodcut-style saint over a crocodile (Room 353), a lion and pump-sun strip (Blue Box). Red, orange, cream and black. Same engraving hand, set inside strict boxes.
3. **The Fox.** The same animal drawn about seven times: scratchy pen hatching (long thin legs, fur as nervous strokes), solid ink silhouette, watercolour wash, crayon, psychedelic multicolour, a cartoon with a hat and rifle. Long limbs, bent curious poses. Mixed media, Bezalel second year.
4. **Bristol Riots.** Three postcards after a mural by Scott Buchanan Barden.
   - Faceless figures: dark flat bodies, one white eye-slit, no features.
   - Soft sprayed gradients and grain on a perspective grid floor, fire as orange spikes.
   - One colour world per plate: amber and brown (1831), grey-violet (1980), deep violet ~#1C1339 to #482892 with orange fire (2011). The colour clash is complementary (violet vs orange).
5. **I Was a Bastard, In Your Great Heart EP.** Black and white op-art: a small solid heart inside about 45 thin concentric heart outlines (23 crossed on half a row at 1000 px). Outline (stroke-only) serif capitals, small sans tracklist. The back is a ring of concentric lines.
6. **Toyota Celica.** An anime-style ad, Bezalel third year. Flat cel colours, hard shadows, deep red on near-black, orange sparks, a light tunnel, green rain streaks, a white explosion on a road.

## Follow-up 2 (Amir, 2026-10-14, in chat)

- **The sun, in his words:** he loves how Eli paints the sun as the acid: the smiley stands for acid house and the TB-303 in pop culture, and he mixed the engraving idea into it very well. That is what he wants for our sun and moon: complete, almost like Eli's.
- **The three foxes he really likes** (named in Effects, family Illustration styles):
  1. **Ink Silhouette, Scratched Light** (`il-fox-ink-scratch`): the black stalking fox, solid brush and ink with a few white scratched lines.
  2. **Red Wash, Calligraphic Line** (`il-fox-red-wash`): the vermilion wash fox with black brush strokes on the contours, sumi-e feel.
  3. **Nervous Pen Feathering** (`il-fox-nervous-pen`): the crowned fox drawn only with thin nervous pen strokes.
- **Half the Night:** two directions only for now: (A) the black swan only; (B) the swan with the sun and the moon. The swan can be coloured, purple, a bit psychedelic, acid.
- **Process rule he set:** check our own archives before taking elements from the web.
- **What matters most in the Chateau Royal covers (his words, 10-14):** how he moves the small elements around the surface of the art, only the small elements, and still the focus never leaves the main thing in the picture. Design terms for it: **scale hierarchy** (one big subject, everything else many times smaller), **peripheral scatter** (the small motifs live at the edges and in the empty space, never touching or crossing the subject), and **one weight for all the small ones** (same line, same ink, so they read as one quiet layer). Measured on JVculture: ~16 stars, 2 daggers, a bat, an egg, each under ~1/12 of the cover width, none overlapping the figure.
