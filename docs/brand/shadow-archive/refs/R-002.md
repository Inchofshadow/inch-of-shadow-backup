### The method (same order in every reel)
1. **Light:** exposure down (-0.2 to -0.65), contrast up (+24 to +35), highlights down (-25 to -38), blacks down hard (-32 to -38), whites a little up (+12 to +14).
2. **Tone curve:** gentle S-curve: the shadows are pushed down (Night City 32 -> 16, 64 -> 43) and the brightest white is pulled just under full (255 -> 250), so lights never clip to pure white.
3. **White balance / presence:** usually left alone; the colour comes from steps 4-5. Vibrance does the work, saturation is often slightly negative.
4. **HSL, all 8 colours:** the core trick. Kill the colours that don't belong (yellow, green, orange: saturation -35 to -55, luminance down) and push the 1-2 colours of the look.
5. **Color grading wheels:** shadows, midtones and highlights get one family of hues (e.g. teal for Tokyo Ghoul, blue for Night City, violet for Edgerunners). Extreme version: Berserk Eclipse mutes every colour except red (red saturation +100, midtones wheel 8/62) and curves the red channel up, green and blue down. Highlights can get the opposite hue for contrast (Night City 322 pink highlights on blue shadows).
6. **Effects:** small texture/clarity, dehaze +6 to +15, vignette -10 to -20, grain 12-20 on the moodier looks.
7. **Optional mask:** Edgerunners adds a light mask (exposure +0.40, highlights +30, whites +34) to make the neon glow.

### Recipes

All 55 recipes are in [recipes/alexandergrades.md](../recipes/alexandergrades.md): an index with one line per look, then the full settings per reel. Amir named Harry Potter (Prisoner of Azkaban), The Odyssey and Berserk (Eclipse) as ones he liked.

- **Techniques:**
  - A look is mostly subtraction: mute every colour that isn't part of the palette, then let 1-2 colours carry it.
  - One hue family across all three grading wheels makes the image read as one colour world; a small opposite hue in the highlights keeps it from going flat.
  - The before/after reveal is the payoff; the settings flash fast, so people save the reel to pause it later (his caption on JoJo: "Save this for your next edit").
  - Series name + franchise logo + "what should I grade next?" = a format that writes its own next episode from the comments.
- **Tip:** for night photos, lower the exposure and blacks first, then add colour. The lights stay bright and the dark parts take the grade.
