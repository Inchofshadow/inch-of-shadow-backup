## What is in the picture (Claude's read)

- **Two exposures in one frame.** Ghost edges sit around the roses (a second, slightly shifted contour), and in the light areas another picture shows through: a red-salmon leafy pattern top left, streaks of a different flower lower right. Light parts of both frames add up, dark parts stay see-through. That is how a film double exposure behaves.
- **Soft everywhere.** No sharp edge in the frame (fine-detail strength 6.5, very low). Some petals smear, as if the camera moved a little during one exposure.
- **Milky, never black.** Luminance percentiles 5/25/50/75/95 = 98/144/190/216/232 (0-255). The darkest spot is mid-grey: lifted blacks, low contrast, bright and airy.
- **Pastel and muted.** Median saturation 0.17 (p90 0.33). Palette (shares of the frame): cream #F0E5DC 14, sage grey-green #646D65 14, dusty rose #D7AC9C 14, blush #EAD9CC 14, pink #EACABE 12, pale sage #99A291 11, khaki cream #CAC7B2 11, mauve brown #9E796D 10. One saturated accent: the red-orange flower at the bottom.
- **Surface.** Fine scratches and dust lines, soft film grain, a faint overall haze.
- **Type.** Two lines of small white serif capitals, centered near the top ("CRAFT SPELLS" over a thin rule, "IDLE LABOR"), about a fifth of the cover wide. The flowers carry the cover; the type is quiet.

## Technique names (to use in briefs)

- **Fact from the image:** multiple exposure / double exposure look, soft focus, lifted blacks, desaturated pastel palette, film scratches.
- **Guess, not known:** made in camera on colour film (two frames on one negative, maybe expired or overexposed stock), possibly with a diffusion filter or a soft lens. It could also be two scans sandwiched in the darkroom or in software; the picture alone does not say which. Photographer not looked up.

## How we could make it (no AI)

- **Camera:** film camera or a digital camera with multiple-exposure mode; two frames of the same subject, shift or refocus slightly between them (or a second subject or texture); each frame about one stop under so the sum is right; diffusion filter or soft lens.
- **Local edit:** two photos blended in Screen or Lighten at 40-60%, second one slightly offset or scaled; a blurred copy screened in at ~30% for the glow; blacks lifted to ~95/255; saturation down ~30%; warm pink highlights, grey-green shadows; fine grain, dust and scratches. Nothing built yet; S7 Pictorialism (soft focus glow) is the nearest existing look.
