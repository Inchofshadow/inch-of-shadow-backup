### The video: 30 reels in 1 day (read from the full transcript)
1. **Mindset / branding first.** "Rehearse in the mirror": decide who you are on camera, what viral performances look like, your unique angle, what to wear. The worst thing is looking like you are faking it.
2. **Pre-production is the big lever.** Content strategy built from three things side by side: the music, a branding document, and a **viral video bank**. Never copy an idea; strip it to its bare skeleton (e.g. "normal situation, unexpected turn, he falls, boom, music") and rebuild it around your song's story.
3. **15 ideas x 2 songs = 30 reels.** Shoot every idea twice, once per song (two songs with a similar vibe).
4. **Videographer:** pick someone who knows short-form, not a music-video specialist. Check camera, lens and portfolio. Brief them in depth (he sends a 15-minute video walkthrough), then one pre-production call about getting the best shots and planning the route.
5. **Locations close together** (or one studio), so a 6-8 hour run-and-gun day is not lost to travel.
6. **Shoot day:** song timestamps on a phone plus a speaker. The first 45 minutes tell how the day will go; if the artist or the clips are off, call it and reshoot another day. The artist leads the pace and energy.
7. **Post:** good editing comes from good strategy and clips. The subtle things count: transition timing, lyric font and colour, colour grade. Review every edit.
8. **Posting:** hashtags don't matter; caption = the first honest thing that comes to mind; batch-made, so never miss a day.
