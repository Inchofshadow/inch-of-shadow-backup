## Study (Claude's read, measured on the 3000 px file scaled to 600, 2026-11-04)

- **One hue for everything.** Median hue 235 (blue-violet), median saturation 0.34: not grey, not saturated. Nothing leaves that hue.
- **Light and airy, not a dark picture.** Luminance percentiles 5/25/50/75/95 = 12/67/123/154/197 (0-255). The big field (the wall) sits mid-light: medians about #777CB2 top, #656999 centre.
- **Darks are lines, not fields.** The darkest tones are the thin bare branches crossing the whole picture and one heavy corner (bottom left, median #17193A) plus the chair. Darkness frames the bottom; it never fills the sky.
- **One lightest subject.** The figure's hair and coat are the brightest area (median #A0A4D3, highlights near #B4C2F8+). Light also glows top left.
- **Light falls off downward and to the left.** Row means top to bottom 124, 125, 129, 115, 100, 79; columns left to right 100, 101, 93, 105, 128, 147. Soft vignette at the bottom.
- **Texture everywhere on the big field.** Crumpled paper / rock surface; fine-texture strength 24.8 (high-pass std), far from a flat graphic. Edges soft, a light dreamy glow, no film grain.
- **What this means for a flat noir cover:** mapping our black night straight into the ramp keeps the night dark and loses the reference's main quality (a light, textured, airy field with dark lines in it). The faithful move is by role: sky = the light textured field, branches/trees = the dark lines, one subject (the moon) = the lightest light with glow, ground = the dark frame at the bottom.
