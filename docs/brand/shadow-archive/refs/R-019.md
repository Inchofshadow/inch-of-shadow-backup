## Design and effects (names for briefs)

What makes it read as "modern witch house" here, measured on the reels and grid:

- **Found and own footage, never studio-shot.** Phone video of beaches, cars, flowers, streets at night, his room. Low resolution is kept, never "cleaned".
- **Datamosh as a fill, not a moment.** In C46BgjjJy4g (8.9K views, flowers) every frame is moshed for the whole 15.6 s; hard cuts only at 8.0, 8.3, 8.5, 11.8 s. Colour pushed to cyan sky + orange / magenta flowers. Existing record: Datamosh (eras-datamosh).
- **Blown-out silhouette:** sky clipped to pure white, the figure a violet silhouette with horizontal scanline banding through it (EP4 reel). New record kodam-blown-out-silhouette.
- **Pixel-sort streaks:** horizontal smear of a sunset or clouds, edges dragged into stripes (C34IqkAL64k opening, also grid). New record kodam-pixel-sort-streaks.
- **Chromatic split cutout:** a flower or object isolated on black with a red/cyan RGB fringe, over a huge glowing moon or planet arc. New record kodam-chromatic-split-cutout.
- **Sticker cutout collage:** a sharp cut-out flower (gladiolus) pasted on top of a moving glitch background, not blended. New record kodam-sticker-cutout-collage.
- **Glitch frame inset:** a clean small video (view from a car window) placed as a box inside a full-frame datamosh. New record kodam-glitch-frame-inset.
- **False colour on nature:** pink rock + lime river, inverted trees (old era, still in the DNA). Existing records: Infrared False Colour, Solarised Film Negative.
- **Night flash + motion blur on faces:** the joint-album cover is a girl in a pink dress under cherry blossom at night, direct flash, her face erased by blur and smoke. Existing records: Digicam Flash, Ghost Form.
- **Monochrome dream footage:** C41D-9CyIEw (single "n'est-ce pas", 6.1K views) = soft grey-blue footage of a woman, horses and a stag on a beach, long hair, white flash frames between shots. Its soft, morphing look suggests AI-generated or heavily smoothed video (Claude's read, not confirmed). 1.7 cuts per second.
- **Palette:** no fixed palette, but three recurring sets: (1) violet + white + black (EP4), (2) cyan + orange + magenta glitch, (3) grey-blue monochrome. The grid stays coherent because every image is soft, low-res and slightly off.
