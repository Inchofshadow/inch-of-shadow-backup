Tool and Puscifer posters, with notes

For looking only. Pictures load from image search. Say what you want by number or in your own words.

Tool, Lateralus era 2001-02

1The body as a machine
Look at the head: the skull is opened and gears and pipes sit where the brain should be. Two inks only (rust red and cyan) on cream paper, with sun rays behind. The name runs DOWN the left side in tall stacked letters.
2001 Berlin, Highway Press
3The body opened up
The spine is drawn running up into the back of an orange head, as if you see through the skin. One flat colour block and a plain background. Simple idea, one image.
2001-02 signed screen print
13Walls of eyes
Behind the figure, rows of eyes fill the whole background and fade into a tunnel. The figure in the middle glows white. This is the Lateralus world in poster form.
PosterScene silkscreen
10Face into face, loud inks
Two heads pressed together, one inside the other. Look at the colour: red, yellow, green, black, no gradients. Halftone and flat ink, screen-print feel.
w/ Meshuggah 2001
9Halftone grain
The face is made of big purple and blue halftone dots. Up close it is only dots; from far it is a face. Name in huge orange condensed letters.
2001 first show

Tool, 10,000 Days era 2006-07

34A holy image with one twist
A classic red Sacred Heart Jesus with a gold halo, and behind him a crowd of riot police in grey. The meaning comes from the collision of the two.
2007 Las Vegas "Jesus"
38An old photo, reprinted
Aleister Crowley in black and white, hands pressed to his face, on pale grey paper. Almost nothing added. A found portrait becomes a poster.
first Tool concert poster
30Statue and sun
A stone angel at the bottom, a gold sun disc at the top, long black spikes in between, on cold grey-blue. Very restrained, lots of empty paper. A tall narrow format.
2006 Upper Darby, Todd Slater
24The X-ray skull
A skull in profile with the brain and machine parts visible, pale green line work on black. Hand-drawn letters at the top and bottom.
2006 Berlin, Emek

Tool, Fear Inoculum era 2019-24

59One figure, lit like a painting
A horned being in the dark, a glowing heart in the chest. Dark background, the light falls only on the figure. The rainbow flashes are the foil layer catching light.
2022 Hamburg, Adi Granov
69Many hands
A pale woman in a white robe with several hands, each making a gesture. Classical and calm, with only the hands strange.
2022 Tulsa, Alejandro Mirabel
73The surreal scene
A cathedral of bones rising from the sea, with hands coming up out of the water. Look at the frame: TOOL in the same tall serif at the top, and one line of date, venue and city at the bottom. Every poster in this era keeps that frame.
2022 Chicago, Alex Reisfar
63Skull in geometry
A pale skull inside a wireframe sphere. One object, centred, and nothing else.
2022 Newark, Eliza Ivanova
57Colour as energy
A figure made of red and blue flames and ribbons on a brown ground. Two strong colours that fight each other.
2019 Zurich
47The exception: modern and flat
Berlin 2019. No figure at all: overlapping circles in warm gradients, a white dot and a black dot, the name in clean white sans. Swiss poster design, very different from the rest.
2019 Berlin (Behance)

Puscifer, without the characters and the logo

88A lone figure in a flat landscape
A man in a black suit walks out of the desert toward you, his head on fire. Flat colours, lilac sky, cacti, a lot of empty space, and the name small at the bottom. Like a film poster.
Vegas, Ken Taylor
96A melting landscape
A pink and purple world melting like wax, with the letters lying in it as if they fell. Surreal, soft, a little funny.
2023 Bethlehem, Jim Mazza
105A dream city
Towers shaped like mushrooms under a crescent moon, in deep blue and green. A whole world in one picture.
Mashantucket 24, Jones
117A classical motif, redrawn
Medusa in teal with snakes for hair, a lace mask, red lips and a geometric cube. An old myth in new colours.
Brooklyn 22, Emek
112A proverb in flames
Three monkeys (see no, hear no, speak no evil) sitting on an old TV, with fire behind. An old symbol updated with one object.
2026 main show print (Gigart)
90Psychedelic ornament
Every millimetre is filled: mirrored, symmetrical, melting 60s letters at the top. Maximum detail.
2022 Baton Rouge, Juan Ma Orozco
