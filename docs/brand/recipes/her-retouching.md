one.

## What her retouching is, and is not

- **It is the jaw and neck line, nothing else.** Every area on him sits under the jaw or on
  the neck. The fold goes; the jawline reads clean.
- **It is rare.** 4 photos of 204. Only where the pose made it show: head tilted down, shot
  from below or from the side.
- **The beard, the pores and the grain stay.** The fill keeps the stubble pattern and the
  skin texture. Nothing looks smoothed.
- **No skin smoothing anywhere.** Texture is 0 or positive on the faces, no negative clarity,
  no masks on skin. One photo in 204 has any local mask at all.
- **No face reshaping, no eyes, no teeth, no body.** Hair was never worked on; on -107 a few
  strands were redrawn only because a door handle behind them was removed.
- The other 14 AI fills are background cleanup: a door handle, litter, a bag, a shoe, people.

So the rule to learn is restraint: fix the one thing the pose caused, keep every texture,
touch nothing else.

## How it could be done later, and what needs his word

His standing rule for Claude's tools is **no AI on his face** (`machine.md`). Her work WAS
generative AI on his jaw and neck, done with him sitting next to her. That is his to allow,
per image; Claude never decides it.

1. **Lightroom, by Amir himself** (if he buys it): the same Remove tool she used. Closest to
   her result, he holds the brush, nothing leaves his account. Her saved settings files show
   the area sizes and brush work to imitate.
2. **Local, no AI:** a shadow-and-shape correction under the jaw (darken and blend the fold
   into the neck shadow). Honest limit: this hides a soft fold in shadow, it cannot redraw a
   jawline the way a generative fill does. Worth a test on one raw before promising anything.
3. **A generative edit through an AI image tool:** only with his explicit go for that one
   image, paid, and it sends his face to an outside service. Last resort.

Whichever route: work on a COPY, show him raw, result and her reference side by side at full
zoom, and he approves before it is used.
