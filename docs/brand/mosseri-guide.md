# Instagram guide: what Mosseri said (Oct 2025 to Oct 2026)

**When to read:** before every post, every post plan, and every campaign plan (Amir, 2026-11-05).
It works alongside `reel-craft.md`, our reel rulebook. If the two disagree, his own numbers in `reel-craft.md` section 1 win.

**Source:** Adam Mosseri's (head of Instagram) public posts from 2025-10-29 to 2026-10-26. The doctrine was compiled in the MIT-licensed `Brain-ai-biz/mosseri-skill`. Here the rules are paraphrased for music. Every rule keeps its date and post code, so you can check it at `instagram.com/p/<code>/`. Anything Mosseri said after 2026-10-26 is not in this file.

## Before-post checklist (read this one every time)

1. **Hook:** does a stranger get a reason to stay in the first 3 seconds? Open on the strongest moment, not on context.
2. **Send:** who would someone DM this to, and why?
3. **Stranger value:** does someone who doesn't know Inch of Shadow get something from it?
4. **Holds to the end:** does every part add something? Is there a dead intro, or padding after the payoff?
5. **Only you:** could only Inch of Shadow have made this? Process, studio, his voice, his gear.
6. **Muted:** does the key text read with the sound off? Check our own safe zones (`render-and-shrink-size-check`).
7. **One topic:** is it one idea, easy to place?

**Stop flags (fix before posting):**
- An app watermark in any frame (CapCut, TikTok, an AI tool).
- A re-upload of something already posted.
- The hook or caption promises something the clip doesn't deliver.
- An AI-smooth look with no human moment.
- Mostly someone else's clip without our own take.

## How ranking works

- Separate ranking runs for Stories, Reels and Explore, each split into followers and non-followers ("recommendations"). Most of the main feed is now accounts people don't follow, so the stranger test matters most. (2026-09-03 Dc1CcoYgZD_; 2026-06-10 DZaTX7EmzPD)
- Ranking predicts actions: like, comment, send, watch, watch to the end. (2026-09-03 Dc1CcoYgZD_; 2026-09-24 DdrY25_gNvi)
- Likes count most with followers. Sends count most with non-followers. (2026-05-26 DYzhl3iAXIo)
- Beyond engagement, ranking also values originality, recency and breaking content. (2026-09-09 DdENi2fP59I)
- **Auditions:** a post is shown to some non-followers first. If it beats expectations for that account, it gets shown again in bigger rounds. (2026-09-09 DdENi2fP59I)
- Recency: the system reacts within hours, not days. Release news goes out on the day. (2026-09-09 DdENi2fP59I)
- The same post can get very different reach depending on randomness, competition and timing. Riding a trend can help. (2026-05-28 DY40JFDGyod)
- Each post is ranked on its own, so there's no need to wait for the last reel to peak. Just don't flood followers within a few hours. (2026-06-24 DZ98T3Oj2Yx)
- **Collabs:** the account that starts the collab matters most for reach, so the bigger account should start it. You can add a collab after posting. Mosseri said this may change. (2025-11-18 DRNMNP1D-Mq)

## Reels: hook, watch time, building on what worked

- Watch time and watching to the end are their own signals. Mosseri named **no** right length. (2026-09-03 Dc1CcoYgZD_)
- When something worked, find its core and build on it. Don't post the same reel again. Ideas get tired over time. (2026-09-30 Dd6ZKifBC1x; 2025-10-30 DQbr-g6EsRE)
- Remake a winning idea in another format: a reel becomes a carousel, a carousel becomes a talk-to-camera reel. (2026-09-30 Dd6ZKifBC1x)
- Comments tell you what followers want more of. (2026-09-30 Dd6ZKifBC1x)

## Sends and DMs

- Reels and DMs drive Instagram's growth. DMs are now the main way people share. (2025-12-04 DR2JkdtEYrV; 2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)
- Long, loose content gets fewer sends. Any part that adds no reason to send is "long". (2026-10-26 De9ZzxeAJ76)
- DMing people does **not** raise reach. (2026-05-15 DYX3h7rxTKd)
- In stories, the reply button opens a DM. The question sticker collects questions you can answer later. (2026-06-02 DZFS2qrBlEj)

## Text and music

- About half of feed viewing is muted. Turn on captions and put the hook on screen. (2025-11-05 DQrPklCAXb1)
- Use music on photos and carousels, and on reels when it fits. (2025-11-05 DQrPklCAXb1)
- Scheduling a reel ahead is fine. (2026-01-23 DT2qWBWgYuN)

## Originality

- An account that mostly posts other people's content in a month stops being recommended to non-followers. This has covered photos and carousels as well since 2026-04. (2026-04-30 DXwUplrhe4j)
- To use someone else's content safely: remix it with your own take, or use the repost button or a collab, both of which credit them. (2026-04-30 DXwUplrhe4j)
- Editing in another app is fine. A **watermark** from another app gets the post ranked lower. (2026-01-09 DTSuEP7kpX7; 2026-08-18 DcLq4DbmxrJ)
- Never delete a reel that failed and re-upload it: it does the same, and followers react less to the second copy. Use trial reels instead. (2026-06-30 DaNZGV3Bckh)
- Reposting your own post helps only at the margins. Replying to and liking comments helps more. (2025-11-26 DRhURTtEZyc)
- The bar has moved to "something only you could make". (2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)

## AI and rawness

- AI content has a look: too slick, skin too smooth. The polished look becomes the tell. (2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)
- Rawness and imperfection are proof now. Trust shifts to who is speaking. (2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)
- Instagram plans to label AI content and show credibility signals. Mosseri said nothing on whether an AI label lowers reach. (2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)
- Viewers can ask for less of something, for example "AI slop". (2026-06-26 DaDWjlEG7vl)

## Trial reels

- Trial reels are shown only to non-followers. If one works, you share it to followers and the profile. Trials can be scheduled. Use them only for new content. (2025-11-05 DQrPklCAXb1; 2026-09-24 DdrY25_gNvi; 2026-04-02 DWooFXuADXR)
- Set the bar for "it worked" before posting, for example 2x the other version (the skill author's rule, not Mosseri's).

## Rhythm and timing

- Posting more gives more chances over time, but only at a pace you can keep. (2026-09-24 DdrY25_gNvi)
- Scheduling does not lower reach. (2026-03-19 DWELQpFFJo-)
- There's no universal best time. At most, post about an hour before your followers' peak, and that is marginal. (2026-06-16 DZpkZ4lAsS3)
- Repeatable concepts and named series give people a reason to follow. (2025-10-30 DQbr-g6EsRE)
- Reach is a means to an end, so decide the goal of each post first. (2025-10-30 DQbr-g6EsRE)
- Creators need steady reach more than followers. (2026-05-12 DYP3Il5kUOc)

## What to measure

- Look at rates, not totals: sends, saves, likes and comments per view. (2026-05-26 DYzhl3iAXIo; 2026-09-24 DdrY25_gNvi)
- Split follower reach from non-follower reach in Insights. A reel seen almost only by followers failed the stranger test. (2026-05-26 DYzhl3iAXIo)

## Carousels, stories, long video

- Personal moments belong in stories and DMs. A feed carousel needs value for a stranger. (2025-12-31 DS7pz7-DuZG)
- Carousels can now have a caption per slide, be reordered after posting, and have their audio replaced after posting. (2026-06-18 DZvEb5tkQZs; 2026-03-23 DWO5XmpAMUx; 2026-07-21 DbD69XHkYO_)
- Stories are for everyday raw sharing. Reels are for telling a story with video. (2026-05-12 DYP3Il5kUOc; 2026-04-08 DW3x2NpigcR)
- Instagram now leans into long video. Clips drive discovery of the full piece (DJ sets, guest mixes), and each clip has to stand on its own. (2026-10-26 De9ZzxeAJ76)

## Account health

- "Account Status" shows whether the account is still eligible to be recommended. Check it now and then, since we run automation. (2026-04-30 DXwUplrhe4j)
- Switching to private and back does not boost reach. (2026-07-20 DbA4_Iaha7s)
- Changing direction (songs lane): post to explain it, and make the profile look like what it is now about at a glance. Stay on the same account. (2026-06-17 DZr6xxzhtvz)
- Keep two-factor authentication on. (2026-07-15 Da0BA22BKqH)

## Myths Mosseri busted

Editing in another app hurts reach (no, only watermarks do). Scheduling hurts (no). There's a best time to post (no). DMing people boosts reach (no). Private then public boosts reach (no). Reposting yourself brings a post back (only at the margins). Delete and re-upload a failed reel (no, use trials). Wait for the last reel to peak (no). The chronological following feed is gone (no, tap the logo).

## What Mosseri never said (don't quote him on these)

The best reel length, hashtags, keywords or caption SEO, comment bait ("comment X and I'll send it"), whether an AI label lowers reach, covers, grid images and cover titles. For these, use our own data (`reel-craft.md`) or say it's unknown.
