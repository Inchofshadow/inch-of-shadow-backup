## Looked at, not just searched (2026-09-20)

The list above started as a NAME LOOKUP: the name was typed into Canva's font search and
the result list was read. That proves a name exists, not that the letters match the
reference. So each one was then set on a real line of type, in a Canva poster, and exported
and looked at: `references/fonts-look12/canva-fonts-specimen.png`, made from the design
`https://www.canva.com/design/DAHVxhRgtak/q8ZBmMGrs89zLKOQnPTrmQ/edit`
(twenty lines reading "Inch of Shadow, <font name>", each line in its own font).

Two answers changed when the letters were actually looked at:

- **Cooper BT**: the family's DEFAULT weight in Canva is Light, a thin soft serif that is
  nothing like The Doors' L.A. Woman. The fat face is in there, as the style **Black**.
  Always pick Black; the default is the wrong answer.
- **Bookmania**: Canva has four upright weights and **no italic and no swash**. The Real
  Estate "Days" logo is a heavy swash ITALIC, and the swash letters are the whole point of
  it. That logo cannot be made in Canva. Bookmania Black is a warm heavy Bookman and worth
  having, but it is not that reference.

The other seven hold up: Albertus Nova really is Wolpe's carved Albertus (Coldplay),
Clarendon is the slab of the Picasso catalogue, Futura and Univers are the plain partners,
and Marcellus, Cormorant Garamond and Forum are the faces already known from Look-01.
