# Inch of Shadow - Brand Foundation

The single source of truth for who Inch of Shadow is and how it looks and sounds.
Skills and tools should read these before writing captions, bios, descriptions,
pitches, or generating visuals. Decisions are made on the control panel board
(`plans/control-panel/tickets.json`, Release Manager `/board`) and land here.

The files:

- `positioning.md` (v2, 2026-09-19) - who, the sound, the words, influences, reach,
  audience, ambition. Read its PUBLIC STATUS block first: the songs are not public yet.
- `proof.md` - facts only. The honest source for any factual claim in copy.
- `voice.md` (v2, 2026-09-20) - how Inch of Shadow writes: warm, first person, length free; the
  two registers, emojis, hashtags per platform, the never-list, the Letter for Listeners idea.
- `frame.md` (v1, 2026-09-20) - the visual spec every tool reads: type, colour moods, safe
  zones, mark, image, face, motion, never-list. Written in the HyperFrames design-spec
  format; copy it into every video project. Motion detail: `motion-library.md`. Font file:
  `fonts/Marcellus-Regular.ttf`.
- `formats.md` (2026-09-20) - the fixed set of objects made per single, two lanes (light =
  electronic drops, full = songs), with the why from our own numbers. Mirrored in the
  Release Manager checklist.
- `font-library.md` (2026-09-20) - Amir's font research, categorised: every face he found,
  where he saw it, where to get it, its status. All potentials, per release. Licence first.
- `style-library.md` (2026-09-20) - seven named looks a release can borrow (S1 Xerox ... S7
  Pictorialism), their history and rules; made with `../../print_looks.py`.
- `machine.md` (2026-09-20) - who makes what: one tool per object, what stays by hand, which
  old tools are out of date. `canva-setup.md` - the values to enter in the Canva Brand Kit.
- `reel-craft.md` (2026-09-20) - the one reel rulebook: what his own numbers say, what
  research verified, what is only an idea to test, what was refuted.
- `motion-library.md` - how videos move: title arrivals T1-T4, the sting, the never-list.
- `../../inch_of_shadow_style.txt` + `../../options/` - the image prompt core and the
  per-image options (used by image_generator.py).
- `CONTROL-PANEL.md` - the image studio and how the visual layers work.
- `mark/` - logo mark drafts (working set, final art to be hand drawn by Amir).
- `research/` - reference research done for the sittings. Add to it, do not start over.
- `CONTROL-PANEL-SETUP-ROADMAP.md`, `VIDEO-BRAND-SESSION-KIT.md` - frozen history from
  the electronic era. Do not take options or wording from them.

`proof.md` was confirmed with Amir on 2026-09-18; `positioning.md` was written from his
own answers on 2026-09-19.
