## 2. What research verified [VERIFIED, June 2026]

- **The opening FRAME has to arrest, not the opening words.** A slow cinematic build is
  fine IF frame one already holds the eye. Three triggers are backed by attention science:
  strong contrast with one colour accent, sudden movement (a zoom, a pan), direct eye
  contact.
- **Instagram's named ranking signals** (Mosseri, January 2025): watch time, completion,
  re-watches and loops, sends and shares. Build clips that loop cleanly and are worth
  sending. Re-check the list each planning cycle, it shifts.
- **Every Reel carries an audio track.** (added 2026-10-06, from the Social Media Skills
  research) A Reel with no audio is treated like a photo for reach and is not pushed to
  non-followers. A studio clip with only room noise still gets a track underneath; never
  a silent file.
- **Do not fake darkness with lifted, milky blacks.** Real darkness is deep blacks placed
  correctly, balanced contrast, selective highlights.
- **Grade first, grain last and lightly** (around 30% opacity, felt not seen). Grain cannot
  rescue a flat image.
- **Match the grade to the mood** of the piece. (The old file added "bleach bypass for this
  palette" and "the EBM lane wants high contrast": that was written for the electronic era.
  It stays one tool for the electronic lane. The grade for the songs is board ticket Look-09,
  and the brand is never only dark.)

## 3. Ideas worth testing, proven by nobody [TEST]

From the August file. Useful as things to TRY with trial reels and then measure:

- Open at the strongest bar of the track, never the intro.
- Large text in frame one carrying the hook for people watching muted. (The "85% watch
  muted" figure is unverified.)
- Hook formats: text-over-music tension line, before and after (raw demo, then the master),
  process reveal (the finished moment first, then how it was built).
- 7 to 15 seconds for release clips, 15 to 30 for process. His own two reels suggest shorter
  costs nothing (section 1), but that is two data points.
- Engineered seamless loops (HyperFrames can make the last frame equal the first).
- Duck or cut the track before the key moment and come back on the beat.
- Words describing the content on screen, for Instagram search.
- **Trial reels:** one build, the first 2 seconds swapped, 2 or 3 versions shown to
  non-followers, the winner goes out. This is in the fixed set (`formats.md`, object 4)
  because of section 1, not because of any blog. The "up to 25 a day" figure is unverified.
- Posting late at night for electronic audiences. Unverified, and it says nothing about
  the songs' audience.

## 4. Refuted: do not state these as facts [REFUTED]

- "The hook must land in 1.7 / 2 / 3 seconds." No evidence for a universal number. (His
  own drop by 0:02 is real, see section 1; it is his data, not a law.)
- "A 3-second hold rate gives 5 to 10 times the reach."
- "Cut every 1.5 to 3 seconds; static shots over 4 seconds are a danger zone."
- "The scratch-record or rewind audio hook works." The August file listed it as a format;
  it is an idea at best.
- "Mid-performance drop-in is the top formula; front-load everything."
- **"Lyric reveals have the highest save rate of any music format."** The August file built
  on this. It failed verification. The lyric reel stays in the fixed set for a different
  reason: the lyrics are his edge (`positioning.md`). Its numbers get measured on the
  first one.
- "Showing yourself on camera beats curated content." No general evidence. His own numbers
  are in section 1.
- "Static performer-with-gear shots are dead." Marked [CONSENSUS] in August; his best reel
  was exactly gear, with motion.
- "Specific export settings keep 60% more quality."

## 6. Brand rules that override any craft tip

The hook opens, the name closes (the sting, B on the flash). No handle by default. One text
effect per video. No text on his face. Nothing that needs an explanation to work on a
stranger. `motion-library.md`, `frame.md`.
