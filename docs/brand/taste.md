# Inch of Shadow - Taste file (started 2026-10-12)

What Amir picked, what he rejected, and why, saved so every reference board and every
design proposal gets closer to his eye. Claude reads this file before building a board
(`toolkit.md`, step 3) and before proposing a visual direction.

## How things get in (Amir, 2026-10-12)

Amir sometimes reads and writes fast. A fast answer must never become a permanent rule
about his taste. So:

- **Only answers to a question marked 📌 are recorded.** Claude marks a question with 📌
  when the answer will be saved here, so Amir knows to answer seriously.
- **Unmarked questions are conversation.** Nothing from them is written here.
- **Read back before saving.** After a 📌 answer Claude shows the exact line it will save
  ("Saving: ..."). Amir corrects it or lets it stand; only then it is written.
- Each line keeps his words where they matter, the date, and what it was about (a board,
  a release, a reference). A line is never generalised beyond what he said: a "no" to one
  image is a "no" to that image, not to its whole category, unless he says so.

## Already known before this file

These were decided earlier and live in their own files; listed so nothing is asked twice:
- Not only dark: sunny, warm and daylight are on brand too (`frame.md`).
- Sunglasses on is the natural state; off is the rare, deliberate move (`frame.md`).
- No AI on his face, ever (`frame.md`, The Face).
- Wide-spaced serif capitals read as fantasy to him ("Lord of the Rings"); not for the name (`frame.md`).
- Two voices: a loud face next to a quiet one "is really my style", chosen per release (`font-library.md`).
- Lucas Garcete (@sepulcrodelaluz) is a direct influence; not the satanic figures (Shadow Archive R-004).
- Liked from @alexandergrades: Harry Potter (Prisoner of Azkaban), The Odyssey, Berserk (Eclipse) (R-002).
- Square from a portrait = the whole photo on warm black, never a crop (memory: square-from-portrait-on-black).
