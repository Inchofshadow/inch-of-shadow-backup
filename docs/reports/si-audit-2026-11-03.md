## 5. Sheet verdict

The Sheet no longer earns its place as a working tool. Reasons: last push 2026-09-01; it holds none of the outreach, booking or tracker data; it is invite-only; sync-back has already caused damage once (2026-09-01 "sync-back damage restored"); the RM /outreach and /stats pages show the live data and the RM does not use the Sheet. Keep it only as a read-only browse/filter view (sorting 1,200 contacts by genre or city is the one thing the web pages do not do). Do not delete it. Stop two-way sync.
