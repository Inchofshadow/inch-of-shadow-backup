# Instagram settings 2026 (research, 2026-10-11)

Three sources: official Meta/Mosseri, creators and strategists, Reddit. Light pass, not exhaustive.
Reddit threads could not be opened in full (blocked), so Reddit rows are from search snippets only.

## Do this

| Setting | Value | Why | Backed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Settings > Media quality > Upload at highest quality | ON, data saver OFF | Upload is otherwise compressed harder | IG Help, creators, Reddit (all agree) |
| HDR | Off (film "Most Compatible" or disable HDR in Media quality) | HDR uploads look washed out / overexposed | Reddit consensus; Edits lead says HDR off gives viewers SDR |
| Reel export | 9:16, 1080x1920 or 1440x2560 (2K), H.264, 30 fps, AAC 48 kHz | IG re-encodes everything; 4K gains nothing; Edits lead recommends 2K | IG Help (minimums), Edits lead Tom Bender (2025-05), Reddit |
| Safe zones | Keep text out of top ~10%, bottom ~20%, right icon column | App UI covers these (no official spec) | Creators, opinion |
| Bitrate | ~10-16 Mbps | No official number; sources range 8-40 | Contested, opinion only |
| Account type | Creator | Full music library; Business gets the smaller commercial library | Creators + Reddit consensus (not officially confirmed) |
| Reel length | 3:00 or less | Longer reels are not recommended to non-followers | IG Help |
| Watermarks | None (no CapCut/TikTok end cards) | IG shows watermarked reels less; editing in CapCut itself is fine | IG Help, Mosseri, Reddit |
| Original content | Most posts in any 30 days your own | Accounts that mostly repost lose recommendations (since 2026-04, also photos/carousels) | Tubefilter/PetaPixel 2026-04-30 |
| Hashtags | Max 5, used as topic labels | Hard cap since 2025-12; Mosseri: they don't drive reach | Social Media Today 2025-12-18 |
| Account Status | Check "content can be recommended" | Shows if non-followers can be shown your posts | IG Help |
| Trial Reels | Use for new formats, one variable per test | Shown to non-followers first; auto-share on 72h results | Meta 2024-12; results on Reddit mixed |
| Google indexing | Pro account, public (check the toggle) | Posts indexed in Google/Bing since 2025-07-10 | WordStream, others |
| Auto captions | Off if captions are burned in | Otherwise two caption layers overlap | RNID / accessibility guides |

## Contested (no clear answer)

- Share Reels to Facebook: extra views vs reports of IG reach dropping; IG-licensed music can be blocked on FB.
- Schedulers: Mosseri says no penalty; some Reddit users report drops; schedulers may crop 3:4 to 4:5.
- Desktop vs phone upload quality: Reddit split.
- Edits app reach boost: one newsletter quotes Mosseri ("a little"), no primary clip found.

## Myths

- 20-30 hashtags is best (2021 Later data, feed only, now capped at 5).
- CapCut editing is penalised (only the watermark is).
- Crediting a repost makes it original.
- The quality toggle guarantees HD playback: Mosseri (2024-10) says low-view videos are served in lower quality.

## Context signals (Mosseri, 2025-01)

Main ranking signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Sends matter most with non-followers.
Originality: 75% of US recommendations now come from original posts (Meta, 2026-01).
Grid is 3:4 since 2025-01; native 3:4 photos (1080x1440) since 2025-05.
